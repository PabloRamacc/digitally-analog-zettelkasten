There is __no exact correspondence__ between the educational system and society (any more than there is between social structures and ideas).
Historians emphasize this time and again (e.g., Talbott 1971, p. 138 → B2733).
But who claims such a thing?
__Certainly not__ the functionalists, to whom Talbott (1971), p. 142ff. → B2733 attributes this to them.
