From a sociological perspective, the function of education stems from the fact that socialization is an __ecological process__ __that does not require an overarching system__—that is, it does not regulate itself; see → 5314/1,6t .
Education, therefore, serves to__ control socialization__ and achieve desired socialization effects.
From a pedagogical perspective, on the other hand, its function is to create better people.
