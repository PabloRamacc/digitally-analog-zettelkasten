For the causal relations of a system it is crucial that, thanks to its invariance, it can be regarded as a unified causal factor (effect or cause), even though it itself is a complex system. From this results the decisive idea of modern systems analysis: classifying complex mechanisms into simple and repeatable – precisely because of inner complexity and thereby guaranteed invariance! – causal connections.
This unity can transform small causes into large effects! And therefore act as potentiality vis-à-vis experiencing, anticipating partners!
See also the corresponding concept of selective amplification → 35/5k3g. A good example is Popitz 1968b → B1405.
