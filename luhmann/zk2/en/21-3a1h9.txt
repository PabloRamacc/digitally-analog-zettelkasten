An initial stock requirement is nothing else than the irreplaceability of a cause or class of causes within a system. See also {1} → 21/3a1a8b .
As a negative concept, the claim of an existential presupposition cannot naturally be empirically verified. This does not need to be discussed or even attempted. However, one can try to reduce the irreplaceability of a system through exchange experiments empirically. And exchange experiments are empirically feasible and controllable.X Once it succeeds in exchanging causes while maintaining their effect, only the class of these causes, defined by the unity of an effect (as a functional reference point), can be treated as existential presuppositions.
