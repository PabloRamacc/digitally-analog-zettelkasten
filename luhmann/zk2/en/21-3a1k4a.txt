In yet another respect, the functional hierarchy of problem levels leads to a departure from causal-scientific analysis.
Causally scientific analysis investigates the __causes__ of problematic states and therefore attempts to solve problems by eliminating their causes. Unfavorable effects are avoided if their causes can be eliminated.
However, the problem of the heterogeneity of purposes or mixed-functional consequences is overlooked here. The causes can also have positive effects that would then be eliminated as well.
Functional problem-solving does not look back at the causes (or only in a kind of pre-analysis of the problem). It rather makes solving a problem into its purpose and seeks means to achieve this end. __The elimination of the cause of the problem__ → 21/3a1k4b
