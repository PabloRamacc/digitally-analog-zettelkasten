The proposals by Lepeletier (Alt 1949 → B2509) The proposals to introduce compulsory schooling and to establish a common school education for all children aged 5 to 12 were among the most radical measures produced by the French Revolution. They remained a utopia. It was not until 1830 that universal elementary education began to be implemented.
