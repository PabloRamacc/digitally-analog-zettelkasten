__Introductory thought__: Neither classical organizational theory nor the first major wave of criticism directed against it—the Human Relations Approach—has been able to influence public administration, not even in the United States, where, admittedly, the academic distinction between business administration and public administration also plays a role.
This is likely because organizational theory has, to date, been developed primarily outside of public administration—which, of course, does not preclude some contact.
Functional theory offers well-founded hope that this difficulty can be overcome through the introduction of a comparative methodology.
