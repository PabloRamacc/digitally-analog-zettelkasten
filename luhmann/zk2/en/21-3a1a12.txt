Another important aspect arises from the limitations of the biol basic model: namely, the absence of death as a criterion and the lack of type-stability in social systems; contributing systems can themselves influence conditions affecting their performance decline—they modify or reshape them. The heart or stomach are bound to a pre-determined demand pattern of the organism, whereas a hospital, a party, or an industrial enterprise can instead contribute to defining public values that regulate assessments of their performances. Brotz 1961 → B359 points particularly to this, albeit without connecting it to the limitations regarding the transferability of the biological system model → 21/3a1a13.
