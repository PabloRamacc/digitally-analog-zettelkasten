The disputes and controversies do not revolve around the question of whether the classics are important and why, but solely around the interpretation of their works.
See, for example, Hans Joas, “The Antinomies of Neofunctionalism—A Discussion with Jeffrey Alexander,” ZfS 17 (1988), pp. 272–285, esp. p. 278 → B3348: “The purely theoretical argument would founder on its own excessive complexity without the focus provided by the reference to the classics.”
