s/f
The thesis that teaching has an effect that is partly motivating, partly demotivating, and thereby reinforces deviations.
Students respond to experiences of success or failure by developing a corresponding self-concept and corresponding levels of self-expectation. In lower socioeconomic classes, parents’ homes are more likely to reinforce a negative, low-expectation outlook, while the opposite is true in upper socioeconomic classes.
See generally Ashton 1973 → B2826.
