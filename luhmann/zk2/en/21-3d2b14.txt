Regarding legal consequences of growing interdependence see Esser 1956, p. 331ff → B531. In particular one should consider: 1.) juridification of auxiliary duties and in connection with that: a rehumanization, ethicalizing of law (Esser, p. 332 → B531)
2.) change of contract principles, informalization of contract law, factual contractual obligations, framework obligation, separation duties, p. 332f judicial modification of contracts, etc.
3.) separation of rules for damage allocation and risk bearing.
Further one would mention: progression of the idea of balancing interests, hardship compensation provisions, juridification of "may" conditions of public donation law.
