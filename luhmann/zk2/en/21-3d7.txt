Systems can only exist relatively invariantly if they enter into causal relationships with their environment – cf. → 21/3a1j –, but not in arbitrary ones, rather those that enable the continuation of the system. [Indeed __because__ this is an essential condition for existence, the distinction between statics and dynamics makes no sense.]
Modern systems analysis therefore conceives systems immediately within a causal context relative to their environment. Evidence: Blalock / A.B.
