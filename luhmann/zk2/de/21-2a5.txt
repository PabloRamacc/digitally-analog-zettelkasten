"The perfectly general definition of a function which has been given above is the culmination of a process of evolution which has proceeded largely in connection with the study of the representation of functions by means of trigonometrical series. By the older mathematicians, a function was understood to mean a single formula, at first usually only a power of the variable; but afterwards it was regarded as defined by any one analytical expression, and was extenden by Euler to include the case in which the function is given implicitly by a formal relation between the two variables.
