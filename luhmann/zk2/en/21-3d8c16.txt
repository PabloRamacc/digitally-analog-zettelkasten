System/Environment Theory
Main achievement of system formation: shifting conflicts from outside to inside as a special case of problem displacement from outside to inside. → 21/3d8c16,2
Internally, conflicts can in some way be heard better – especially when motivation is generalized so that it covers victories and defeats. Internal formal procedures and informal feuds are thus a substitute for joint external fighting – and this shift naturally presupposes a changeable motivational situation.
See also → 533/15a10; → 3411/5h; → 21/4i; → 21/3d8c16,1; → 532/8p.
