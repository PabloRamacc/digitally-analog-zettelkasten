Communication regarding structural issues can take the form __of indirect communication__—communication through allusion, by implying premises, etc.; see → 536/11dA .
Indirect communication is important as a form of communication regarding __structural issues__ insofar as it is __unanswerable__.
It can be used to establish consensus (or presumed consensus) on structural issues without making the structure itself the subject of interactive communication—that is, without putting it up for discussion.
This means of structure formation is particularly important, indeed indispensable, for simple systems, → 536/11cNd4
