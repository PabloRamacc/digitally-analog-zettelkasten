A __scathing__ critique of the inadequate justification of the Kantian transfer in Ritter’s 1798 work → B2454.
A purpose or a practical maxim cannot be a theoretical principle. The __unity__ of the concept of education has not yet been defined. Consequently, the dichotomies of “education” versus “upbringing” (development) and “human being” versus “citizen” coexist unresolved.
The problem is: How can one educate despite freedom? How can one foresee that a person will behave correctly despite freedom (and despite being educated for freedom)?
