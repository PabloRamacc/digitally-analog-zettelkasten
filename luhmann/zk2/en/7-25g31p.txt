In the classroom interaction system, the teacher must pay attention to two different types of structures at the same time. __Both__ are potential sources of resistance and may be intercorrelated:
1.) the students as individuals, constituting the system’s environment;
2.) the social structures of peer groups, sociometric preferences, alignment with opinions, etc. The question is: when is social differentiation likely to occur, and in what context? Social structures __cannot__ be predicted based solely on knowledge of the individuals.
