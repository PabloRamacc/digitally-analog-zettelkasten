The term respecification stands in an important connection with the substantive-temporal character of system/environment relations. Specifications seem to be highly bound to the __frequency__ of occurrences of triggers or supporters in the environment; they are at the same time more directly dependent on the environment. Respecifications must, if they are to compensate for __generalization__ and thereby remain available __permanently__, be made independent from the temporal structure of the environment to a high degree. They can only refer to their __internal__ function in this independence.
