→ 7/25g28 __Education / Economy__; see → 7/25g37B ; → 7/25g28Ca ; → 7/25g28Cb
The economy has an interest in overproduction in the field of education because this opens up __opportunities for selection__ in the area of human resources.
Initial selection becomes all the more important because it is the most crucial form of selection and establishes welfare entitlements that complicate subsequent selection processes, particularly demotion or dismissal.
