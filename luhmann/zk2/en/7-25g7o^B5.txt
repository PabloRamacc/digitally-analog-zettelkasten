Awareness of the political risks of state school supervision had emerged during the French Revolution.
The reports by both Mirabeau and Condorcet (Alt 1949 → B2509) are aware of the problem in this regard and seek to avoid transferring school supervision to the administrative bureaucracy. Instead, they propose direct subordination to the people’s representatives and, for the rest, within the school system itself (higher schools overseeing lower schools, according to Condorcet).
