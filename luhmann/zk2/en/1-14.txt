[*] __Organizational theory – system considerations__
Starting point: the concept of a system. Systems are structured through the generalization of expectations. This is the actual process of system formation. Therefore, a threefold problem framework based on the three dimensions of the generalization of expectations must be established at the outset as the basic schema for the discussion, namely (according to → 5311/7 ): 1.) __social stabilization__: system/environment issues;
2.) __substantive stabilization__: role structure;
3.) __temporal stabilization__: changes.
This order is recommended for reasons of presentation and comprehension. The order of the problems is → 1/14a
