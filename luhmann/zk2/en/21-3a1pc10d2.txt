The notion that reinforcement arises through generalization comes about because generalized stimuli or responses recur and thereby become imprinted.
An overview is provided by: Klaus Eyferth, Learning as Adaptation of the Organism Through Conditioned Response → B646, and: The Learning of Attitudes, Needs, and Social Behaviors → B647, in: Handbook of Psychology I, Göttingen 1964, pp. 76-117 (pp. 103-110) → B646 or pp. 347-370 (pp. 357-60) → B647.
