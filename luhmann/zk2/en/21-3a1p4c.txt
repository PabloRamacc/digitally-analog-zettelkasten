The distinction between identity on the one hand and symbol and form on the other is necessary as a functional differentiation. A consideration of symbols and forms alone could neither make their functional performances nor the function of identification understandable.
Distinction is an analytical tool: it does not need to be mystified into a distinction between the experiential world and the background of actual being. Such distinctions were indeed based on ontological difficulties, which always means: approaches to functional analysis lie underneath.
