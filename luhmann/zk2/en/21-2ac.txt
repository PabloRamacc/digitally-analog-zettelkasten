To coordinate both functional concepts, Bernard (1963) → B291 makes a particular effort but does not go much beyond the observation that it concerns two – each necessary in itself – aspects which stand side by side due to an "ambivalence de la structure sociale" (p. 609 → B291) and allow for two different "modes explicatifs" (p. 610 → B291).
The two further important thoughts are missing that could really promote integration: 1.) definition of function through ambiguity;
2.) definition of the variable through functional equivalence.
