Trapp 1913, p. 152 → B2516 outlines a whole series of consequential problems that arise from the fact that education in reform schools does not take place in a natural way—that is, not in a way that requires the student to earn his own living in the process. For example: One must provide activities to keep them occupied; the purpose of instruction is not recognized and does not become a motivating factor for the youth, etc.
