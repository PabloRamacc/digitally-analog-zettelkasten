Since the world does not exclude other possibilities but rather seeks to identify them as excluded, mere constitution of social identities leads to a problematicity __formulated ontologically as difference between being and appearance__ → 21/3a1p5c2,1, better grasped however as difference between identification and other possibilities, with the crucial thought that only through identification are other possibilities constituted as __other__. Thus the problem becomes independent from a theory of truth which considers only being, not also appearance as capable of truth.
This is essentially an application case of phenomenological reduction in Husserl's sense. See also → 21/12g4.
