There must be specializations of interests, preferences, roles and organizational units with regard to these three areas within the system. On the other hand it is questionable __if their difference coincides with other structural lines of the system__, because this could lead to too strong an interruption of interdependence.
Example: for reflection, it is __developed__; the __profession__ stands under performance or functional requirements (typically theologians in ecclesiastical professions). Or: the dividing line coincides with differences between professionals and clients.
