The system’s inability to differentiate means that it cannot differentiate its environment either.
For simple systems, there are no significant environmental distinctions other than the __basic distinction between the present environment and the absent environment__. This distinction is, of course, of central importance because the present environment includes the acting individuals (contingent, highly perturtable systems).
“Social control” is the regulation of relationships with the present environment; see → 536/11e .
