As a characteristic counterexample of behavior that is immoral and disrespectful in this sense, the __interview__ can be cited. → 21/7l
The meaning of an interview is typical: to draw conclusions from another person's self-presentation about the functional connections underlying their identification, thus taking a look behind the scenes. It is not simply about recording the presentation but evaluating it for insight into what remains unrepresented [except in statistically selected opinion research, where personality interests no one at all and an generalized question lies underneath]. The technique and prospects of an interview rely primarily on the difficulties and deficiencies of a consistent, rounded self-presentation. From lessons in interviewing techniques one can therefore draw valuable conclusions about the therein presupposed techniques of self-presentation by those interviewed. → 21/3a1p5c1B8
