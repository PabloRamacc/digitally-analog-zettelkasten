If the production of compatibility runs through avoiding incompatibility via (more or less decentralized) match/mismatch procedures, then the process requires __time__. The substantive problem of consistency shifts into the time dimension. One must see how far one gets.
Time—and for systems: temporal stability—belongs to the complex of functional equivalents for a positive criterion of compatibility.
If one knew what is good, no time would be needed!
