In addition, Parsons [Lecture 1960, p. 8R → B702[*]] has not yet used the term __integrative standards__ in print outside of a brief reference in PV Revisited → B956] to denote this system-internal generalization of exchange processes.
For money-using input/output systems (__especially__ Economy, but all that use money), the __solvency criterion__ → 21/3a3lb2aa serves as such an integrative standard. For systems operating with power mechanisms (especially Polity) Parsons calls this criterion __Effectiveness__. "Effectiveness is the criterion of fulfillment of organizational purposes." (Lecture 1960, p. 9 → B702)[*]
Additionally, there are regulatory standards that regulate the exchange itself: "in the economic field: property – control; in the political field: authority – leadership" (Lecture 1960, p. 9R → B702) [*]
