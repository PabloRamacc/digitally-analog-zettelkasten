→ 536/11c __General and overarching aspect__:
In __each__ dimension, the specific problem arises only—or at least, the acute nature of a problem becomes apparent only—through the general structural feature of thematic concentration.
It is through the themes—or with the help of the themes—that the problems take shape.
Thus, the problems can be constructed as consequential problems of the “simple system” type.
