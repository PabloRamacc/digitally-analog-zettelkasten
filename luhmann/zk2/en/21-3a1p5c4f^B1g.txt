Loyalty is to be defined as an emotionally grounded restriction of the readiness for modification within one's own attitudes. To the extent that one is loyal, one is not ready to change their attitude, __even if there would be grounds for doing so__. Loyalty in this general sense is the opposite of adaptability. "Opposite" does not mean that both are incompatible. Rather, all adaptability only makes sense with regard to the existence of a system. The problem lies in generalization: selecting the reference points of loyalty for the system such that they are compatible with high elasticity and modifiability of the system.
