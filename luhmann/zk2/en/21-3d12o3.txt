The deparadoxing of the world enables a __Repetition__ of the determination of system references in the sense of an operable operation. According to the initial reference, there are two possibilities: 1.) The system is treated as a unit of further system/environment differences (system differentiation).
2.) The environment is treated as a site of further system/environment differences, which leads to the system placing itself into the environments of other systems of its own environment.
