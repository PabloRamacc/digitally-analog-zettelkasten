Reference to self-reference: Selfishness does not in and of itself make a person moral; it must first be instrumentalized for this purpose. This is done expertly by the educator. See, for example, Resewitz 1776, esp. pp. 37ff. → B2135
The focus here is not yet on didactics and teaching technology, but only on the measures necessary for the perfection of the human being. The problem still lies in the tension between nature and morality.
