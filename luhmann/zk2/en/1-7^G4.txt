The concept of a system is used as an analytical tool—not simply in the sense of a true description of an existing state of affairs. Rather, the concept of a system captures the unity of an organizational problem-solving process. However, what is treated as a system and where the system’s boundaries lie depend on the problem at hand. Although these perspectives are rarely clearly articulated, they seem to be particularly evident in Optner’s 1960 book → B107 to underlie it.
