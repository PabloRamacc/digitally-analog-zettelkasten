The differentiation of education largely meant __differentiation away from religion__.
1.) School supervision in Prussia in the 18th century was still in the hands of the clergy → 7/25g7m5,1 ; → 7/25g7m5,2 ;
2.) Grammar schools in England remained firmly in the hands of the Established Church until the 19th century (Vincent, pp. 86ff., 108 → B2262), with about 80% of teachers being members of religious orders. Vincent 1969, pp. 113ff., 121 → B2262
