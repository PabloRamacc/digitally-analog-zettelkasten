The four system problems can, since they are allegedly derivable from a general action theory, be applied to any action system. However, the localization of functional performances with regard to these problems must always be understood relative to specific systems. The flip side of this relativity is that concrete performances in different action systems may be relevant with respect to different system problems. So explicitly Parsons et al., Working P., p. 176 → B954. This is a partial aspect of the general multifunctionality of concrete performances. → 21/3a1h1b
