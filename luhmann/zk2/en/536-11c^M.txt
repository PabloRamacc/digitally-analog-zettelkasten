→ 536/11c __Internal Differentiation in General__
Above all: What does differentiation mean in __meaningfully__ constituted systems (as opposed to organisms)?
Note on the high degree of flexibility in differentiation: The subsystems do not always need to be active; they can overlap and can become operational simultaneously despite conflicting objectives. One can experience and act within different systemic contexts at the same time—working for one’s office, for the state, and for one’s family. The same applies all the more to a sequence of activities.
Herein lies the problem and the condition of possibility → 536/11cM1
