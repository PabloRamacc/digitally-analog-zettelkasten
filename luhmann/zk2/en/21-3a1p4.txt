Identity → 21/3a1p4(R) / Form / Symbol
Every secure expectation presupposes the constitution of identical meaning in the world. Expectations are nothing other than future aspects of identities.
The stabilization of identical meaning is thus indirectly at the same time a stabilization of meaning-related expectations. From this perspective, two forms of possibilities are functionally equivalent: __identification through perceptive form → 21/3a1p4B and identification through symbols__ → 21/3a1p4A.
