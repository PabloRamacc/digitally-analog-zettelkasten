For this see especially WP, p. 228ff. → B954 For example a system learns through generalization the performance value (G) of a specific orientation (A), the reference of an affective satisfaction (G) to particular system-internal orientations (I), the necessity of affect-neutral patterns (L) for the continuation of a diffus-particular integration (I), and the necessity of a universalistic internal orientation (A) for the persistence of these patterns (L). Connections of this kind are learned through symbolic generalization. Conversely, there was therefore an analysis of the concept of symbol (WP, p. 31ff. → B776) which Parsons gave the key to clarifying the relationship between pattern variables and system problems (WP, p. 80ff. → B953).
