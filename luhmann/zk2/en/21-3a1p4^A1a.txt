→ 21/3a1p4A1 more precisely: each identical meaning has double references in this sense to its own "inner" and into the world, depending on which context one keeps in mind.
The specific function of the symbol is to make such referential integrations accessible to others.
This means above all: that one understands objects of one's own experience through their meaning in alien experiential systems with the aid of symbolic communication. One does not understand an immediate correspondence between word and worldly thing, but rather the meaning of the worldly thing for others, within alien experiential systems, by means of the word.
In this way, the internal connection between factual horizon and social horizon can be made clear with the help of symbol theory.
