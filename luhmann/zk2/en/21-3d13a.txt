The concept of the horizon must synthesize with the theory of expecting expectations; in other words, it must be seen not only as a horizon for individual expectation and the expectation of the single subject but also as a horizon for others and their expectations, and further still as a horizon within which others expect that I am expecting within a horizon.
The difference between the expectation of a horizon for expectations and the expectation of expectations enables an interpretation of discrepancies in expectations, surprises, etc.
