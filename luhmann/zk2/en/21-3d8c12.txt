__Functions of Open Conflict Behavior for the Involved Systems (Dispute)__
The starting point of a functional analysis of dispute lies in freeing oneself from the prejudice held by all normative sciences against dispute and impartially assessing its functions and dysfunctions for the involved systems.
__Disputes have essential positive functions for adapting social systems to their environment__ → 21/3d8c12,1 ; → 21/3d8c12,2 and are therefore difficult to replace, certainly only through other adaptive performances.
By adaptation is thus meant nothing gentle or yielding, not conformism without resistance, but simply the solution of the problem of preserving the system- → 21/3d8c12a
