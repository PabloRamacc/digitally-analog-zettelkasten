Definitionen:
Holzner 1964, S. 73 → B1596: "An anomic situation is any situation of existential relevance to the actor which is unmanageable in terms of the routine alternatives deriving from institutionally provided hierarchies of norms. A departure from 'normal' action orientations then can occur by changing the whole hierarchy of norms or by deviating, as it were, around one particular level of normative orientation."
