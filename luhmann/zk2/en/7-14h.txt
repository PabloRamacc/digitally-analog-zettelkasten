__History of the Concept__
Differentiation requires a generalization and universalization (not a narrowing) of the educational function; this comes easily to the 18th century,
1.) because it rejects innate ideas;
2.) it views society as highly individualized;
3.) considers cumulative acquisition and improvements to be possible, including the transfer of educational achievements to the next generation.
