The problems of symbolic order treated above (→ 21/3a1p5c4aA) have an essential communicative aspect and are socially relevant through it. In this sense they contain a wealth of strategies and techniques for controlling the experience of others, which serve to secure the representation of an identification. However, these are not the only possible means for this purpose.
Another complex of – functionally equivalent, so far as this goes – means consists in the __regulation of those information that a viewer receives about the representation and the identifications underlying it__. A sub-complex here are limitations on contact with and thus insight into the representation through mechanisms of __social distancing__. → 21/3a1p5c4aBa See also → 534/1i, where → 21/3a1p5c4aB1
