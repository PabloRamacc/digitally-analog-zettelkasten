→ 21/3d5b9 Vgl. auch 1961c, S. 223f. → B1227, wo klargestellt wird, dass die Unit/System-Relativität mit der prominenten Stellung des Normbegriffs in Parsons' Systemtheorie zusammenhängt.
"The proposition that the relational patterns of social systems are normative, […], can, in fact, be extended to the structure of units as well. One way of making this clear is to point out that what at one level of reference is a unit at another is a system. What we are calling the structural properties of the unit, therefore, are at the next level the relational patterns which order the relations between what in turn are the subunits making __it__ up. Therefore, it is justified to assert, in the wider perspective, that the structure of social systems in general __consists__ [S. 224] in institutionalized patterns of normative culture."
