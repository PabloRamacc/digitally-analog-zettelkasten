The opposition between open and closed systems remains unclear (if more is meant than an opposition between right and wrong system theory), because there cannot be any closed systems without any causal connection whatsoever to the environment. {1}
In contrast, it offers advantages to assume not a dichotomously formulated opposition but rather a variable, namely the __variable openness of the system or permeability of its boundaries__. A system is the more open, the more information from its environment it can absorb and process. See also → 21/3d19g ; → 21/3d7i.
