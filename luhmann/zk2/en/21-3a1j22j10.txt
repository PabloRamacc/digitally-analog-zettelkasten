Every observation is an operation and is as an operation __naive with reference to its referent__.
This is merely another formulation of the theory of the blind spot.
From this it follows that all observations, including the observation of observers, are equated under this aspect. There is therefore no reflexivity hierarchy in which increasing distance from reality leads to increased mediation of real-world relations.
See for this, however more radically critical, critique of such conceptions at Latour 1988, p. 168f → B479.
