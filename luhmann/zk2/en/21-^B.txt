__On the functional concept in management science__
Stahlmann, p. 13ff. → B3385, with an overview showing that function is defined as a task, subtask, or task-related throughout.
Stahlmann, p. 13 → B3385: "When dividing tasks among individual task holders, we speak of functions. Function can therefore only be seen in relation to the overarching task of an organizational structure." Very often, function is viewed instrumentally rather than as a task: as means or performance.
So (according to Stahlmann, p. 13f. → B3385) E. Potthoff, *Betriebsorganisation*, Handbuch der Wirtschaftswissenschaften 1958, p. 52 → B3386; Ulrich, *Betriebwirtschaftliche Organisationslehre*, p. 108 → B3387, and Nordsieck → B3388.
See also W. Hasenack, *Funktionenlehre*, betriebswirtschaftliches Handwörterbuch der Betriebswirtschaft, Stuttgart 1957, col. 2095 → B3389.
