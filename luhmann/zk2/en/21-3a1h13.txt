Perhaps a careful consideration of the method of equivalence functionalism can contribute to clarifying the relationship between system and existential prerequisites.
The starting point must be that certain methodological requirements are placed on the formulation of a functional reference problem, which the formula regarding the continuation of the system does not satisfy. It is not specific enough for this purpose, or in other words: no "complete" formula. It needs to be specified. This can (must?) happen by specifying a direction of endangerment. There are always several such directions. From this results the necessity to distinguish multiple functional reference aspects within a system.
It is important that the specification does not occur in such a way that individual components, individual traits of the system are extracted and their existence called into question. In this manner one necessarily loses the system as such → 21/3a1h14
