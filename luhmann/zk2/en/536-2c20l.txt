The question of rationality (or of possible criteria for rationality) under the structural conditions of a discussion system.
Based on the general systems theory thesis that rationality is not a given criterion for evaluating systems, but rather, conversely, that the possibilities and forms of rationality depend on the systems and their structure.
In relation to this discussion, one could speak of something like __“negotiated rationality__.”
