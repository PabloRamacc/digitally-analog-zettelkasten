Two directions of the shift in reflection away from the educational system:
1.) Toward __science__, see → 7/25g3 .
2.) Toward __individuality__; see here → 3411/19 [*].
__Both__ instances are posited as a __relationship to the world__; in both, the ability to reflect on oneself within this relationship to the world is assumed or cultivated.
As a result, the reflection of the educational system itself is shifted to its object.
This leads, especially in Humboldt’s work, to a kind of re-teleologization.
