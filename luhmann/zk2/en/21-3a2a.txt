→ 21/3a2 The criterion for identifying the variable as such is its functional performance for the system's stock, not something else. This is a particularly noteworthy feature of this systems approach—e.g., compared to that of Nagel → B252 – cf. → 21/3a3c -, which forces a highly abstract concept formation. A variable in this sense can encompass entirely different empirical types and externally completely heterogeneous performances. It thereby becomes problematic and difficult to investigate the law of internal order of the variable and to secure completeness of coverage of possible input values. This is decidedly a disadvantage of this approach.
