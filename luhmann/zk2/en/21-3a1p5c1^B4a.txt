→ 21/3a1p5c1B4 Caution is closely related to: 1.) __social distance__ – i.e., contact, especially communication restriction for the purpose of preserving respect. Respect rests on ignorance of inconsistent facts. See → 21/3a1p5c4aB.
2.) __shame__ – as an internal sanction in case of disrespect.
On the connection between social distance/respect (reverence) and shame: Goffman, p. 46 → B739 (p. 69f.) → B814[*] → B814, based on Kurt Riezler, Comment on the Social Psychology of Shame, American Journal of Sociology 43, pp. 462ff. → B780.
