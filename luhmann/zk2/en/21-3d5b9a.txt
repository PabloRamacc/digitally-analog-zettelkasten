→ 21/3d5b9 See also 1961c, p. 223f. → B1227, where it is clarified that the unit/system relativity relates to the prominent position of the concept of norm in Parsons' systems theory.
The proposition that the relational patterns of social systems are normative, […], can indeed be extended to the structure of units as well. One way to make this clear is to point out that what at one level of reference constitutes a unit and at another is a system. What we call the structural properties of the unit therefore become, at the next level, relational patterns which order relations between subunits making __it__ up. Therefore, it is justified to assert in the wider perspective that the structure of social systems in general __consists__ [S. 224] in institutionalized patterns of normative culture."
