Means of deception are __symbols__ that allow one to infer a "attitude" or an "intended meaning" which is not, not so much as such, not quite there as it appears, or possesses more components than become recognizable. The use of symbols can be arranged so that the identifications conveyed do not become visible in all those relations that are recognizable for the communicator. In particular, a meaning can be communicated by omitting all markers of unreality, all negative references, everything that would betray its untruthfulness. Deception is thus always selective symbol communication
