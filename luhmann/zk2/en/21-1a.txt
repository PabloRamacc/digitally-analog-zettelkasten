__On the relationship between functionalism and conscious purpose-setting__ → 21/1ac
Typical is the attitude of a conscious defense directed against teleological presuppositions. One must not assume that functions correspond to a conscious purpose. → 21/1a(R)
In particular, this characterizes biology. However, it is also repeatedly emphasized in social sciences within a kind of critical stance that practically runs into open doors; e.g., Hempel 1959, p. 298f. → B258
