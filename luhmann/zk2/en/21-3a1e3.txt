A special problem of the relationship between inventory presuppositions is their __consistency__. While models can be constructed as consistency-capable, with the inventory presuppositions of empirical systems the __presupposition of consistency cannot be made__. This is merely another formula for the problem of dysfunctional consequences (→ 21/4) and basis for the claim that the purpose model is insufficient for interpreting empirical systems; cf. → 532/4b6d.
