In substantive generalization there is a distinction to be made: 1.) content abstraction; formation of more generally conceived (indeterminate) expectations;
2.) system generalization; formation of complex expectation structures (e.g., things, roles), by which one then orients oneself as such.
System generalization has the advantage that it neither presupposes equalities nor must accept indeterminacies.
