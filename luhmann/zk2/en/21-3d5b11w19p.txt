For systems with functional differentiation, the important maxim: a system that has access to its environment also has access to itself (→ 21/3a1j23), is relevant even for internal relations.
One might even ask whether in functionally differentiated societies all (important) functional subsystems are not subject to this law precisely __when__ they become effective, because society as the environment of all these subsystems depends on the fulfillment of all functions.
