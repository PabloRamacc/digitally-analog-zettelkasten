Stronger differentiation implies a change in social interdependence. [Whether one can speak of stronger interdependence as attempted in → 21/3d2b, remains open due to the lack of exact possibilities for comparison.]
Interdependence itself is generalized: one depends on changing persons, long-term considerations, long chains of causality acting indirectly, and in many other ways.
This style of interdependence requires generalized forms of communication.
Connection with the theory of fundamental rights. → 3414/10 See also → 21/3d5b11d, furthermore → 52/17.
