__Differentiation and the Internal Social Environment__
In __Europe,__ this differentiation requires a restructuring of the intra-societal relationships surrounding education—__ultimately concentrating these dependencies on the state__. → 7/25g7ma ; → 7/25g7mb ; → 7/25g7mc
Breaking away from the medieval entanglement of ecclesiastical / state, and municipal influences—in which schooling was primarily viewed as a clerical matter—is necessary because only the state can handle the desired scale of operations financially, legally, and in terms of bureaucratic efficiency. Private schools will remain, but they will be subject to licensing and oversight.
