6.) Every social system, including a situational system, is defined by __boundaries__. This idea is discussed by Goffman in 1961, pp. 19ff. → B1729 developed in detail and finally (pp. 65ff. → B1729) expressed through the image of __a__ __membrane__ that encloses the situational system like a cell. Three types of rules are involved in boundary determination: those that exclude something, those that include something, and transformational rules that govern the translation of environmental events into the system’s language.X Goffman, pp. 19–6634 → B1729 demonstrates these three rules for encounters. He calls them: a.) __rules of irrelevance__ (pp. 19ff. → B1729), illustrated by the example of the exclusionary effect of game rules (pp. 19ff. → B1729) the impersonality of bureaucracy and, more generally, professional orientation (pp. 21ff. → B1729).
