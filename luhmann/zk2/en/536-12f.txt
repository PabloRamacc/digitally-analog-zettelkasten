For __self-reference and externalization__, see → 536/12b15
Interaction develops as a self-referential form of sociability and therefore requires, as an external manifestation, “un mélange de divertissement et d'affaires” (Saint-Evremond 1927, 1, p. 74 → B2053, quoted in full in: Sociology of Knowledge, Ms. III[*]).
Only gradually does the individual, in their inner life, develop into a sufficient guarantor of the external aspects of social communication—at least in the theory of love.
