Within the conceptual area of "self-description" various versions must be distinguished; cf. → 10/4a. 1.) An operator is available that can discriminate between belonging/not-belonging (example already: immune system).
2.) The name of the system represents within the system the system as totality in difference to its environment.
This, in turn, can mean very different things depending on which selections are guided by which conceptions.
