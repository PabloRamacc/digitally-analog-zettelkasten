If one frees the thought of a recovery mechanism → 21/3a3ld from all causal-scientific implications, → 21/3a3lc what remains is the thought of a generalizing principle → 21/3a3lg. Its core is the function to enable substitutions. → 21/3a3la
The crucial thought here is: The concept of the generalizing mechanism → 21/3a3l(R) contains a contradiction: Either a mechanism in the sense of a specific connection between causes and effects, even within the framework of a highly complex system with feedback and so on, __or__ generalization in the sense of openness to multiple possibilities.
Therefore, the concept of equilibrium—at least under a causal-mechanical interpretation—is inadequate as a model for normative systems because these are stabilized through generalization. The equilibrium model → 21/3a3l1
