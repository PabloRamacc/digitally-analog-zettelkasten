Deception was—in this conceptual framework—the conscious maintenance of an appearance without corresponding reality vis-à-vis other people. Here too, the finally unresolved problem of the social dimension stood in the way of further elaboration. Deception presupposes the exploitability of intersubjective differences in knowledge of being; how is that possible?
