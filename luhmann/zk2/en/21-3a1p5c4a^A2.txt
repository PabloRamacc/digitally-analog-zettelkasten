Increasing differentiation of roles. For example, symbols usable in many occupational fields such as cleanliness, modernity, discretion, professionalism, etc. What is decisive is that not only the acquisition but also the recognition of these symbols is facilitated. The white coat suggests the same thing for not just the doctor, but also the nurse, hairdresser, perfume seller, and even the chimney sweep in London (Goffman, p. 16 → B739).
Furthermore, see p. 17 → B739 (p. 27 → B814)[*]: "In addition to the fact that different routines may employ the same front, it is to be noted that a given social front __tends to become institutionalized in terms of the abstract stereotyped expectations__ to which it gives rise, and tends to take on a meaning and stability apart from the specific tasks which happen at the time to be performed in its name."
