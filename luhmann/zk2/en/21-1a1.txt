It is important that the problem __cannot__ simply be solved by distinguishing between __manifest and latent functions__. X purposes are not identical with manifest functions. This also emphasizes Emmet, p. 84 → B314: "Manifest functions are those objective consequences contributing to the adjustment or adaptation of the system which are intended or recognized by participants in the system; latent functions, correlatively, being those which are neither intended nor recognized. This distinction is not quite parallel to my distinction of 'purpose' and 'function'; it is a distinction between intended and unintended social function. 'Purposive' acts as I shall be considering them in the next chapter need not have an intended or manifest social function." → 21/1a2
