System nesting enables '__access__' → 21/3d8i(R) from one system to another, more precisely: of a system for members of another. Access problems can be studied via the concept of connecting action.
Loomis, S. 34 → B1224 points out that in political science system-nesting problems are addressed under the term "access" (cf. David B. Truman, The Governmental Process, New York 1951, pp. 321-351 → B1666; S.M. Lipset, Political Sociology in Merton et al., Sociology Today, p. 106ff. → B1667) and in anthropology under the term "acculturation" (Edward H. Spicer / Edward P. Dozier / George C. Baker, Social Structure and Acculturation Process, American Anthropologist 60 (1958) → B1668; Ralph Beals, Acculturation in: A.L. Kroeber (ed.), Anthropology Today, Chicago 1953 → 21/3d8i1 → B1460.
