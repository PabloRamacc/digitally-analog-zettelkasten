Heusinger 1794, p. 67ff., esp. p. 109f. → B2456 and 1795, p. XIV; 90ff. → B2147 to distinguish between education and instruction as a fundamental aspect of pedagogical planning.
Instruction is merely the imparting of knowledge: “One instructs another when one tells them what they do not yet know.” (1795, p. 90 → B2147)
The purpose of defining “education” is to enable people to act independently and to exercise independent judgment.
See also 1794[*], p. 4 → B2147: Whoever promotes the formation of a faculty is teaching … Whoever promotes the development of a faculty is educating.
