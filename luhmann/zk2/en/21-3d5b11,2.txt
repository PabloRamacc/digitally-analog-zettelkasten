→ 21/3d5b11 __General starting point__ of the theory of system differentiation is the insight that systems can cross a certain threshold of complexity only by forming subsystems and thus granting relative autonomy to parts, so as to be able to draw on them in their own systemic performances. This also means at the same time that the intensity of regulative control diminishes and generalizing mechanisms with an area of indifference replace absolute central rule.
