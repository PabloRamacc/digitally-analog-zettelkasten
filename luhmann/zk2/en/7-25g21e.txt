Education cannot (in terms of media) be a “microcosm” of society in the sense that it allows __all__ media to play their proper educational role in accordance with their distribution in society. (One need only consider the consequences of embedding power in the educational process: the macro-experiment of recent years!)
Specifying the function and the mode of differentiation also requires selectivity in the choice of appropriate media.
Nevertheless, there is no way out: a retreat to morally infused influence.
