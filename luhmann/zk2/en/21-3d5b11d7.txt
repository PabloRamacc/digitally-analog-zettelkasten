Generalization means __reduction of particular bindings of orientation__. These were, however, diffusely functional and must therefore be replaced in the course of developing a differentiated and generalized system __by various institutions.__ (See also → 21/3d5b11n8.)
In particular, social integration, which was based on particular bonds, must not simply be destroyed but transferred to other institutions. Often seen, e.g., Parsons 1960d → B1265; 1961c, in particular p. 230f. → B1227; Hoselitz 1963, p. 191f. → B1226
Here lies the significance of modern national sentiment; cf. also → 21/3d5b11d1a → 21/3d5b11d1b ; → 352/19a.
