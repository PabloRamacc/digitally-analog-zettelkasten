For the significance of the __impartial third party__ in conflict resolution see Aubert 1965, p. 100ff. → B1496, especially following ethnological material (Hoebel 1954 → B1584).
Above all, one must focus – beyond what is concretely stated by Aubert → B1496 – on the increase in complexity within the dispute system. This gain of complexity lies primarily in the fact that it remains __uncertain__ who the third party will join.
It is this complexity that enables new solutions.
