General education for all is initially justified entirely in terms of the class system: it helps ensure that everyone can be happy in their proper place.
La Chalotais, Essai d’Education nationale ou Plan d’Etudes pour la jeunesse → B2279 German translation, Göttingen / Gotha 1771, pp. 44–45 → B2513 (quoted in Iven, p. 34 → B2256: “It is the duty of the government to make every citizen happy in his station, so that he is not forced to leave it.”
