→ 7/25g56 [*] __Engineering and technology__ are classic points of reference in the discussion of whether __pedagogy__ is __a science__.
This question is __unavoidable__ because there are technology-related sciences.
The thesis that pedagogy is a science is developed __in opposition to__ this model of technology-related sciences within the context of the general distinction between the natural sciences and the humanities.
