The master/servant analysis of Hegel saves the hierarchy by allowing observation at a second order only on one side, with the servant, and thus presupposing and re-establishing asymmetry. But this presupposes that the master behaves in such a way. In modern society there are no masters anymore.
A __master__ is someone who can deal with things but does not need to engage in observation at a second order; cf. → 353/19i .
