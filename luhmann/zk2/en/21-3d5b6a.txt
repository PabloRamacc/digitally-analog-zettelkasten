Based on Parsons, so also does Johnson, p. 56ff. → B273 the distinction between **functional** and **structural** subsystems.
p. 59 → B273A functional subsystem of society, as we have seen, is not composed of concrete groups; it is composed of all those aspects of the total social structure which have a bearing on one of the four functional problems of the society. A structural subsystem, on the other hand, is made up of concrete groups.
The meaning of this distinction lies according to Johnson, p. 59 {1} → B273 In the abstraction of the concept functional subsystem, which allows a comparison of various social orders and enables the question to what extent a social order is functionally differentiated in its structure. To the extent that this is not the case, the concept functional subsystem has for the social order only analytical significance.
