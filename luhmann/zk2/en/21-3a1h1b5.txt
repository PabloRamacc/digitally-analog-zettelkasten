Can multifunctionality be described as __stabilizing__ on the grounds of the idea that all single-function institutions appear interchangeable from the perspective of their function?
Approaches to such an assessment, without being explicitly made and justified, are found in Schelsky 1952, p. 7 → B172.
This thesis is expressly stated for multivalence by Nadel 1953 → B390 (photocopy), which means approximately the same thing: namely, relevance of behaviors for several different role contexts.
