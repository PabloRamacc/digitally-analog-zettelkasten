→ 21/3d5b11 Historical of dogmas:
Most important process: expansion of the purely economic theory of division of labor from the 19th century into a general theory of social differentiation. Already very clear with Durkheim – e.g., through inclusion of the functional difference of gender roles in his concept of division of labor. Intended, however, still as work roles of genders.
