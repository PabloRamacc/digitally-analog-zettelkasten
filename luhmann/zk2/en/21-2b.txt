On usual usage of language p. 249 → B252: "However, Biologists themselves appear to use the word 'function' in several ways. (i) They sometimes employ it in such contexts as 'the function of an organ' to refer to the role it plays in some systems of which it is a part. This corresponds to … the …. (ii) They also employ it in such context as 'the vital functions', to refer to certain broad types of organic processes (reproduction, assimilation, respirations, etc.) which are either definitory of a living organism, or are indispensable to the continuance of the organism or the species.
