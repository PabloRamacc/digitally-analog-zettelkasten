→ 536/4c Questions have the following effects: 1.) They relinquish the initiative.
2.) They are unlikely to lead to disappointment, so they have an emotionally soothing effect.
3.) They do not significantly enhance the status of the person asking—at least in relatively simple situations. Of course, one can also ask in a way that reveals a claim to superiority. But that is already a case of indirect communication.
