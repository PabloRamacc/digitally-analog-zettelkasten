The respecification or operationalization of key competencies is __not merely__ an __application__ of the concept of learning ability to specific subjects or professions (architects capable of learning, French language skills capable of learning, etc.).
Added to this—as it were, an inductive generalization from the bottom up—is the concept of skills that are as context-independent, mobile, and transferable as possible.
