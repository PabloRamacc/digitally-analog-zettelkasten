Fundamental to the explanation of this all-g action-system paradigm is 1.) the idea of internal differentiation (= system, cf. → 21/3d5b11) in terms of system problems;
2.) the actor (attitude)-situation (modalities of objects distinction).
This results in the formulation of four functionally specific subsystems.
Two of them are specialized for orienting the acting person or meaning of objects using the Actor-situation distinction. Specifically, orientation of the acting person is a pattern maintenance problem, while the meaning of objects is a goal gratification attainment problem. Already here lie considerable distortions.
