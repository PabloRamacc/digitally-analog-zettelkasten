__Scarcity and Money__ → 21/3d8c18da see legal theory
Scarcity is a contingency principle, money a medium. Hence no identity.
But only with the help of the generalizing performance of the medium money does scarcity become a universal contingency principle. The money carries the expansion of the scarcity principle as a unified ! system-related framework of reference for the relevance of other possibilities.
