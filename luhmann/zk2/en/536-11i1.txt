__The problem__ to which embarrassment functionally relates arises from __the differentiation of a communication system__. The self observes the other without realizing it and without being able to adapt its behavior to communication. This is only problematic if it is clear that one would behave __differently__ under the conditions of communication. The differentiation of society creates awkward situations at its __peripheries__—avoidable in individual cases, but structurally inevitable when viewed as a whole. → 536/11i2
