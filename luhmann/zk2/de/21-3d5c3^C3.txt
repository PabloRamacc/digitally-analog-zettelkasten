Der Performance-Begriff bezeichnet die Identifikation eines Objektes, sofern es Motivationen enthüllt.
WP, S. 81 → B953: "Performances as objects or object-properties of the situation of action constitute the fundamental link between the situational and[*] motivational aspects of the action system. If the intentions which are 'manifested' in a performance are __generalized__ into a pattern of performance-intentions, we impute to the actor, not a series of discrete intentions, but an __attitude__, which, in the present terms, is understandable only as a case of the __symbolic generalization of cathexis__, which is the expressive counterpart of cognitive generalization."
