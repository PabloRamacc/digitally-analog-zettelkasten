In the context of education, individuality is not only meant __in a self-referential sense__ (as it always has been), but is also __unique__ and __concrete__.
As a result, the concept of education comes into a problematic proximity to psychology (Dilthey, Spranger). It is treated as something that can be established or addressed as a characteristic of individuals, and at this level, scientifically valid psychology leaves it behind.
From a psychological perspective, education becomes a fiction, an assumption by way of attribution.
