The tendency of her answer is: one must consider both functions and purposes (and alongside that also creativeness); cf. p. 276 → B314: "We can keep them separate in the interest of different kinds of discussion, and talk in terms of 'function' when we are looking from the outside on how activities dovetail into each other as elements in an ongoing system; of 'purpose' when we are concerned with the rational justifications which might be offered for them; and of 'creativeness' when we are concerned with individual flair.
This is of course not a solution to the problem either, even if one adds that the concept of role connects these three aspects.
