For Parsons, characteristic is that the problem of system nesting presents itself to him as a System-Subsystem problem within the conceptual categories Whole/Part. On this and against it → 21/3d8d.
Furthermore important is that the relativity of system references relates to the problem of the diverse functions of concrete cases: A specific performance can have different functions in various systems (indeed also: in the same system!) as one and the same performance; with Parsons, referred to different "system problems".
