__Conflict as a Functional Reference Point__ → 21/3d8c4(R)
Consistently, conflict is treated in American literature—whether viewed as avoidable or unavoidable—as the cause of active reactions toward resolving the conflict. Similarly to terms such as tension (tension → 25/1n, strain) or need, a problem is readily turned into a motive that operates causally, i.e., by assuming a law-like regularity. → 21/3d8c4,1
In this way, the concept of conflict already assumes in causal-scientific literature the position of a functional reference point. It enables a systematic investigation of which equivalent solutions to conflicts are possible.
