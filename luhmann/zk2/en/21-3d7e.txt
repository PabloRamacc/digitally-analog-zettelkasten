The preceding considerations lead to a (__cautious__) __critique of the old thesis__ that __a stable system presupposes a stable__ (i.e.: unchanging) __environment.__ This thesis is still today a widespread premise. Cf. Strauss 1959, p. 141ff., → B666 with a touch of critique. The critique however only goes against the objective character of the usual assumption: It does not matter whether there is objective constancy or rather expectability (which nowadays hardly any sociologist would doubt).
Cf. also → 21/3d7c3 on fictitious stabilization of the environment.
