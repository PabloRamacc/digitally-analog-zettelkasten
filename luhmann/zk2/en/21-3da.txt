→ 21/3d The problematic of the concept of system can to a certain extent be traced back to an premature transfer of the functional concept from biology into social sciences. Biology has in the organism a clear, especially empirically referable and limited reference point for functional analysis. What takes its place in the social sciences? And how far can properties of the organism, e.g., reacting mechanisms, also be assumed in sociological systems? See also → 21/2b3.
