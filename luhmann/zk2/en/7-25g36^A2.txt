A closer analysis of the program structure of curricula leads to a realistic assessment of the high degree of decision-making and reduction of complexity that is required.
In other words: structuring the organizations of the education system through curricula leads to a loss of autonomy in favor of the political system (which would not be the case if the structure were determined by individuals). This loss of autonomy is not mitigated, but only underscored, by the fact that educators themselves begin to engage in politics.
