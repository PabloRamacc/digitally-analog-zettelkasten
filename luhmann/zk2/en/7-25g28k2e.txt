One must—and this is an important consideration for the entire technology of social selection—be aware that __evaluation itself is an act of social selection__ and, moreover, an act that takes place “publicly,” and is thus subject to pressure to maintain consistency.
Assessment is never merely a factual acknowledgment of an existing achievement (with no future, so to speak), but inherently contains elements of a self-fulfilling prophecy (see Rosenthal and Jacobson 1968, *Pygmalion in the Classroom* → B2835).
