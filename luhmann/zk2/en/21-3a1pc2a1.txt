All general experience shows that distrust just as much as trust and perhaps even more so __potentiates realities__.
Evidence for this often arising thesis:
Braibanti 1963, p. 388f → B605: "Even if such consideration[*] [nepotism] did not, in fact, influence decisions, the suspicion that they did may have had as much or greater effect than the reality."
This potentiating effect is due to the fact that one must reckon with __potentialities__ and can do so __currently__.
There is also a close connection with the problem of uncertainty absorption.
