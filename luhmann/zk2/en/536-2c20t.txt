Basic structure of a discussion system: __separation of topic and contributions.__
This is what makes the following possible: a.) neutralizing the question of truth;
b.) Neutralizing personal references, __objective__ discussion in the sense of neutralizing the sources of the contributions.
The separation of topic and contributions can be understood according to the schema of differentiation, generalization, and respecification. The __differentiation__ of the question of truth into true or false statements requires a __generalization__ of topics, which must then be __respecified__ by contributions.
