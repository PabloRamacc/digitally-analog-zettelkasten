See also Niemeyer 1796 → B2458 / In 1970, p. 75, he still makes a sharp distinction, because nothing external can be added to education—only what is internal can be developed:
"In this respect, common usage quite correctly distinguishes __instruction__ from __education__, referring to the former as the effort to impart knowledge and ideas to another, and to the latter as the deliberate promotion of the free development of the faculties of the person being educated."
Similarly, p. 208 → B2458, __but there is a note__ opposing the exclusion of education from instruction: “and the truth remains: that the idea of education must not be entirely excluded from instruction, or that, in a sense, every good instruction should be __educational__.”
