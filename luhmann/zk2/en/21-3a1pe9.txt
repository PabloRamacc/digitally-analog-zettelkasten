Highly generalized expectations with a satisfaction value that is largely made independent of the probability of fulfillment through generalization are also frequently called __values__. → 21/3a1pe9(R) ; → 21/3a1pe9,1 ; → 21/3a1pe9,3 ; → 21/3a1pe9,4 In this sense, one can define an approach to a general, ahistorical and logically unpretentious theory of values.
See also Stogdill, p. 72 → B321: "A personal __value__ system may be defined as a highly generalized set of expectations in which desirability estimates are mutually confirmed with little reference to probability estimates, and which serves as a referent or criterion for evaluating the desirability of alternative outcomes."
What is decisive here is the systemic function of values. Through their high degree of generalization they are applicable in diverse ways and thus have a high connecting value.
