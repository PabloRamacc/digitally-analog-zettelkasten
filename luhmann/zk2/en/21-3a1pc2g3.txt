In this context, interesting lessons can be drawn from the investigation by Guest 1962 → B619. A change in management at a factory interrupted a disastrous cycle of mistrust and search for those responsible for errors. The new manager was granted trust by corporate administration to behave relatively freely. __As a result__, he had the opportunity to win the trust of his subordinates and spread general trustworthy cooperation within the enterprise.
A good example of how mistrust breeds mistrust, so that a system can only be restored to foundations of trust through external interventions again.
