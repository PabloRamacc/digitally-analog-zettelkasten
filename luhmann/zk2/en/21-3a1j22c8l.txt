Self-descriptions tend towards the __incorporation of normative components__ – for two or three reasons: 1.) because there is only one system that describes itself;
2.) because description anyway relies on counterfactual simplifications.
3.) Resolution of paradoxes.
This applies especially to self-descriptions of the social system.
See also Bourdieu 1982, p. 149ff → B446 for the connection between décrire and prescrire in another theoretical context and with political implications.
