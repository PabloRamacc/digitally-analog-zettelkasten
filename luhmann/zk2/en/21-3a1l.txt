→ 21/3a1 This formula is not self-evident. In the existing functional literature, Merton emphasizes more strongly the __problem__ character of the unit of reference, while Parsons emphasizes more strongly the __system__ character (of) the unit of reference. For this contrast see also Gouldner 1959, p. 242 → B257. Basically, however, even Merton must presuppose a systematic connection among units-of-reference problems, e.g., in the concept of functional requirements. Moreover, there are no "random" but only systematically relevant problems. And Parsons must regard his systems of reference as problematic; otherwise functional performances for their preservation would not be necessary.
