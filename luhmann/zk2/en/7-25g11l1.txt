The fundamental question is to what extent __teacher__ __behavior__ can be understood and regulated __as a decision-making process__.
A distinction must be made between:
1.) __Theoretical__ reconstruction of behavior as decision-making. See Smith / Geoffrey 1968, pp. 88ff., 232ff. → B2561 under the heading: “Decision-Making: The Metatheory of Teaching.”
2.) __Organization__ of behavior as a decision-making process: In this case, the decision-making process must be accessible to the decision-maker.
