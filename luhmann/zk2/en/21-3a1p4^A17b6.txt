Shame arises when properties or events become visible that do not fit into a specific social system.
Social significance can therefore only gain shame through (1.) __strong systemic differentiation__, by which it repeatedly happens that information that does not fit into a specific system nevertheless gets inside anyway;
(2.) __at the social visibility of system boundaries__. The disturbance must not only be recognizable to the person who feels ashamed; they must also recognize that others can see it too.
