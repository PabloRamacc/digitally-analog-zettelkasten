A technique of shifting problems and functions is particularly important for the relationship between the education system and the business sector:
__Part of the educational provision can be carried out under the organizational control of the business sector__.
Apprenticeship training under the dual system is the most prominent example.
This also means that the politically allocated lump-sum funding provided to the business sector for education is supplemented by this more specific (business-oriented) detailed funding. No political intervention is necessary here (which, under suspicion of class bias, is once again becoming a political issue today).
