→ 21/3a1h1 The recognition of a plurality of conditions for existence and the recognition of a distinction between the system's overall interest in existing and the fulfillment of individual conditions for existence amounts to recognizing contradictions within the system and its viability despite such contradictions.
The usual formulation is that the individual stock prerequisites are only __limitedly complementary__, i.e., not all endeavors to fulfill them can be pursued without harming other stock prerequisites. In short: Not all stock prerequisites can be maximized simultaneously. Or: The optimal state of the system does not equate with the maximal realization of individual (or all) stock prerequisites.
