The problem of multiple system reference arises not only at the level of functional analysis but primarily and initially at the level of expected action.
One can fulfill or cross-cut the expectations of several different systems with one and the same act.
See Barnard 1938, p. 111f → B32, who designates this possibility of contributing to various organizations in a single act as "the critical fact in all complex organization". (p. 112 → B32) → 21/3d12ba
It is a question of the intensity of system nesting regarding the extent to which acts are possible that fulfill the expectations of several systems; it belongs to good organization to enlarge this area. See generally → 532/5a(1).
