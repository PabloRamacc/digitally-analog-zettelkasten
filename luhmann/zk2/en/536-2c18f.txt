General thesis on the structure of time: that time __flows faster__ among those present.
The reasons for this stem from the structure of the situational system—the elementary contact among those present—and would need to be analyzed more closely in light of this.
One of the reasons is presumably that the full presence of those involved prevents “technicization” and allows diffuse, complex relationships—or at least the possibility of such relationships—to emerge.
This is also related to the specific risks of elementary situations.
