Furthermore, the concept of a subsystem includes certain assumptions about relationships between the subsystems.
Here it must be assumed on the insight that elements of a system are distinguished by their systemic membership insofar as any change to them necessarily entails changes to other elements within the system if the generalized meaning of the system is to be preserved. Were elements changeable in isolation, they would thereby reveal themselves as not belonging to the system.
