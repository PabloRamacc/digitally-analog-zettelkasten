If it becomes clear that systems cannot be ordered in relation to one another as whole/part (because this is simply a contradiction of the system concept), the question could arise what systems actually consist of.
This question can only be rejected methodologically now, since it cannot be made explicit within the framework of functional thinking.
One can indeed speak of action systems, but it must remain clear that systems do not "consist" of actions in the sense of a unique and exclusive assignment of pieces to a complex structure.
