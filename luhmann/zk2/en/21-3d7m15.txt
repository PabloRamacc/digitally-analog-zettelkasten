There are __two different forms of representing system/environment relationships__, → 21/3d7m15,1 namely: 1.) through structural characterization of their differences – especially complexity gradients. Here one must work with __identical__ terms.
2.) through dynamic, process-oriented concepts such as problem/solution, need/gratification, expectation/fulfillment. Here the system and environment must be characterized as __unequal__.
A brief reference to this difference also in Hunt 1971, p. 6 → B1451.
