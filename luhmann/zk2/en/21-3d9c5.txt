The preceding considerations can now be specified with the aid of __possibility theory__. This assumes that elements, such as behaviors, are aggregated into systems by being generalized as possibilities and then represented and connected as selections from their possibilities. See also → 5311/2d4. The constitutive function of the "whole" – better: a meta-level of structure – is thus twofold: it consists simultaneously in generating possibilities and steering selections. This cannot be expressed with a simple concept of __relation__. → 21/3d9c6
