One can also understand these three types as different possible functions of __secrets__.
With regard to the __secrets__ of other teams, Goffman distinguishes on page 88f. → B739 "entrusted" and "free" secrets, depending on whether the confidant is bound in their role as a confidant to keep the secret (confidentiality obligations for lawyers, doctors, etc.) or not.
