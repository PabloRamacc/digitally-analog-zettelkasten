Soziologie als second order cybernetics – __als Beobachtung eines beobachtenden Systems__.
Explizit Giddens 1984, S. 284 → B2961 in Übereinstimmung mit seinem Insistieren auf knowledgeability of actors. Zit: "The concepts that sociological observers invent are 'second order' concepts in so far as they presume certain conceptual abilities on the part of the actors to whose conduct they refer. But it is in the nature of social science that these can become 'first order' concepts by being appropriated within the social life itself."
