Precisely because of this renunciation of causal determination – which one cannot quite trust oneself to possess – Eisenstadt arrives in 1964b, p. 384f. → B1284 at the problematic escape route of elite theory. "Innovating elites" are assumed as pace-setters for development; however, they do not emerge automatically with increasing differentiation but only under special circumstances that require closer investigation.
Perhaps this general methodological difficulty is indeed the key to the strong, inexhaustible impulses of elite theory. Cf. also → 533/15c15 with approaches toward a more positive assessment.
