__Textbooks__
Textbooks have so far been an irreplaceable means of facilitating an interaction that is understandable to __everyone__, above all easy to follow and comprehensible, yet personalized. The teacher discusses a text with __a__ student—a text that is available to __everyone__.
See Smith / Geoffrey 1968, pp. 124ff., 239 → B2561: Text as (functionally equivalent to the teacher) “activity-structure.”
