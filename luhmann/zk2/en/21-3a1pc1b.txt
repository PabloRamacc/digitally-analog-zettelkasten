→ 21/3a1pc1 Definitions
Deutsch 1958, p. 266 → B570: "An individual may be said to have trust in the occurrence of an event if he expects its occurrence and his expectation leads to behavior which he perceives to have greater negative motivational consequences if the expectation is not confirmed than positive motivational consequences if it is confirmed."
The following are narrower definitions for social action by narrowing the term *occurrence of an event* and a definition of mutual trust through reciprocity.
The characteristic of predominant risk may be dispensable; however, it serves to highlight relationships that one would not enter without trust.
