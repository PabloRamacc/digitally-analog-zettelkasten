2.) The __definition__ of the problem is in this respect __decisive for the problem-solving process__. If the problem is well-defined, the solution criteria are fixed; if they are ill-defined, there are only "open attributes", i.e., only "open constraints" (p. 292ff. → B541). Open constraints are characteristic of very complex problems. Their solutions facilitate p. 293 → B541: "... that frequently it is just exactly the openness of certain constraints that makes a solution of the problem possible. These constraints provide definitional slack. They allow the problem solver to take a new tack on his problem, not by violating his constraints and setting up new ones (though he may of course do that too), but by adjusting those parameters of the constraint that are open to him."
