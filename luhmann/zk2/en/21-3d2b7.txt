The doctrine of the variability of interdependence also opens up a view to the meaning and organizational forms of widely dispersed pluralistic societies that can maintain very great differences through reduction of interdependence.
Van den Berghe (1963) → B284, in this regard, refers to J.C. Mitchell's *Tribalism and the Plural Society*, London 1960 → B1147 → 21/3d2b7a, with the interesting remark that neither dialectical nor functional social theories have adequately addressed such social orders due to their overestimation of interrelations.
