Systems can be connected in that they provide functional services for each other directly or indirectly, i.e., help fulfill stock prerequisites. This relationship of __reciprocity__ → 21/3d8ab ; → 21/3d8ac (Gouldner 1959 → B257; 1960 → B986; X cf. also → 21/5a3b) can exist both between → 21/3d8ad subsystem/systems, among involved systems, and among independent systems.
X also in Merton 1959, esp. p. 423ff. → B1180
For the investigation it is important that the idea of an equivalence of exchanged performances be abandoned. → 21/3d8aa For this means in two respects drastic limitations on application: 1.) Equivalence can only be ascertained where a common standard exists;
2.) there is an abundance of stable reciprocity relations based on unequal exchange (cf. the → 21/3d8a1)
