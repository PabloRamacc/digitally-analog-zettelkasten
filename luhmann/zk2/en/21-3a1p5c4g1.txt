__Consensus does not need to be perfect.__ → 21/3a1p5c4g1(R)
In particular, the fragmentary nature, limitations, and constructed arrangement of the representation make perfect consensus impossible. The members are aware of the painted-over cracks, contradictions, backgrounds, artificiality, and therefore: the unnaturalness of the representation. They can hold different opinions on these aspects. What is necessary is only that the representation comes to a close before the audience, appears consistent, because any visible dissent among team members gives rise to doubts. Thus, __only consensus about the fact that consensus is shown__ is required. The motives for this formalized consensus may differ.
