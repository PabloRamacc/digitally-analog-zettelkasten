It must be investigated where the necessary system elasticities lie when a system adapts to conflicts of its environment. They probably lie less in expectation elasticity than in action elasticity. The willingness to change one's own normative expectations in case of disappointment (indifference) is less important, indeed harmful, compared to the tactical elasticity of acting. Therefore, stronger emotional engagement will also be necessary and harmless. See → 532/5a22c1A for this.
