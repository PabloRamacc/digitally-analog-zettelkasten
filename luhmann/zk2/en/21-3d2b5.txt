Variability of interdependence means: relative independence __from other system variables__ implies, in particular, that with the degree of interdependence for other system variables, perhaps the reference problems change, but not the other variables themselves in fixed correlation. Above all, the extent of expectation security and the degree of rationality of action in terms of certain criteria and the extent of possible freedom are not __fixed__ to specific interdependency situations. These other variables __must,__ __can__, however also be newly constructed for each level of interdependence.
