The insight that the educational process is class-dependent suggests the hypothesis that the extent to which the educational functions of schools and the home can be distinguished varies with social class—that is, depends on social class; see possibly Kob 1963 → B3121.
In the middle class, there is a tendency not to shift __all__ educational responsibility onto the school, but rather to explicitly make use of the potential of family education, possibly while limiting the school’s involvement.
