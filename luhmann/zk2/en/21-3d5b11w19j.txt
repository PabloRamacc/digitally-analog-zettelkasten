__Societal interpretation__ under the premise of functional differentiation. → 21/3d5b11w19ja
Generally: Society can (for those who experience it and act within it) __not__ __not__ exist. There is no alternative to it.
In functionally differentiated societies, this becomes conscious __along individual further-differentiated functions__. Thus, thinking in terms of "state of nature / civil state" → 21/7s3b and the problematics of "justification of society" are abolished. Politics, education, research, economy, etc., must exist. This is everyday insight; cf. → 21/3d27fB37(3).
