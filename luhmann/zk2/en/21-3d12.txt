To the extent that it becomes conscious that every functional analysis must be conducted relative to a unit of reference, the recognition of a majority of possible system references becomes inevitable. This means that a specific structure can be functionally relevant for different systems and, depending on which system reference is chosen: 1.) possess other functional equivalents;
2.) have other dysfunctional consequences;
3.) exhibit another distribution of latency and manifestness of these consequences.
Furthermore, it must be considered that these systems do not exist independently of one another but are nested through identical structures.
