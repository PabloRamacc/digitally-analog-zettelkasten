The determination of the function enables the question regarding functional equivalents.
Gouldner 1960 → B986 mentions – e.g., pp. 165ff., 174 → B986 – __power superiority__. This alternative can, however, be incorporated by formalizing the concept of reciprocity. Reciprocal performances can include __not only action but also omission__, for example not exploiting power opportunities. And this formalization is necessary because action and omission cannot logically or practically be distinguished from one another anyway.
