A prerequisite for perceptual performance as a systemic process—and, above all, a prerequisite for the perceptual horizon to reveal more possibilities than can be articulated—is __the observability of others’ perception.__ One can see that the other person is seeing; one can also see, to some extent, what the other person is seeing, and can build on this in linguistic communication without having to fully articulate the perceived content—which, by virtue of perception itself, one knows to be shared.
