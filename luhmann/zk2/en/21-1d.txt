According to the prevailing view, functional relationships contain __no evaluative judgment__. → 21/1d(R) One can examine the functions of parasites in the blood of malaria mosquitoes without thereby being against their extermination – an argument that Emmet, p. 78 → B314 uses for this purpose.
On the contrary, especially Blau 1955, esp. pp. 10f. → B326 – however not convincing; cf. → 21/4,4 - → 21/4,5 ; → 21/3,3c - → 21/3,3d ; differently (critically) Myrdal, p. 1056 → B316: "…if a thing has a 'function' it is good or at least essential."; similarly also Nadel 1951, p. 378 → B259; Fallding 1963 → B281 → 21/1da ; Isajiw 1968, pp. 109ff. → B304. Cf. also → 21/12g2.
Cf. also V.A. Thompson 1961, p. 8 → B327: "By 'function' is meant the practical result produced by an action, relationship, event (or a combination of them) in relation to some value or group of values.
