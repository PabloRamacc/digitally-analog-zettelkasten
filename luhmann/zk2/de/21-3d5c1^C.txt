→ 21/3d5c1 __Adaptation__
__Aus der Vorlesung → B702__[*]: "In a complex system you cannot ignore the distributive breakdown of the relations system-environment vis-à-vis the components of the system. The relations to the environment are not carried out by the system as a whole (except for a few special cases) but by components of the system. Components are specialized for certain environment relations, information gathering etc. This is not only a problem of internal organization of the system but also a problem of optimal external relations over a wide range of differentiated functions. Also the differentiation of the environment itself is important. There have to be selected sets of features of the environment. Adaptation therefore means not only elimination of the unfit but ways of coping with a differentiated environment."
