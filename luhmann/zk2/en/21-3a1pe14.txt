__Triebverzicht – deferred gratification__ → 21/3a1pe14,1 ; → 21/3a1pe14,2
The theory of expectation must be seen in close connection with the doctrine of __deferred gratification__, at least genetically and probably also more deeply; cf. → 21/3a1pe5.
__Therefore, learning to expect always means learning to wait__, and presumably (at any rate for psychoanalytic theory) it is the latter that is primary. Learning begins with the necessities of waiting, with suspending immediate instinctual satisfaction in the womb, and forms expectations so as to give meaning to waiting.
