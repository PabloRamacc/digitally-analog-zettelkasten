__Object relationship__, see → 7/31fB , __not__ synonymous with environmental relations.
The object is the conceptual design of a relevant segment of the environment; as such, however, it must be evaluated in the context of other environments (i.e., not evaluated __solely__ in conceptual or concrete terms). Only in this way can it be shown, among other things, that conceptual frameworks vary with the degree of differentiation within the scientific system.
See also → 7/31f1 ; → 7/25b49b .
