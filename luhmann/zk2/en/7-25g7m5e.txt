In England, from the Middle Ages through the 18th century, the main __legal__ issue seems to have been the extent to which the Church could grant licenses to “independent” school operators or bring them under its control—a kind of issue concerning freedom of trade. See de Montmorency (1902) for extensive case material on this subject. → B2302.
This appears, at least, to be the __legally__ determinable aspect of the problem of differentiation.
This is also related to the seemingly unregulatable “Winkel” schools found in cities across the continent.
