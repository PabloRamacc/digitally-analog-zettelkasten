The __internal differentiation__ of a system presupposes a corresponding __differentiation of the environment__. In other words, functional differentiation cannot be pursued very far within a system alone. One often approaches this thesis. See e.g., → 35/5i6f.
It is further enriched by the insight that in the process of system formation itself there lies a form of environmental differentiation. This explains – as LaPalombara, p. 41f. especially points out – the possibility of imbalance and lack of correspondence; see also LaPalombara, p. 41f., → B1280. The differentiated system does not require a fully differentiated __environmental structure__; it suffices that it finds sufficient resonance with a mode of differentiated environmental treatment.
Evidence for organizations: Goldner 1970 → B1281, an Ind Relations Department.
