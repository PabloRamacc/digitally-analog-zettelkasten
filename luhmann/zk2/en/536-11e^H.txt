→ 536/11e __Deviation__
Deviation makes systemic problems manifest—if not in their original form, then at least in a derived form—and draws attention to itself; it absorbs scarce conscious attention.
People turn toward those who hold a dissenting opinion in a way that, according to the group’s norms, is no longer worthy of respect.
There is ample empirical support for this: L. Festinger and J. Thibaut, “Interpersonal Communication in Small Groups,” J. Abn. Soc. Psych. 46 (1951), pp. 92–99 → B1972 S. Schachter, “Deviation, Rejection, and Communication,” J. Abn. Soc. Psychology 46 (1951), pp. 190–207 → B1973 → 536/11eH1
