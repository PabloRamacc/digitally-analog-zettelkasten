__Disappointment__ → 21/3a1pe8(R)
Disappointment is the experience of an event that contradicts expectations.
The term can be used independently of the distinction between probability and satisfaction value, and independently of whether a positive or negative "satisfaction" was expected; one needs a overarching concept. This does not mean, however, that disappointment in relation to every type of expectation and with regard to probability or satisfaction value always signifies the same thing.
