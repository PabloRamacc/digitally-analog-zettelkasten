__The function of coordinating generalizations requires that they must be multifunctional__. Perhaps here lies the starting point for a general analysis of multifunctionality. → 21/3d8f5(R)
This is not a paradox. Rather, different levels of generalization are to be distinguished.
In very broad terms, a coordinating generalization has only this one function: namely, that of coordination of various systems. Thus, however, only one functional class of various possibilities is denoted that can fulfill this function. And from each individual possibility, this general function demands multifunctionality, at least functionality for different systems.
