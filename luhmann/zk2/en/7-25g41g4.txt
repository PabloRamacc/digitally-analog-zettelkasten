Education is highly __situation-dependent__; it therefore relies on having and recognizing situations in which an example is effective, in which rejection or reprimand does not cause offense, and in which a word falls on fertile ground (see dictated note).[*] → B2958
Such situations arise in families, but not in school classrooms. School settings are too highly standardized for this; moreover, they are burdened by the large number of students with learning difficulties.
That is why, in relation to schools, a differentiation from family upbringing develops → 7/25g41g4a
