The process of becoming known is largely determined by "__first impressions__" and hasty judgments. They define the perceptual schemas for further perceptions.
Presumably, first impressions are so firm and enduring because they must immediately serve as a basis for action and therefore connect with one's own self-conception in situations, with what one has been, done, and with the necessity to remain consistent. See also Zalkind - Costello, ASQ, p. 221f. → B650 (not very fruitful) and C.A. Charles Dailey, The Effects of Premature Conclusion upon the Acquisition of Understanding of a Person, Journal of Psychology 33 (1952), pp. 133-152 → B920. Also Thibaut - Kelley, p. 65 → B657 and especially p. 75 → B657 on "primacy effect" due to general psychological considerations → 21/3a1p5c8b8.
