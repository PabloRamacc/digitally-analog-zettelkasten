It is important to clarify the system reference:
The deviation refers to the system’s own norms, not to the norms of external or overarching systems.
Simple systems can be very tolerant of violations of environmental norms—often even demanding them—yet remain very strict with regard to their own norms.
Laxity in __both__ directions has been reported from Bars; see Cavan 1966, p. 67ff. → B1477 This seems to be possible primarily because the system is differentiated to the point of being inconsequential.
