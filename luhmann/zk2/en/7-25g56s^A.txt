→ 7/25g56s Starting point: Destabilizing the teacher’s attribution practices.
1.) He must learn to recognize that his attributions are initially a function of his __own__ causal schema—specifically, that he attributes successes to himself and failures to the students.
2.) He must recognize that, through attribution, he is over-stabilizing the student—that is, interpreting the student as more stable than the student perceives himself to be.
