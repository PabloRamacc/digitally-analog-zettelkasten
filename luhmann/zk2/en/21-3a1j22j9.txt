Problem of __forecasting__ in second order cybernetics:
How can one forecast the observation of other observers' observations?
__What__ they observe! __How__ they observe it.
In a shared world and within the realm of perceptions, this seems to be without problems. One leads someone to the place before the thermometer and sees what he sees in a common environment.
But one does not immediately see the schema with which he observes. Already problematic when observing works of art.
