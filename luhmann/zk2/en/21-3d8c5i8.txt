Anomie as a special version of the problem of indeterminate contingency (equipossibility, everything could be otherwise, undetermined when and how).
This problem is itself a special case of the general problem of generalization and respecification.
Indeterminate contingency arises as functional orientation and requires respecification through contingency formulas.
The __narrowing__ of the concept of anomie lies in its restriction to __normative__ reference points.
