__Education as Reinforcement of Deviance__
The creation of inequalities arises solely from the temporal structure of the educational system. Stratification ( → 7/25g17 ) is a special case that __limits__ the reinforcement of deviance, namely by channeling it in a class-specific manner.
The __root__ __cause__ of inequality is not stratification itself, but time.
Stratification is merely the __form__ of inequality.
