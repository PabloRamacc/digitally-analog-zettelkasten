Selection procedures as a method of __hypothesis confirmation__ under the conditions of (1) high uncertainty and (2) the irreversibility of subdecisions. The decision becomes certain.
Research in this area:
a.) The problem of cognitive inconsistency;
b.) Probable events provide a better basis for action than improbable ones and thus induce commitment and certainty.
See Irle 1971, pp. 156ff. → B3299 based on H.-J. Grabitz, “Experimental Studies on the Evaluation of Information Prior to Decisions,” dissertation, Mannheim 1969 → B3300.
