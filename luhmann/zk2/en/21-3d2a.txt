→ 21/3d2 That interdependence of the parts is a defining characteristic of the system concept should be generally acknowledged. → 21/3d2a(R); → 21/3d2aa Therefore it is very controversial and often unclear what this means. It must certainly not be required (though also not excluded) that 1.) causal interdependence in the sense of mutual causal determination;
2.) functional interdependence in the sense of 'being functionally related for one another'. The latter applies only to models and their realizations. However, a system cannot simply be interpreted as a model. Here lies the possibility to formulate the distinction between system and model more sharply than before.
Another option, which is presumably more fruitful, would be interdependence through problem context → 21/3d2a1
