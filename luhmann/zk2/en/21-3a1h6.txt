The problem of the relationship between system and stock prerequisites is also central to the question of __empirical verification__ of functional statements—and here it becomes evident that the systematic advantages of this separation must be paid for dearly. → 21/3a1h6b Cf. in principle → 21/7.
Existence presuppositions are not empirically indicable facts (cf. also → 21/3a1k5), whereas the continuation of the system is; → 21/3a1h6(R) Statements that use the term existence presuppositions can therefore be verified only empirically (causal-scientifically) by establishing the continuity of the system or those parts of it treated as problematic. → 21/3a1h6a If one attaches value to such verification, their systemic reference must consequently be clarified (cf. also → 21/3a1e4 - → 21/3a1e5). → 21/3a1h7
