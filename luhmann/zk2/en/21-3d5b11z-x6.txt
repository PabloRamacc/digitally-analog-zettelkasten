At the societal level, segmentation leads to high differentiation (variety) __between__ segments. This applies especially to archaic societies and here serves as a kind of guarantee for evolution.
This no longer holds true for organizations. This is also evidence for the differentiation between society/organization.
One could also say: Segmentation can dispense with integrating societies within an encompassing system, i.e., limiting them, and seeking a uniform form to do so.
