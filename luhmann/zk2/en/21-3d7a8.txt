Stimulating in this regard is Angyal 1939 → B1003. See generally → 21/3d29 .
Angyal 1939 → B1003 treats causality (perhaps erroneously) as a relation (and thus not as a system), because it would be a dyadic relationship. His approach could instead be used to show that causality is in each case a system – namely an order pattern that cannot be reduced either to immanent qualities or to dyadic relationships.
