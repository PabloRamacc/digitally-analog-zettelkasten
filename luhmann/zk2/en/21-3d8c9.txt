formulate the finding that a conflict must somehow be expressed (because expression fulfills an equivalent function to the blocked need) and that there are usually several, but not arbitrarily many, functional-equivalent possibilities for this. (See also → 21/3d8c.)
A consequence of this distinction is that __the identification of a conflict case__ becomes problematic. It occurs differently depending on which level is meant: the scientific one regarding the basis of the conflict or the everyday-experiential-thematic one concerning natural experience. When the disputing parties bring about an end to a conflict, the scientist may at times see in it only a diversion of the problem and a shift in the thematic focus of the conflict.
