The process of reduction is a process of self-entanglement whose binding effects, because they have structural value, are moralized and then provide starting points for social control (in the sense of → 536/11e ).
Typically, the rescission of binding commitments is subject to exceptions—it is not normally permitted. Obligation to state reasons! Legally formulated as a prohibition against __venire contra factum proprium__.
See also the discussion by Mayhew 1968c, pp. 260 ff. → B1268 on “moral estoppel”—cited → 21/3d14g1A7 . See the article on “institutionalization” (pp. 1–31 → B1268).
