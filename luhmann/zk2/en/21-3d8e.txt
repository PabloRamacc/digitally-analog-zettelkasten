The basic principle of system nesting is the __multifunctionality of concrete action__ – as it acquires a rationalized formulation through causal interpretation of action.
System connection → 21/3a1h1b ; see also → 21/3d5c6 regarding Parsons' version.
Multifunctionality makes systems connectable in the first place, and simultaneously renders that connection problematic. It is the reason why different systems do not simply exist side by side but, when seeking fulfillment options for their existential prerequisites, claim the same actions and thereby generate conflicts.
