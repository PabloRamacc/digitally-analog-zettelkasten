Within the general problem of institutionalizing conflicts, two fundamentally different paths must be distinguished. They are functionally equivalent forms of conflict containment: namely, __whether specific roles for handling conflicts have been established or not__; cf. → 21/3d8c3b.
This distinction also contains a characterization of the social system encompassing the conflicting parties and a gradation in its intensity of order. It cannot be assumed from the outset that conflict mitigation through specific roles is more effective than other forms.
The problem lies similarly to that of specific leadership roles, which crystallize out of leadership functions originally diffus distributed within the system → 21/3d8c11a1.
