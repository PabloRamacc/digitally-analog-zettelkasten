Dumonceaux 1971, pp. 356ff. → B3176 shows that, among several meanings, __one__ disappeared in the 17th century, namely the expression of will related to authority, which was based on “mon plaisir”: the pleasure of God or the sovereign.
The remaining, more subjective meanings persist; they refer primarily to the subjective enjoyment of the recipient of a gift, as distinct from the gift itself.
