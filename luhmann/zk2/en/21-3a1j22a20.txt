Observing consists of distinguishing and designating, so it is a paradoxical operation.
The paradox lies 1.) in the unity of a dyad;
2.) in a distinction (between observing and designating), in which observing recurs again.
While the observer observes __what__ he observes, on the level of second order cybernetics besides the __What__ question also the How question must be asked. How does the observed observer observe, i.e., how does he deal with his paradox; how does he de-paradoxize → 21/3a1j22a21
