__Problems of Cooperation in the Representation of Identities__ → 21/3a1p5c4b(R)
Identities can be individual achievements by (socially oriented) individuals in the sense that an individual takes responsibility for them, arranges their representation, and identifies with them. __All__ other people then function only as spectators.
However, it is also possible → 21/3a1p5c4b1 that several persons identify themselves with shared identities, i.e., those experienced as common to all. Their representation then becomes a matter of joint concern. It can become the common __purpose__, but may also accompany another group purpose directed elsewhere.
To the extent that in human coexistence shared identities emerge, certain follow-up problems arise. The representation and care for these identities then become a cooperative task, → 21/3a1p5c4c
