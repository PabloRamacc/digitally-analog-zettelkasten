Opportunism theory is a dynamic value theory and must therefore be linked to time theory.
One thought on this: that the ordering of preferences and priorities of a system cannot be set independently of its general pace of behavior. The slower a system is in fulfilling values, the more carefully it must plan its preferences. And conversely, deficiencies (or impossibilities) of such planning can partly be compensated by increasing the tempo.
An aspect of the overall connection between time ordering and substantive ordering.
