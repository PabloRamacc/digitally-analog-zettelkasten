__Conflict / Differentiation__
Verification of the hypothesis: "the greater the differentiation between groups, the greater the likelihood of[*] conflict" by Mannheim 1960 → B1628. Almost unusable because differentiation is seen merely as different funneling of a status conception for the overall group and a leadership style.
A reduction of conflict to differentiation can also be found in Merton, Role-Set, p. 112 → B1459.
