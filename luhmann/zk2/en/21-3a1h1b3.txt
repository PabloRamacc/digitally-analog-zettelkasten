a system within a subsystem (or a structure) in matters of a specific function that dominates the overall system and becomes prominent, such that this function can no longer be served elsewhere without regard for the actual main structure. See also → 21/3d5b12.
In a similar sense Almond 1960, p. 18 → B388 speaks of the regulative role of certain structures for certain functions, which however does not claim exclusivity: "What we mean when we speak of modern systems as being specialized is that certain structures emerge which have a functional distinctiveness, and which tend to perform what we may call a regulatory role in relation to that function within the political system as a whole.
