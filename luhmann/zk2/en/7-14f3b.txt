The effort to make pedagogy scientific is linked to the shift in the focus of education toward __schools__.
It is only in schools, outside the family, that the legitimacy and situational context of the teacher/educator are so precarious that they must rely on science; see Kajetan Weiller, I, 1802, p. 43 → B2140. The elaborate edifice of educational science is a necessity only for the school (not for the world).
