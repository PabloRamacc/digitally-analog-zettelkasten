→ 21/3a1p5c Vgl. Kenneth Burke 1935, S. 214 → B665: "An orientation is largely a self-perpetuating system, in which each part tends to corroborate the other parts. Even when one attempts to criticize the structure, one must leave some parts of it intact in order to have a point of reference for his criticism. However, for all the self-perpetuating qualities of an orientation, it contains the germs of its dissolution. As some people may not prosper by its own tests of prosperity, the orientation itself gives them grounds to question its validity."
