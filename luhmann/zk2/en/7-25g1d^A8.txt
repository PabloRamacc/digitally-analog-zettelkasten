A typical example of a shift in theory that affects only the labeling, but not the content:
__Anonymous (Günther), A Brief Theory of the Art of Teaching According to the Principles of Critical Philosophy, Züllichau 1796 (Frommann) → B2453__
__Content__: Continuation of the philanthropic discussion, including etiquette and the exclusion of “bliss”—__both of which are__ __adaptations to current trends without any far-reaching theoretical consequences__.
Resewitz, Rochow, Campe, Villaume, Trapp, etc., are cited and emphasized; the focus is on: action, experience, skill, and self-initiative.
