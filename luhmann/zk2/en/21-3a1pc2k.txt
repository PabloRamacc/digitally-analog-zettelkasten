The connection between __social homogeneity__ and trust must be taken into account. It is effective as a foundation for rational action. Good example: the __nation-state__. The homogeneity of the nation makes it possible to overcome mistrust between groups and to orient action towards universal and specific rather than particularistic and diffuse (group-related) considerations. See Riggs 1961, p. 37ff on this → B620. Also compare with connections to personnel selection problems via → 42/1A2f1 .
There is also ancient literature on this: equality as a prerequisite for state formation.
