An illustrative example:
Failure to take the initiative when one sees that others are also doing nothing, e.g., in emergencies, when assistance is required, or when adapting to new situations that one (as well as others) perceives.
Passivity of the crowd is based on a mechanism of “expecting expectations.” Evidence: Latané / Darley 1968 → B1944 demonstrating that an individual who knows they are the only one who has noticed something is more likely to react to signs of danger than someone who notices it alongside others who are also doing nothing.
