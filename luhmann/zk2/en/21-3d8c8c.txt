→ 21/3d8c8 This distinction → 21/3d8c8c(R) underlies the plasticity of human emotional formation. Hostile feelings do not necessarily direct themselves toward the object that has pre-caused the tension which gives the feeling its function – if at all __one__ object causes the tension; mostly it is complex conditions.
This assumption still underlies today's literature on conflict, aggression, prejudice, etc.

Coser, p. 39ff. → B1501 (on "Group-Preserving Functions of Conflict and the Significance of Safety-Valve Institutions"); p. 48ff. → B1501 on "Realistic and Nonrealistic Conflict". There cited: Heinrich Schurtz, Age Classes and Men's Clubs, → 21/3d8c8d → B1611
