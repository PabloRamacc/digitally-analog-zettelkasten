→ 536/11eA1 Good material on this can be found in Cavan 1966 → B1477.
In a bar, one can present oneself as an “open person” who allows anyone to make contact (but can also visibly shut oneself off by portraying oneself as a lonely drinker). Making contact involves very few demands, p. 49ff. → B1477
This freedom of recruitment correlates with: a.) The lack of consequences of the contact: People can ignore each other in other contexts (Cavan 1966, pp. 63ff. → B1477);
b.) the ability to end the relationship freely.
At the same time, it means that no great demands can be placed on the social system (in part because of this lack of consequences). → 536/11eA1b
