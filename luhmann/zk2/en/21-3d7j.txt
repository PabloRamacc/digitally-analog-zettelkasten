__System – Causality__; cf. → 22/10
The concept of system must be prioritized over the causal concept—not subordinated to it. A parallel to equivalence functionalism; there under the aspect of method, here under the aspect of theory.
Causalities must be organized through systems for anything specific at all to happen.
Continuing a tradition that always knew precisely that one single "cause" would not suffice, but rather four were necessary: Form and Matter. I replace the terms form and matter with the concept of reduction of complexity, ultimately thus (structurally speaking) by means of the system concept.
It therefore indeed has its traditional background, → 21/3d7j1
