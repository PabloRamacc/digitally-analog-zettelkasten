Internal differentiation must be viewed in the context of the requirements for __environmental relationships__.
To this end, differentiation based solely on the smallest unit—the class period as an interaction system—is not sufficient. Specific requirements regarding the environment and increased discrepancies in terms of subject matter (topics!), temporal, and social terms are only possible if internal differentiation is placed on a more sophisticated organizational foundation (school systems!), which then simultaneously guarantees a controlled, specialized environment within the classroom lesson itself.
