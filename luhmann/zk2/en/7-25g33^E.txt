→ 7/25g33 Differentiation leads to a reliance on verbal, symbolizing mechanisms of transference.
A shift in the focus of education from action to symbolization. See Bruner et al. 1971, esp. p. 27ff. → B1170
Learning through the imitation of actions binds learners much more strongly to fixed sequences and habits, leading to the rigidity of sequential patterns.
Symbolization creates distance (or at least allows for distance) from demonstrated sequential patterns.
