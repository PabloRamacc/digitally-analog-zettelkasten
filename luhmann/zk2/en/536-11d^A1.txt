A distinction must be made between answerable and unanswerable communication.
What techniques are used to make communications unanswerable?
One important aspect, among others, seems to be: __moralization__ (see also → 7/19b ) → 536/11eC .
One attributes a moral aspect to one’s own statements—one that is also binding on the other person—and presents oneself as committed to it, so that there is no longer any possibility of a tactful rejection.
This ties in with the general concept of morality, which involves a modalization of communication through the conveyance of the conditions for establishing a relationship between human beings → 536/11dA2
