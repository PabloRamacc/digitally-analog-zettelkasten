The subsystems are differentiated based on their performance for society as a whole. This contribution is simply termed a *subsystem-goal*, without regard to whether it actually functions as an end within the subsystem – which Parsons → B370 expresses by stating that subsystems need not be collectivities.
A __economy__: P - Smelser, p. 47 → B370: "the goal of the economy is the production of income which is at the disposal of society." Moreover, this production has its adaptive function for society as a whole only if it produces generalized facilities rather than special ones, so that they are available to all subsystems.
