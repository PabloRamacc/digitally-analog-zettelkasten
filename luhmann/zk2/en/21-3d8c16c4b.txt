A medium that is naturally not scarce must be protected against arbitrary multiplication outside the rules of the system – against counterfeiting, self-awarding reputation, unauthorized use of physical violence, merely subjectively perceived but intersubjectively unprovable truth, etc.
This would undermine the function of the medium to convey social control and transmit reduced complexity.
