The fact that the focus is on __the function__ of education corresponds to
1.) the view of education (and ultimately of learning per se) as __a self-referential process__ (see → 54/1t );
2.) the __rejection__ of the assumption of a __natural teleology__.
See on both → 21/3d26g70m ; → 23/11f5 .
These conceptual choices lead to a reinterpretation of teleology. They do not lie on the level of value judgments.
