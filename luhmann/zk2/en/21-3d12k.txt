The assumption of a majority of (non-reducible) system references differs fundamentally from methodologically demanded __reductionism__, → 21/3d12ka; → 21/3d12kc which seeks to reduce complex systems to simpler ones.
Here Schütte 1971, p. 116 → B1035: "It is hardly possible to claim that systems are not reducible to one another [note on Parsons 1959c, pp. 613-619 → B959], unless one has adopted a conception that views theories as conceptual schemes."
Why? The thesis could indeed be formulated as a thesis about differences in reality! Rather, the opposite is true.
