__Disappointment / Generalization__ cf. → 5312/4; → 21/3a1pc.
The disappointability of an expectation and the impact of disappointment on its strength – these are two different things! – depend on the generalization of the expectation. 1.) An expectation is only disappointable to the extent that it is precise. One can speak of operative and non-operative expectations, thereby also indicating the degree of disappointability.
Evaluation Purpose → 532/4b8
Whether and to what extent expectations are operationally specified is a question of generalization. One can generalize away from individual events. The individual event, as it comes about, has no relevance for the expectation. → 21/3a1pe8b1
