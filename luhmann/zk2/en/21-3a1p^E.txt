→ 21/3a1p Another open problem is the relationship between individual key experiences, traumatic events, prominent experiences and the issue of expectation strength.
In the sense of psychoanalysis, traumatic experiences are presumably precisely those events that do not serve to strengthen certain expectations; it is precisely for this reason that they are "deep-seated experiences" and unconsciously structure further experiences. {1} {2} {3} **Trauma**
Traumatically conditioned expectations regarding experiential dispositions are therefore not on the same level of intensity as those experiential disposition expectations that can be strengthened (and thus also: weakened) through consensus, repetition or (and) factual context. They are immune to these procedures for strengthening expectation; precisely this makes their peculiar problematic nature out.
