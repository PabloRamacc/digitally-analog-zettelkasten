__Value Relations as a Medium of Communication__
A central premise of Aristotelian dialectics, rhetoric, and the theory of argumentation had been that when reasoning about values one __simultaneously__ decides on action, because values are guiding considerations for action. → 21/3a1pe9pa ; → 21/3a1pe9pb
This assumption has broken down. The gap between abstractly consensus-capable values and concrete decisions to act has grown so large that value consensus no longer leads to action; therefore it is deemed worthless.
