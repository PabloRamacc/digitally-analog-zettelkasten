Among the essential advances in addressing the problem of differentiation is the insight that social differentiation does not simultaneously constitute a scientific problem-differentiation; specifically, one cannot study differentiated roles separately and subsequently construct an image of the total system from them. For the failure of such attempts, see Argyris, Understanding, p. 28 → B30.
Also see J.Z. Young, The Evolution of Organization Within the Nervous System, Adv. of Sci. 14 / No. 54 (1957), pp. 48-57 → B1261. Furthermore Helen Lynd, Shame → B779.
