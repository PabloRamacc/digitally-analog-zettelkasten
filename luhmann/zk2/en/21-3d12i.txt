Logically speaking, it seems that for a __discussion about the shift of system references__, a __different language__ is required than for discussing individual systems.
The question is whether this language shift can be regulated by a theory of system differentiation in the sense that the language through which a reference change is treated can always be connected with the choice of a specific system reference—namely, that of the encompassing system.
