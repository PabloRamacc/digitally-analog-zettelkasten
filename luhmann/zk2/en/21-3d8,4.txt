→ 21/3d8 __Fundamental starting point__: There are problems in social life that cannot be formulated within a single system reference and which also cannot simply be posed as descriptions and classifications of relationships between systems. → 21/3d8,4(R) It was initially extremely difficult to find an approach going beyond this. See Weber, Durkheim as first approaches.
The recognition of multiple system references for concrete structures belongs together with the recognition of dysfunctional consequences and latent consequences of action in systems among the major extensions that social science makes beyond the horizon of everyday life. The conscious formulation of this extension is of very recent date. It essentially refers to Merton regarding dysf and latent consequences, Parsons regarding the problem → 21/3d8,5
