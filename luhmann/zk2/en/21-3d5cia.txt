→ 21/3d5ci Subject-relativeity, as Parsons characteristically sees it, is not found merely in awareness, but rather as system-relativity, that is, independence from environment regarding goal-setting.
See 1934, p. 515 → B1362: "Ends are 'subjective' not merely in the sense of being 'reflected in the consciousness' of the actor but in the more radical sense of being adhered to by him independently of those 'conditions' of the situation which are outside his control.
