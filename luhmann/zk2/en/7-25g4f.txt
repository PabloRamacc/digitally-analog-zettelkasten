Education, as an idea, could be formulated in a way that was also appropriate for the lower classes: It must be earned through work within the educational system—and only in this way!
Related to this is the fact that—in contrast to earlier notions of education tailored to one’s household and social class—it is no longer the __use__ of one’s education, but at most access to a livelihood, that is tied to social class; specifically: anachronism as a distance from social stratification per se.
