The concept of solvability can be linked to the complex "increase through reduction of complexity"; cf. → 21/3a1k28.
Problems depend on structural constraints. These constraints stem from system structures that simultaneously function as enabling conditions for possibility. On this duality of structure in relation to possibilities: creation and restriction, rests the duality of the concept of problem as solvable and requiring a solution, as __indicator not only for one reality__.
