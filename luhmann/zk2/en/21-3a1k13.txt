An interesting parallel is offered by the more recent foundational legal discussion that places the concept of __legal problem__ at the center of methodological theory and a functionally conceived and thus comparable legal dogmatics. See for this → 3414/11c with references.
Especially interesting Esser 1956 → B531 with explicit development of a comparative doctrine focused on problems, which explains doctrinal differences between individual legal orders by the diversity of systemically determined starting points for solving a unified problem.
See also Strebel 1964, p. 419f. → B532: factual problem as starting point of comparative law, whereby at least the starting point is shifted to pre-legal matters.
