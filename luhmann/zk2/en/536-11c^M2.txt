In the case of internal differentiation, one must consider not only role differentiation but first and foremost the __formation__ of__ smaller subsystems__ (specific circles, groups of people who stick together for a while, etc.).
The relevant questions must be elaborated, e.g.: 1.) Is the formation of subsystems legitimized by the overarching system; is it provided for in its system structure or not?
2.) The question of whether subsystems can be disrupted by others or by the overarching system. See Cavan 1966, p. 59ff. → B1477 on the difficulty of forming small groups in a bar without feeling self-conscious → 536/11cM3
