→ 1/7 __Variable Constructions as Organizational Theory__ → 1/7Fa
Polemical starting point: Critique of generalization based on 1) purely theoretical considerations and 2) case studies (for the latter, see e.g., Pugh et al., p. 291) → B93).
Instead: a proposal for a theory of organization—or, more narrowly, a theory of bureaucracy—consisting of variables that can take on different values both on a case-by-case basis and over time. Each specific organization can be mapped onto these variables using its respective values. The statistical analysis of mass surveys then makes it possible to identify frequency combinations and types and, if applicable—so one hopes—also variable relationships that can be generalized in the sense in which they have always been posited, e.g.: increasing size – greater formalization. → 1/7F1
