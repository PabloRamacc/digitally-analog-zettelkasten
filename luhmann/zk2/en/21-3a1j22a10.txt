The observer (the observation) excludes itself from its own distinction through its own operation. It is the excluded third (__self__-excluded third, like God out of creation).
Michel Serres, The Parasite, p. 365 → B426: "The observer is the unobservable." The observer is the parasite—but this suggests too much of a situation already pre-arranged.
The same applies to self-observation, which thereby becomes paradoxical.
