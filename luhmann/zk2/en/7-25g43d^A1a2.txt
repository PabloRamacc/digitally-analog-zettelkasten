A more detailed analysis of the concepts would need to clarify how the following elements are related:
1.) the harmonious development of all strengths and abilities;
2.) The universalism of the idea as authoritative for all;
3.) Reflexivity.
Mauzi 1960, pp. 113ff. → B3173 also mentions these similar aspects, but further distinguishes between *unité* and *synthèse et universalité* and emphasizes the aspect of balancing pleasant and unpleasant sensations.
