Main actors lead; he becomes a pure figure of representation whose appearance is decided elsewhere. From such differing prominence can arise difficult status problems – what Goffman → B739 no longer discusses.
This distinction, initially formulated for prominent roles, is then applied by Goffman, p. 63f. → B739, to all roles of the team. All roles can be distinguished based on whether their accent lies more in the represented behavior or in the representation of the behavior, and whether greater attention is paid to the immediate purpose structure of the behavior or to making an impression. The latter roles tend toward pure ceremony in the extreme case. See also → 21/3a1p5c4aA8a.
Another aspect is that in representations of teams, i.e., situation definitions necessarily carried by a team, there generally exists a → 21/3a1p5c4fE2.
