s/f
Systems designed as a collection of latent causes that are updated according to selection rules; cf. → 353/1h – → 44/2b2 .
The concept of latent causality is furthermore crucial for understanding the causal structure of systems that are not causally determined. Because these systems possess a latent potentiality of causes, which they actualize according to selection rules, they are not fixed to a single mode of operation. The selection rules can foresee multiple possibilities or, in individual cases, unpredictable and non-programmed possibilities of cause composition and thus efficacy. The potentiality of secured causes is the form of freedom for such systems.
