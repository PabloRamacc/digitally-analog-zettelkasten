The analysis of the problematic nature of representations simultaneously yields, as a flip side, starting points for a __strategy of representation disruption__ or destruction.
Some of this is discussed under the title "Deliberate Embarrassment" by Gross / Stone 1964, p. 13ff. → B621 There three reasons for such behavior are distinguished: 1.) teaching, socialization in overcoming embarrassments, in maintaining and steering representations (p. 13f. → B621);
2.) sanctions against undesired representations (p. 14ff. → B621);
3.) Gains and maintenance of power (p. 15 → B621). This refers to the fact that scenes are constructed so that the person concerned can maintain their self-presentation and maneuver only on the desired course. However, this presupposes a very far-reaching factual control over the situation.
