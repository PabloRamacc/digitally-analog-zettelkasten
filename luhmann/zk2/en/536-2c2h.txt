It is important not to confuse the specific nature of the problems within a situational system with the personality problems of those involved (in the sense of their __chronic__ issues, e.g., shyness, uncontrolled talkativeness, etc.).
Leavitt 1962, pp. 221ff. → B832 combines both—typical of psychologists—but identifies a specific category of “Problems of Personnel”: certain problems may arise __within__ the group—someone’s idea is ignored, someone else’s is laughed at, someone else says absolutely nothing and just smiles, thereby frightening some of his colleagues and encouraging others. […] No way to avoid such problems has yet been found, but there are ways to minimize them.
