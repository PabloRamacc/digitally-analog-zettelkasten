4.) __Conflicts between values or combinations of values__ – see here Braybrooke-Lindblom, p. 29ff. → B509; details → 3411/5. → 21/3a1pe9g3a The conflict does not take the form of a logical contradiction that rules out every compatibility once and for all via an exclusive either/or, but rather takes the form of a collision caused by implementation constraints. Not all values can be maximized simultaneously. Thus, even contradictions must be understood as relative to the situation.
Value-collision practically means that values are burdened with costs; thus they cannot be pursued "purely," without further considerations. That is, values cannot be assessed without regard for the resources required for their realization. → 21/3a1pe9g3a
5.) __Disagreement over values__: Under the aforementioned circumstances one cannot expect that values as such will generally find consensus, unless in useless abstraction. See here Braybrooke-Lindblom, p. 33ff. → B509 → 21/3a1pe9g4
