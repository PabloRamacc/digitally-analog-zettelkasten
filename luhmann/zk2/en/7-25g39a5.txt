In addition to their interactive nature, the __organizational function__ of exams must be emphasized (it is problematic to speak of “domination” instead!).
Exams appear to be an organizational tool for remotely controlling teaching and learning processes that are less readily accessible from an organizational standpoint.
Thus, since the 19th century, the state has relied heavily on this means to exert control over the education system. For a detailed account of this in England, see R.J. Montgomery, 1965 → B2781.
