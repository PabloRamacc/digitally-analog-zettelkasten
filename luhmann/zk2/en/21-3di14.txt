System as a means of cognition, reduction of complexity, gaining more precise and limited questions. Very explicit in Morellet 1769, p. 2f. → B696 – however without the term System. Instead, work is done with Principle and Relation. It is about bringing facts into connection through relationalization:
"If I state one of these facts, it must be that the one which correlates to him also exists; in this case the one I advance is confirmed. If this correlating fact does not exist, I recognize my error." Morellet 1769, p. 3 → B696
