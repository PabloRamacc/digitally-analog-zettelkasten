This theoretical approach can be connected with considerations on variable complexity (see esp. → 21/3d18c; → 21/3d20).
With the increase of complexity of environment and system, an increase in complexity load is to be expected, that is higher demands on the conflict resolution potential of systems. {1} {2} {3}.
This conception is briefly outlined in Vickers 1959, esp. p. 107f. → B1432 → 21/3d8c16ba (with regard to growing decision needs) and p. 107 → B1432: X "No man ever is or ever should be so fully satisfied as a well-fed cat. For the fuller his development, the more largely the possibilities open to him exceed all that he can actualize. A wide choice is a chance to choose one of many acceptable alternatives.
