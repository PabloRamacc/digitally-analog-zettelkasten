→ 21/3d2 Methodologically, the concept of stock is an instrument to transcend the problem of contradiction. Stock must be understood as being comparable to logically contradictory performances; indeed, it requires them.
Stock functionalism is a theory that takes dysfunctions and contradictory functional requirements into account. See also → 21/4; → 3411/5d. This is the central difference compared with traditional teleological thinking.
On system/contradiction in principle see → 21/4i.
