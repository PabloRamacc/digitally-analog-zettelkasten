The critique of objectivity is based on experimental tests that disregard various sources of knowledge possessed by a typical classroom teacher, who grades essays and the like “with knowledge of the person.”
This is the counterargument presented by Orlik in 1967, p. 25ff. → B2804: One might think of ordeals, witch trials, etc., which, based on knowledge of the social milieu, also proceed more “fairly” than chance would suggest.
