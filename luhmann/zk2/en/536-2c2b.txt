They relate to a dual existence of the situation in accordance with its meaning, without serious behavioral difficulties—in particular, without setbacks in one’s own self-presentation as a participant.
In a similar vein, Goffman (1961, p. 44) → B1729 of “euphoria function,” the absence of any tension resulting from the congruence of representations and involvements, and uses this idea—albeit not explicitly in methodological terms—as a point of reference for further analyses that inquire into the conditions for the creation of this—utterly unattainable—circumstance.
