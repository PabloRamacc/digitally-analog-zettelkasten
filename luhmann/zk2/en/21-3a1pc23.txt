The (behaviorist) concept of __generalization__ essentially denotes nothing other than the (phenomenological) concept of __typification__. → 21/3a1pc23,1 ; → 21/3a1pc23,2 See for the lifeworld especially Schutz and Gurwitsch 1962 → B659. Common to both research approaches is also the dynamism of constitutive analysis, which attempts to free itself from seemingly given constants.
With Schütz, who describes the lifeworld as socially constituted only, unfortunately much of Husserl's transcendental approach is lost; the reason for this is the unsolvability of the alter ego-problem at Husserl. Perhaps there is however a third way: to uncover behind the alter ego-problem the complexity problem and to understand how the world is constituted through horizon- and type formation as complexity apprehension and reduction.
