The phrasing "mon système" could be connected to a variant of the history of terminology documented by Kambartel in 1969 → B1084, namely the deliberate __hypothetical__ use of the system concept. Its origin from astronomy (systema mundi) is presumably already based on ancient foundations, positing an awareness among the majority regarding multiple explanatory possibilities for hypothetical world systems with Eudoxos. Then naturally as a contribution to the development of the post-Copernican discussion.
See Losano, I, 1968, p. 97ff → B1085, who attributes the hypothetical, partly ironic use of the system concept by French Enlightenment thinkers to this context. Implicit criticism of rationalist exaggeration.
