__Compared to the economy__, __the educational system has greater elasticity__ (flexibility) __in terms of status change__.
It can change outcomes more quickly because it ties career status to __fewer constraints__.
It follows that exploiting this structural advantage will likely lead to an imbalance between the educational system and the economic system; see Vontobel and Marthy (1969) → B2732, who further break down this more general statement according to a society’s stage of development.
