2.) __Dramatic organization of expressive means in action situations__.
Identifications must – and can only be – displayed on certain occasions. [See also → 21/3a1p5c1C regarding the limitations of representational possibility as "scene".] Then it is necessary to make visible, if possible everything that confirms and elucidates the representation.
Goffman, S. 19-22 → B739 (p. 30-34 → B814)[*] speaks in this sense of "Dramatic Realization": "While in the presence of others, the individual typically infuses his activity with signs which dramatically highlight and portray confirmatory facts that might otherwise remain unapparent or obscure. For if the individual's activity is to [p. 20] become significant to others, he must mobilize his activity so that it will express __during the interaction__ what he wishes to convey." → 21/3a1p5c4aA7
