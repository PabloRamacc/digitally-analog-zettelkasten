The problem is solved in the Kantian sense through morality—as a duty!
See in particular pp. 98ff., “On the Possibility of Education” → B2151.
A theoretical analysis of the problem does not lead to the goal. The solution lies in the fact that “practical reason itself makes such an education a duty, regardless of its incomprehensibility” (p. 108 → B2151).
This is possible only because practical reason has an addressee in the educator.
