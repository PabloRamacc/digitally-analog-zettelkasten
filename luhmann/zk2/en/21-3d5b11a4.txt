__General conditions for progressive differentiability:__ 1.) Sufficient size – with respect to the elements belonging to the system.
2.) Sufficient relief from existential problems that preoccupy the communication system (see Loubser 1968 → B1260). It must be excluded that continuously overwhelming urgencies disrupt a function-specific communicative process; in this sense: "otium" as a prerequisite for culture.
