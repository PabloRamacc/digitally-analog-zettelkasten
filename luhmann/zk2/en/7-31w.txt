__Frankfurt School__
Reduced to __a single__ principle: magnificent and futile at the same time.
Society is described from the subject’s point of view.
The unity of society—no longer to be introduced into society, and thus conceived ab extra, post-Gödelian.
Alternatives, for example: the manifest/latent schema.
A statement on the paradox of unitas multiplex, and at the same time a refusal to make a statement.
