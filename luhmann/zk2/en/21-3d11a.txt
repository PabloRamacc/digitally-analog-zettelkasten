For the __difference between the action-theoretical and system-theoretical approaches__, it is already the concept of meaning that is decisive. See also → 31/3a8.
The concept of meaning is neither analyzed by Max Weber nor by Parsons, but remains as a kind of __property__ of action.
But there is no ontological feature as schema for action; see → 31.
Instead, taking up phenomenological thoughts, the reference to the world must be worked out for all senses. This leads to the problem of complexity and the necessity of its reduction to a "manageable" format. With this step, the transition from action theories to system theories is accomplished.
