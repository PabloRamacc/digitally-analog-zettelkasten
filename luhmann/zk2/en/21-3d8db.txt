→ 21/3d8d In the thinking of school philosophy, membership of parts to a whole is determined by the notion of __qualitative similarity of the parts__ (i.e., an appropriate interpretation of equality; see in principle → 21/15a), which simultaneously reflects what is common and participation in the system. The system is thus conceived as a genus in relation to its exemplars: it impresses qualitative characteristics onto the essence of the parts through its own nature, so that these become qualitatively homogeneous.
