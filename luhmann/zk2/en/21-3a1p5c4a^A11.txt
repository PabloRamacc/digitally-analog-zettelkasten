When the idealization technique is chosen, it involves a kind of ongoing renewal and ceremonial confirmation of corresponding moral values and norms, ceremony in the sense of Durkheim and Radcliffe-Brown. So Goffman, p. 23 → B739 (p. 35 → B814)[*].
A main component of idealization is __hiding and concealing what does not fit__, that cannot be reconciled with the standards of ideal representation. → 21/3a1p5c4aA11(R) ; → 21/3a1p5c4aA11a ; → 21/3a1p5c4aA11e. For this, Goffman, p. 26ff. → B739, gives the following examples: - Concealment of secret pleasures incompatible with the displayed level (p. 26f. → B739). - Concealment of secret frugality that cannot be reconciled with the impression of wealthy generosity. - Concealment of side earnings (p. 27 → B739). - Concealment of errors, mistakes, false approaches and efforts required for their correction (p. 27f. → B739). - Concealment of preparations for representation, → 21/3a1p5c4aA12
