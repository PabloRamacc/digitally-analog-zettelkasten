Praise and criticism are regulated by __levels of expectation__.
This perspective runs counter to their selective function. That is to say: praise and reprimands are not chosen with a view to a selective purpose. Nevertheless, they do have a selective function. → 7/25g57oA1,1
Levels of expectation will have to be further broken down into constant and variable factors, in accordance with the general scheme of causal plans: high expectations regarding variable factors result in __criticism__, while low expectations regarding constant factors result in praise.
