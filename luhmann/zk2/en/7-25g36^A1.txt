As a program with a specific purpose—in accordance with the function of education—a curriculum must, at a minimum, provide a description of the state in which a student is expected to be upon completing the course of study.
! > For reasons of principle, it is not possible to specify both the purpose and the means in detail when there are unknown and discriminatory intervening factors. < !
In this sense, “open curricula” are the only sensible option.
