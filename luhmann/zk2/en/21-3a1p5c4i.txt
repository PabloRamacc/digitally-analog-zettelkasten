__Complexity__
The problem of representation must be viewed more than previously, also from the perspective of complexity. The stronger consciousness shifts from spontaneous to strategically considered representational performances, the more it burdens itself with complexity.
Berger 1963, p. 109 → B514 explicitly points out how much self-control untruthful representations require, making them psychologically too difficult and therefore rare. "Sincerity is the consciousness of the man who is taken in by his own act. Or as it has been put by David Riesman, the sincere man is the one who believes in his own propaganda." (p. 109 → B514)
