The process of social differentiation is viewed by functional theory, according to its general change theory, as a __substitution of a functional equivalent__ (in the form of a complex, differentiated and integrated system order) for an original unity. See e.g., Smelser's definition → B1192 → 21/3d5b11,8; systemic connection → 54/2f.
This is also the basic conception in Parsons' discussion 1961c → B1227, although the term "functional equivalent" does not appear there.
Functional equivalence holds, i.e., diachronically but not synchronically, and this enables progress toward functional differentiation. The newly created subsystems are no longer related to one another, → 21/3d5b11n8a
