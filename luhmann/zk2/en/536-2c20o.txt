__On the Fruits of Thought__
Thinking is too taxing to take place in the presence of others. → 536/2c20oa One can only expect that the need to speak will prompt one to think.
But where do your thoughts go during a discussion? The ideas aren’t sustainable. They quickly lose their spark, aren’t thought through properly, and in the end, all that remains is a vague memory of having thought something significant during the discussion—much like the memory of a dream in which you were thinking.
