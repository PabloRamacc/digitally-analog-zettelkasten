In differentiated systems, the __self-reference of the total system is reconstructed__, namely transferred to internal system/environment relations.
The total system necessarily refers to itself through this, since it also functions as the environment of its subsystems. The subsystems can also be directly connected to external environments (e.g., education with persons), but they remain additionally bound to and oriented toward the internal environment of the total system by way of the difference between these environments.
