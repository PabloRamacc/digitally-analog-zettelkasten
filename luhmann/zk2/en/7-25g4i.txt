The concept of education in the classical period (Fichte, Schlegel, Schiller, Schelling, Hegel) depended on metaphysical speculation and was pedagogically relevant only through it: see Menze 1970 → B2176.
The abandonment of this speculation after Hegel leaves the concept of education, so to speak, on its own—metaphysically orphaned. This leads to a restriction to functional systemic relevance within the educational system.
