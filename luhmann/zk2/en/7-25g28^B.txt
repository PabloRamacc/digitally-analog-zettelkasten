→ 7/25g28 General Theoretical Framework
The general societal function of education must be distinguished from its subsystem-specific outputs (analogous to the distinction between education and training).
At the output level, the function already becomes more complex in three respects:
1.) __more diverse in terms of content__ (with the addition of further services such as the issuance of report cards; → 7/25g28b ;
2.) more socially contentious (questions of priority);
3.) requiring temporal synchronization.
