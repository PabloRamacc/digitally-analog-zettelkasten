The performance concept denotes the identification of an object, provided it reveals motivations.
WP, p. 81 → B953: "Performances as objects or object-properties of the situation of action constitute the fundamental link between the situational and[*] motivational aspects of the action system. If the intentions which are 'manifested' in a performance are __generalized__ into a pattern of performance-intentions, we impute to the actor, not a series of discrete intentions, but an __attitude__, which, in the present terms, is understandable only as a case of the __symbolic generalization of cathexis__, which is the expressive counterpart of cognitive generalization." Here it remains unclear whether ego or alter are meant.
