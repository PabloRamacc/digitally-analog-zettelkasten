→ 1/7D1 Kosiol, G., pp. 42–45. → B41 This is not about the full means-ends relationship (whose connection to the overall framework remains entirely unclear), but rather a division into exogenously and endogenously induced tasks. This refers to the distinction between operational tasks directly dictated by the market and administrative tasks that serve to maintain and safeguard the company performing these tasks. See also → 35/5d2,6 .
Ultimately, and to put it simply, this refers to the difference between production and administration.
