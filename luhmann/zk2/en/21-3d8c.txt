The theory of relations between systems provides the general framework for a __functional theory of conflict__. → 21/3d8c(R) ; → 21/3d8ca ; → 21/3d8cb ; → 21/3d8cd ; → 21/3d8cf ; → 21/3d8cg ; → 21/3d8ch ; → 21/3d8ci ; → 21/3d8cj
A conflict between systems always arises when a positively functional performance is claimed by one system and has dysfunctional consequences for another. The theory of conflict thus corresponds to the problem of dysfunctional consequences, applied to the case of multiple systems. → 21/3d8ce
Furthermore, following Aubert 1965, p. 84 → B1496, it must be required that systems express their antagonistic relationship in some form __expression__ (consciousness of conflict); see also → 21/3d8c9.
