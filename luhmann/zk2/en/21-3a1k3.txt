Since problem-solving generally entails costs (= has dysfunctional or otherwise unpleasant consequences), it simultaneously creates new problems (__secondary problems__), whose solutions in turn are problematic. This results in a hierarchy of functional reference problems, which is important in their logical structure because derived problems change or cease to exist when solutions at a higher level are replaced; cf. → 21/3a1f ; → 21/4b ; → 532/5c8 ; → 5311/2b1 ; → 21/7e.
