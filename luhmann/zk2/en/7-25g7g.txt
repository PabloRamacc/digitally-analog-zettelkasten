Differentiation __does__ __not__ mean that the __causal contribution__ determining educational success is determined more by the educational system itself than by its environment.
The conclusion in Boocock 1972, pp. 309ff., is striking. → B2494...that the contribution of the school system itself is smaller than that of its environmental factors and may even be “declining.”
Very similar to Jencks 1973 → B2420, who recommends school merely as a pleasant place to spend time.
So why differentiate? To establish a performance level in relation to which causal laws can be formulated?
