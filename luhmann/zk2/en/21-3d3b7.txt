Fred Katz, ASQ, 1965 → B1188 suggests replacing the term "theory of informal organization" with a theory of autonomy of system parts – understanding autonomy as a structural variable.
See esp. Note on the Concept of Autonomy, p. 221ff. → B1188, which however provides no essential insight into the concept of autonomy but merely develops the idea that autonomy should not be conceived in terms of deviation, but rather as a structural component enabling integration.
