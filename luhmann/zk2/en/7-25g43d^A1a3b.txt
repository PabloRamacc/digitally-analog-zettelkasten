Pleasure is (similar to happiness; cf. → 7/73a3 ) only to be destroyed by reasoning.
See Le Sage 1718, pp. 289ff. → B3177: “It is a great mistake to try to reason about pleasures: on the contrary, nothing contributes more to making pleasures vanish and rendering them insipid than [p. 290] reasoning: one must allow oneself to be deceived if one wishes to be happy, and this is what makes all men of every kind [so!], in a certain way, equal ….X
X The argument here is meant in a historical sense, not in terms of social class.
