The particular nature of this dependence remains to be characterized more closely. It consists in that a discrepancy between being and seeming must remain hidden, and that everyone potentially has the power to expose it and blame others. Everyone has the power to discredit the actions of others. The scope of this possibility naturally depends on the extent of existing inconsistencies and any possible escape routes for saving the show, also from the "credit" enjoyed by a potential disruptor in the audience, or their tactful cooperation in saving the show.
