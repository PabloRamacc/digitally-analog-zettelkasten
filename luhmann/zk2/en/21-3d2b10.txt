__Interdependence / Boundary Definition__
Only when interdependence is treated as a variable and as problematic does the problem of structural invariance and boundary definition come to the center of systems theory. In classical systems theory it was precisely the postulated interdependence of parts that held the system together from within. When this premise is shattered, not only does it become possible to treat interdependence as variable; simultaneously it becomes necessary to search for processes of maintaining invariance and defining boundaries. Both can only become problematic with one another.
