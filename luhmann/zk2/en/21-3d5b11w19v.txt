__Universalism of Function Systems__
It can be inferred from this that even __non-inactivity__ (non-decisions) constitutes activity in the sense of function. → 21/3d5b11w19va The universe of every function system is a closed universe, within which one cannot not act or where omissions must be taken responsibility for.
This corresponds to the theory of self-referentially closed systems. No functionally differentiated system can evade its own function through inactivity.
