There is an __organization-dependent substitute__ for the concept of giftedness—namely, the student’s educational history to date within the educational system: a history of grades and report cards, promotions, diplomas, and exams.
To the extent that this substitute is developed and made available to everyone and documents the learning ability attributed to them (cf. → 7/25g58a ), school pedagogy can also generalize its concept of giftedness and even promote guidelines for compensatory education tailored to students with contrasting abilities.
