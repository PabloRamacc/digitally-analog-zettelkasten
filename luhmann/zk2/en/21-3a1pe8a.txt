Disappointment as an experience presupposes that the expectation persists; cf. → 5312/4.
Therefore – just as in the case of confirmation cf. → 21/3a1pe7b – one must fundamentally __distinguish between disappointment and extinguishing or weakening of expectation strength__. This is connected to the fact that disappointment or fulfillment are not the only problem factors for strengthening expectations. Furthermore, this distinction forms a basis for discussing the problem of generalizing expectations.
