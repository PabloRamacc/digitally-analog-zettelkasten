__Expectation Fulfillment__
The fulfillment of an expectation must not be viewed merely as a singular event that comes from the outside—or fails to come about—rather.
Instead, expectation fulfillment is always situation-bound. Every expectation is coupled with a wealth of other expectations, more or less significant, which refer to one another; their fulfillment points toward the fulfillment of others or does not. Thus, in the situation there are always "signs" whether an expectation is fulfilled or not. And the person expecting must __act__ during the fulfillment of the expectation. Roasted doves do not fly into his mouth, and certainly not without signs beforehand. He expects lunch, already hears clattering plates, smells something, hears the gong. But he must go to the dining room, sit down at the table, etc. → 21/3a1pe7(R)
