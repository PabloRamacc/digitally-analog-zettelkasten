Regarding scholarly achievement:
Richard Hönigswald, Studies on the Theory of Basic Pedagogical Concepts: A Critical Examination, 1913 → B2143, Reprint Darmstadt 1966, p. 1 → B2144 begins:
“The undeniable imbalance between volume and scholarly productivity, which characterizes a vast portion of contemporary pedagogical literature, stems not least from a lack of adequate theoretical orientation”—a quote that remains relevant today.
