__Aus der Vorlesung → B702__: [*] goal state = optimum relation between system and environment. Postulated is a "tendency to order the relations in the direction of the optimum. Goal-state is a directional concept, not to pinpoint. 'Goal' is ambiguous because there are several directions of deviance and reestablishing tendencies." → 21/3d5c1B1a
From this it becomes clear that __every__ type of environmental stabilization of a system is termed goal-attainment, regardless of whether specific purposes are actually pursued or not. This renders the Goal concept entirely unclear, abstracted from certain empirical states – which in itself would not be bad, but: __and__ unsuitable as a functional reference point, i.e., unsuitable for making visible certain functional solution possibilities.
In General Theory, p. 6 → B955, Parsons → B955 calls the system-external / consummatory relationship Goal Attainment. Intended → 21/3d5c1B2
