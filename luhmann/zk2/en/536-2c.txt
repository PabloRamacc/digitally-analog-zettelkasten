The theory of identification offers the possibility of analyzing interactions as __situational → 536/2cc systems__ → 536/2cd ; → 536/2c(R) to be addressed → 536/2ca ; → 536/2cb and analyzed functionally. Situations of interactive action become systems in that they are subject to norms and thereby identified in terms of an inside/outside distinction: certain actions and expectations are appropriate in __this__ situation, while others are not.
The problems of maintaining identity essentially coincide with the problems of maintaining this inside-outside distinction. If they are successfully resolved, this allows contact to continue until the situation reaches its “natural” (expected) conclusion. Situational systems can be analyzed functionally with regard to these problems.
