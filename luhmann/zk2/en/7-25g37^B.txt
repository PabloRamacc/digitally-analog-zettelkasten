A defining feature of early theories of bourgeois society is the dominance of economic categories __in the very concept of education and training__—in the understanding of the core and the goal of the educational process. See John Locke, Some Thoughts on Education, Paderborn 1967 → B3059. The category of rationality, which lies at the heart of educational theory, is evidenced by economic success and the rational use of the means of economic production.
This is reflected in the fact that, well into the 18th century, work was regarded as the most important means of education.
