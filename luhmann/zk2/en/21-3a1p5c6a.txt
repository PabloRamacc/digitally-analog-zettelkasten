The problem of publicity in this sense is __to be distinguished from statements about the composition of the audience__. In particular, a heterogeneous audience alone does not yet constitute publicness—even though for the performer related problems arise in both situations.
Distinct from the problem of a heterogeneously composed audience is the narrower case of mixing different circles of listeners. Mixing occurs when circles of listeners intended for different representations come into contact with one another. This is a case of heterogeneity, but one in which no unified representation was planned beforehand. → 21/3a1p5c6aa
