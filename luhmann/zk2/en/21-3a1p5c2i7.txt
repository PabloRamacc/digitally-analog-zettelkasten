Youth and attractiveness are exaggerated; one can only guess what the reality is like. Or: a certain "grooming" of one's life story for societal purposes is common. One can accept this as a basis for action (if there is no fear that an embarrassing truth will come to light). The same applies to the evaluation of advertising when purchasing.
In this sense, there is expected – and therefore not condemnable (because it does not disappoint) deception, and there are "lies" in all shades.
A particularly important case of "legitimate" deceptions (which relate precisely to this functional problem) is that of socially institutionalized __fictions__. For this → 532/3b.
