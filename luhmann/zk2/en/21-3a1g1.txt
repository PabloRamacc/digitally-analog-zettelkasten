Functionalism must be restricted to determined "self-maintaining" systems. The traits G are then system-relevant insofar as the system provides determining mechanisms for their preservation or restoration. But this is merely a causal observation that misses the actual stimulus of functional analysis and leaves open why and with which equivalent alternative possibilities these traits are preserved. See also __→ 21/3a3__.
Therefore, it is not possible to clarify the concept of stock prerequisites from such factually existing system qualities as Nagel attempts → 21/3a1g1a.
