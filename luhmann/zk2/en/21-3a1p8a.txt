Perspektive durch Inkongruenz
It is a discovery of the 19th century – or at least that begins there with the systematic use of this insight – that incongruities convey special information and insights. Precisely through the use of incongruous terms, incongruous descriptions, by placing what is described into a context foreign to it, a special light falls upon itself; it becomes recognizable in a sense in which it was not intended.
Kenneth Burke 1935, p. 95 ff. → B665 found the appropriate designation "perspective by incongruity": "It is a 'perspective by incongruity', since it is established by violating the 'properties' of the word in its previous linkages" (p. 119) → B665The term is attributed to Burke → B665' concept of **piety** related; cf. → 21/3a1p4e . → 21/3a1p8a1
