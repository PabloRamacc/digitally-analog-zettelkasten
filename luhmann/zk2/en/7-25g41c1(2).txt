Well beyond the mid-18th century, a purely critical, scathing, and moralizing tone prevailed—a rich but not constructive body of work in the moral weekly journals. See Gebauer 1930 → B3129.
It was not until the last few decades, with the growing interest in public education, that a turning point occurred. Gebauer, p. 40 → B3129: “Until well into the mid-18th century, the bourgeoisie’s educational reform was generally limited to criticism and the undermining of the corrupt educational principles of the ‘galants’ and the ‘pedants,’ without creating much that was __positively__ new.”
