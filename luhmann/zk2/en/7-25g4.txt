The idea of education is by no means an arbitrary, purely cultural invention; rather, it is an expression of the fact that the educational process is guided by (truth-bearing) topics. It is closely related to the prerequisites for reflexivity in teaching and learning; see → 54/1i1 .
Lit: R. Fiedler 1972 → B2464 Weil, The Origins of the German Educational Principle, Bonn 1930 → B2172
