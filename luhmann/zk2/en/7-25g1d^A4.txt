The replacement of philanthropy by the new philosophy is not solely due to an attempt to circumvent the problem of induction.
Kant also opens up better possibilities for representing autonomy.
Starting point: that the synthesis can be determined only by the subject itself—and not from outside—and thus not by things-in-themselves either.
This excludes all religious powers, as well as nature, insofar as it acts cosmically and uses things as a medium.
