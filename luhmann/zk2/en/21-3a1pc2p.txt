The problem of trust must be seen in connection with the general problem of the complexity of a social world and the necessity to reduce it. Fundamentally → 533/12a(1).
Trust is a form of generalization that specifically relates functionally to this problem and determines it as the solution. Trust is therefore necessarily excessive information.
From this, indications can be gained for the representation of the technical-strategic role of trust in social systems, especially organizations. → 21/3a1pc2pa The generalized character of trust reveals itself at the same time as both an opportunity and a shackle – as capital that opens up possibilities and therefore must be cultivated – and which thereby becomes an obligation to present oneself in a trustworthy manner from which one can only descend again with considerable sacrifices.
