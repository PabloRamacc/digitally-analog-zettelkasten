→ 21/3d8c An interesting narrowing, specification and rationalization of this approach is provided by the conflict theory of Thibaut - Kelley, p. 51ff. → B657 It is organized around the concept of __response interference__, which denotes the finitude of the action capacity of acting systems. → 21/3d8cga
See p. 51 → B657: "A person often cannot do two things at the same time and do them efficiently and well. This is the phenomenon referred to as __response interference__, by which is meant that the performance of one response (or even the existence of a tendency to make that response), may be incompatible with the performance of another."
This concept is supplemented by that of facilitation: acting can facilitate other actions. From this arises the possibility of conflict in interactive systems: what constitutes facilitation for one, constitutes interference for the other. → 21/3d8cg1
