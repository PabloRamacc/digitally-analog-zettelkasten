In further extension beyond the religious sphere to every symbolically-emotional connection, including art in particular. Piety in this sense can develop in any social system, even among criminal groups. The term is formal and independent of any moral value ideas.
Piety in this sense is always traditional; revolutionary prophetic religious breakthroughs are typically un-pious, e.g., the Gospel (Burke, S. 106ff. → B665).
