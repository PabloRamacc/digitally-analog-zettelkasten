"We describe a task as __well-defined__ if and only if: 1.) the input categories are mutually exclusive.
2.) the input categories are exhaustive.
3.) one and only one strategy is specified (by map t) for each input category. (The map t may be many-to-one but never one-to- many).
4.) the input categories and strategies are defined in purely objective terms (i.e. different performers perceptions of the categories and strategies are the same". → 7/25g56,9,1a
