Classics are not “sources” in the sense of external references for the production of knowledge ( → 3414/12 ). They allow for an autopoietic description of the system. They are “our own” classics, self-produced classics. But they fulfill a similar function to that of a source-like simplification of knowledge, an __identification__ of points of orientation.
If one must do without sources, then one must at least have access to classics.
