The problem of technology must, analogous to the approach of functional analysis, be reframed as a __relationship between causes__ in light of a given intended effect.
Some causes may be called __“inputs”__; others, __“strategies”__; see → 7/25g56,9,1 .
Technology presupposes __contingent__ combinations of causes and seeks to control that combination with a view to specific effects.
Precisely for this reason, a __cognition__ that overcomes contingency must be incorporated as an additional contributing cause, one that guides the selection of strategies and, where appropriate, also the selection of inputs, e.g., the selection of → 7/25g56n1
