Niche and environment according to Maturana 1982, p. 36f. → B437
__In my terminology__: An environment has __for a system of which it is the environment__, no boundaries but only horizons that shield the environment against a variable beyond. Every interaction with the environment expands or shortens these horizons.
For an observer, the situation appears differently. He can see the boundaries of other systems' environments because he can observe the limitations in their capacity for interaction.
