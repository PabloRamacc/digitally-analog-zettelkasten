The problem of individuals’ vulnerability through interaction as such is a __structural__ problem that is defined morally only secondarily.
This is related to the fact that interaction must be governed by __expectations of expectations__, and that this results in __inhibition__ and __a compulsion__ __to__ __select__ among one’s own impulses for the participant—a problem that exists for him, and in the resolution of which, knowing this, the others observe him. See also Blumer 1953, pp. 195ff. → B1894
Consequently, the individual also behaves contingently and selectively toward himself.
