The decisive difference is that the idea of "survival" cannot be empirically defined unambiguously in the social sciences, because there is no death in the sense of a strict empirical criterion: **"Societies do not literally die, though to be sure a society may disappear because all the human beings who constitute it die without leaving heirs or are permanently dispersed. It is therefore not easy to fix upon a criterion of social survival that can have fruitful uses and not be purely arbitrary."**
