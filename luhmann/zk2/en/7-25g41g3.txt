An important point in Ehlers 1766, pp. 165ff. → B2515: Unlike teachers, parents have a naturally generalized basis of trust (“natural love,” taken for granted and not further explained) that they do not lose even when they occasionally make mistakes. Teachers, on the other hand, must earn that trust through their behavior. → 7/25g41g3,1 (Since trust is much more easily jeopardized here in the specific context of education by individual missteps.)
This also marks the beginning of a completely different style of pedagogical reflection, one that must be much more socially reflective.
