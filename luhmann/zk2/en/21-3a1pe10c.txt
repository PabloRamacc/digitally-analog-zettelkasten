5.) __Novelty__ presupposes generalization. For strict stimulus-response behaviorism, novelty was therefore an unsolvable problem, as the phenomenon of generalization could not be processed by behaviorism at all; cf. → 21/3a1pc10; → 52/7c. Novelty is only possible within typically but incompletely specified expectations.
