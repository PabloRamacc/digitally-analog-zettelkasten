__Autonomy of the educational system__ is __structural indeterminacy__, as __enforced__ by __the differentiation__ of a self-referential functional system. It is not merely a defensive claim, but __rather a predicament__; not a right to freedom to be fought for, but a new form of dependency—namely, dependency on bases of plausibility.
These change in the course of social development. In this respect, becoming historical is itself an aspect of autonomy.
