For understanding second order cybernetics it is crucial (in comparison, for example, to the "objective-psychological" methodology of Arne Ness[*] 1936 → B478), that the observed observer __is not__ as an __object__ (but also not as a __subject__) but rather as system/environment difference and its handling by a system.
