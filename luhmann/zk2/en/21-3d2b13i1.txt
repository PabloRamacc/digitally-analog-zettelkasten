Tempoideologies are all the more peculiar in a time conceived as __infinite__ per se, since speed is meaningful only within limited periods of time. Thus arises the impression that we are working towards an impending end of time to which we ourselves do not believe.
Precisely because we have infinite time in principle, this conception must be ideologically tabooed and morally forbidden, for it is incompatible with the factual and social structures of our social systems.
