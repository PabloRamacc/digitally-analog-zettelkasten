There is a lack of an elaborated reference to the reality-truth question and therefore also the question whether the terms used sincerity (belief) – cynism still make sense at all. If one assumes that there is no pre-constituted social reality, these terms cannot be formulated as terms for a certain positive or negative attitude towards such being.
Instead of this and as a replacement for it, terms are needed that denote the scope of consciousness of inconsistent other possibilities.
__Sincere is whoever falls back on himself and consequently sees no other possibilities__.
A cynic is whoever is aware of the constant endangerment of his belief by other possibilities and protects himself against them through corresponding techniques – or becomes passive and gives up.
