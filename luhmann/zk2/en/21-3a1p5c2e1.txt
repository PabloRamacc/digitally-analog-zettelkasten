The contradiction between being and seeming treated here, along with the necessity of coordinating techniques, can perhaps be traced back to the general problem of various system references in identification. The ideal character of presentation serves the necessities of social communication and maintaining contact among people—that is, the functional necessities of the social system. That "real" attitudes must remain relatively variable by contrast enables personal adaptation to these conventions.
