By the abandonment of one-factor theories in explaining social systems, the concept of a causal __factor__ becomes problematic or the demarcation between factor and non-factor difficult.
Matza 1964, p. 23 → B1441: "A principle of multicausation has other defects. When factors become too numerous, there is a tendency for them to be not factors at all, but rather contingencies" etc., in the sense that the concept of factor requires discernible demarcation possibilities.
At the same time interesting and symptomatic for the shift towards an information-processing perspective within the system, which lies hidden in the concept of contingency (but here hardly intended).
