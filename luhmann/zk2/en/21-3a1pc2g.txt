A special form of trust arises when social systems are formed that expand immediate and simultaneous exchange of performance, especially cooperative systems. → 21/3a1pc2gd
This problem is addressed by Deutsch in 1960 → B569. → 21/3a1pc2ga Entry into such a system requires trust in the system-appropriate cooperation of other members and thus differs from normal forms of rational choice.
Important also is the insight (p. 124 → B569) that "mutual trust" constitutes a social relationship that cannot be attributed solely to personal characteristics (of the trusting party or those trusted).
