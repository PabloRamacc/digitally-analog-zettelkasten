A significant alternative to structural generalization (which is very difficult and not unlimitedly possible) is the __choice of differentiation area__. See → 21/3d18c6f3 following Foa/Mitchell/Fiedler 1971 → B1274.
This implies that differentiated societies must grant greater freedoms in the choice of area for differentiation with complex depth of field—that is, they must be able to absorb greater heterogeneity in this respect.
