Zur Grenzziehung goal attainment / pattern maintenance vgl. WP, S. 91 → B953: "If there were only one goal in the system of action, goal attainment and the zero point of tension would be identical. But a system of action is a system composed of many such units, i.e. roles or need-dispositions, each with its goal. The tension state of any particular unit therefore expresses some kind of an 'economic' or allocative balance as between the energy which is allowed to be involved in performance relative to the particular goal of the particular unit act, and other goals or 'interests' of the system. It would seem that it is fundamentally for this reason that tension reduction and build-up must be treated as independently variable relative to goal-attainment."
