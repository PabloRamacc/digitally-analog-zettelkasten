The Emergence of a “Subculture of Delinquency”: A good example of __a structure-forming process of reduction__.
The starting point is open situations without a formulated ideology (p. 51ff. → B1441). “A distinctive feature of the subculture of delinquency is that its beliefs are embedded in action.” (p. 50 → B1441)
This gives rise to a process that Matza → B1441 refers to as “mutual inference”: “they infer ideology from each other”; (p. 52 → B1441): “… mutual inference is accomplished through concrete verbal directives, hints, sentiments, gestures, and activities. But as long as the subculture is inferred, it is not taught in the usual sense of the term. Instead, it is cued.” → 536/11cH4
