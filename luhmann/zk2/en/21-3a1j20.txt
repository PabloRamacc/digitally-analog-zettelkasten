On the Semantics of Environment and Milieu from a Historico-Sociological Perspective
Starting point: 18th century; → 21/3a1j20,1 see also Georges Canguilhem → B400, cited in → 21/3a1j6.
Theoretical conception: Abandoning the self-referential intra-unit perspective (which, according to Poggi → B403, was still typical for sociology) with a mere reproduction of self-reference.
Instead: Adopting an observer's perspective, for which system and environment are given as __relation__. See Maturana, e.g., 1974, p. 427 → B413 on the relationship between closed self-reference and observer. → 21/3a1j20a
