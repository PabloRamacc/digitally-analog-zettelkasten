The system-subsystem differentiation is closely linked to the notion that the four system problems constitute a __complete__ list of functional differentiation criteria for any action system. Therefore, there can be no expansion or further subdivision at the same systemic level. Any further differentiation must therefore refer to other systems (which likewise exhibit those four problems and none others).
Cf. Lecture 1960 → B702[*] explicitly: "Any more elaborated classification involves an elaboration of the system scheme: one total system and different subsystems." → 21/3d5b14(R) This necessity in turn forces us to adopt an analytical concept of system.
