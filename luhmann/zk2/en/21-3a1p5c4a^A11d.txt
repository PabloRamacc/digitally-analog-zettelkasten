A characteristic dilemma of all team secrets lies in the question of the scope of those initiated: "Those in the group who are not brought in on the secret will feel excluded and affronted when the secret finally comes to light; on the other hand, the greater the number of persons who are brought in on the secret, the greater the likelihood of intentional or unintentional disclosure." (Goffman, p. 88 → B739)
For making the right decision regarding this question, the leadership structure and power distribution within the group will serve as a guide; additionally, naturally, the function of secrets.
