In the period up to 1750, the __causes__ of differentiation or other developments in the educational system still seem to lie primarily in the religious sphere—but they already have outcomes that are specifically related to schooling or pedagogy.
1.) The dissenting academies were initially the result of counter-movements or the consequences of the Conformity Legislation of 1662 and thereafter. However, they no longer understood “dissenting” in a religious sense, but rather in a curricular and educational-policy sense directed against the grammar schools.
