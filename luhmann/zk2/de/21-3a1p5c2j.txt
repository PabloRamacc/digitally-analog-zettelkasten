Die Ausklammerung der Wahrheits/Realitätsfrage im Sinne einer entscheidbaren Seinsfrage bedeutet ferner eine Aufhebung des Schemas aufrichtig __oder__ unaufrichtig → 21/3a1p5c2j(R) ; → 21/3a1p5c2ja (wahrhaftig oder unwahrhaftig) als Schema der Beurteilung von Einstellungen des Sich-Darstellenden.
Dazu bereits Roethlisberger - Dickson 1939, S. 276ff. → B820 (im Zusammenhang → 21/3a1p5c1B9 ): "The tendency completely to believe or disbelieve the speaker also arises in part from oversimplifying the relation between what the speaker says and what he thinks. It is commonly supposed, although there is very little evidence to warrant such a supposition, that there exists a simple and logical relation between what a person says and what he thinks. On this assumption, any deviation from a logically explicit statement is taken as implying a willful and conscious intent on the part of the speaker to distort and disguise what he really is thinking." (S. 276 → B820) → 21/3a1p5c2j1
