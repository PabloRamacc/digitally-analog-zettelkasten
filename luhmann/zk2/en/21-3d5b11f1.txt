The possibility of conflict is given *eo ipso* with the formation of subsystems that are not causally closed off from their environment but require each other as causes or inflict intended and unintended effects on one another. Competition for scarce resources in the overall system is only a part of this general structural problem. Bertalanffy (1950, p. 151ff.) → B989 attempts a theoretical formulation about competition between parts of a system that claims universal validity.
