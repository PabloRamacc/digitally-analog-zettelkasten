For the connection of the concept of respect with that of self-presentation, the insight is decisive: no one is able to present themselves alone. He cannot represent all those good qualities and values for which he claims credit by himself, even in the presence of others. Self-praise is institutionally frowned upon. Moreover, no one would find an audience if everyone were only out to praise themselves. The creation of an adequate self-portrait is always a matter of social cooperation; therefore, each person needs respect as a contribution from others for their own self-presentation. Good remarks on this can be found in Goffman 1956 → B796.
