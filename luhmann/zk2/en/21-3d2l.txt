→ 21/3d2 The thought of relative invariance (independence) or the stock has three fundamental applications: 1.) To the system itself.
2.) To the subsystems of the system. → 21/3d2lA
3.) To the environment of the system (the manner of its structuring). → 21/3d2lB
In all these cases, one can treat relative invariance or interdependence as a variable that can assume different values depending on the case.
