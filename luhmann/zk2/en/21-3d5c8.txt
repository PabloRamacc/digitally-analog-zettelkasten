The four system problems are based on a general functional concept of stability that is difficult to grasp given the detailed care with which Parsons pursues functional differentiation. In any case, this seems to belong here: 1.)[*] The distinction between __performance processes__ and __learning processes__, depending on whether stability is achieved through change in the environment or through change in the system. Fundamentally, a fundamental equivalence relationship is formulated here that presupposes a functional reference point transcending individual system problems. See PV Revisited, p. 481f. → B956 → 21/3d5c8(R), where this distinction is made and applied to the System Problems in the sense that performance processes primarily constitute an A – G relationship → 21/3d5c8a.
