→ 7/25g31 Fundamental Distinction: __Instruction / Class__
__Instruction__ is the __interaction system__ that can, under certain circumstances, be interrupted and sequenced: the concept of the intermittent interaction system.
__The class__ is the basic organizational unit to which scheduling and staffing processes can be linked; see → 7/25g32b(1) .
Coherence (congruence) is achieved through the rule of teaching in classes and utilizing the class as a unit to enable the interruption of interaction systems with an expected readiness to resume.
