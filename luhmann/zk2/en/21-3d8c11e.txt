Institutionalization of conflicts can only succeed if it succeeds in creating __sufficiently ambivalent situations__, which prevent an overly direct confrontation, specifically preventing individual actors from reducing the complexity for each other too drastically. This can happen in various ways: 1.) through rules of __play__ as behavioral conditions;
2.) through institutionalization of a decision-making body that can only be influenced;
3.) through institutionalization of the principle of majority rule as a decision rule;
4.) through ambiguity about opponents and allies via crossing loyalties.
