Parsons' decision for an analytical concept of system results from his epistemological position, which does not allow facts independent of the analytical schema. This is particularly clear now in Ackerman / Parsons 1966 → B1220, where this becomes the central point of the presentation.
In doing so, the problem of the "analytical" potential of others (people not oriented towards science) is not addressed and remains unspecified what exactly analytical means.
