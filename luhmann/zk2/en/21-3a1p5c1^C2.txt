The boundaries of a region are not spatially-temporally fixed lines – or this is only secondary. They are determined by the perceptual possibilities of an __audience__ → 21/3a1p5c1C2(R) – or a group of audiences, namely those for whom identification is shown.
On the problem of the audience also Simmel → B575 and Sartre (L'Etre et le Néant) → B821. Furthermore, this thought governs Goffman's investigations → B739. The core point of regional limitation, that is to say equally spatial as well as temporal, is thus "audience segregation" (Goffman, p. 31) → B739 (p. 49 → B814).[*] → B814
Just like the term front region, so too the backstage audience- or performance-relative must be understood. That means: that even behind the scenes in another sense a scene begins, in which one appears before team members, colleagues, relatives, initiates. Other customs apply there, but often no less laborious ones.
