Curriculum planning must __break down__ the teaching/learning process into __units__ in which the achievement of objectives can be assessed. → 7/25g33a1,1 If effective monitoring and evaluation methods are to be used, these can only be relatively __small units__—especially given the fact that learning paces vary and the process takes place in a social context.
The unit of the teaching/learning process is the basis for its breakdown into units of goal achievement.
