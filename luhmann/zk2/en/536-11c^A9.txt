Presence is a principle of self-referential system formation. Among those present, the concept of presence can be redefined by defining what is present in the space of presence as absent—that is, as the system’s environment (though the effort required for this definition is evident in the system). Disciplining participants against distraction.
It should also be noted that self-referential system formations run their course, and that this does not require any sense of identity at the system level—that is, no reflection.
