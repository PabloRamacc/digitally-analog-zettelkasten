It must be noted that the concept of stock – interpreted functionally – is not an attribute of a system (in particular, no quality; just as little was this true for the substance concept), but rather denotes a relationship to the environment that consists in maintaining an inside/outside difference.
Therefore, preservation does not imply that certain qualities (essential qualities) of a system should be preferentially preserved.
