The absence of a technology does not in and of itself imply a likelihood of acting incorrectly, but rather uncertainty or the inability to determine whether one has acted correctly or incorrectly.
Compensation: Intuition, tact, experience—including the sense that something has gone wrong.
Closely related to this is the fact that it remains unclear how much effort is actually still worthwhile, or at what point the marginal benefit of one’s own effort has been reached.
