In economics, typical of the separation, Alberts’ → B170 Distinction between market sociology and decision logic.
In business administration, following this line of thought, Steffens argued in 1962 → B171 a distinction between empirical-cognitive systems analysis and decision-logical or mathematical problem analysis (operations research) (p. 752ff. → B171). Business administration __must abstract both starting points and combine them__ into an investigation of typical systems and typical decision-making tasks. In doing so, empirical research delineates the potential set of problems within these disciplines. In this respect, the situation here differs from that in general economics.
