As a problem-solving strategy, the secret is a reconstruction of dangers, a displacement of problems. The secret can be revealed, it can be betrayed.
For this there are __two__ solutions to the problem: 1.) Tabooing access and ritualizing introduction into the secret; cf. Barth 1975 → B876;
2.) Remaining unknown about the secrecy of the secret itself.
With the process of societal secularization, only possibility 2) remains available.
