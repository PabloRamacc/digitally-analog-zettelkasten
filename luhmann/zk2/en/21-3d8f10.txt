A special type of coordinating generalization lies in the area of causal—as opposed to symbolic—coordinations. There are system connections through __formalized intermediate actions__ (fundamentally → 23/11b).
Such formalizations limit the possibilities of mutual influence to a few communications and thus force each system to generalize its own intentions, while allowing the other system relative freedom in evaluating such formal communications. See also → 5/1d1; → 532/5a26.
