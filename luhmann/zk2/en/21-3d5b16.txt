s/f
The idea of a __hierarchical order__ is essential both for the system/subsystem nesting and to Parsons' concept of function. __Functions exist only from subsystems for superordinate systems, not vice versa.__ See also Johnson → B273, cit → 21/3,3f.
This connects to the further thought that the higher system defines the "boundaries" of the subordinate one. Parsons apparently attempts here to reconcile hierarchical orders of various kinds, in particular those of roles / collectivities / norms / values and that of function-specific subsystems of an action system through the general four-problem-paradigm. See also → 21/3a3lb2a1 ; → 21/3d19j1.
