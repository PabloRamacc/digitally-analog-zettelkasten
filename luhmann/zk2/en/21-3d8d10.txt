A particularly delicate topic in ontological systems theory is the relationship between the "parts" and those "relations" within a system that cause the "whole to be more than the sum of its parts." These relations evidently cannot themselves be understood as "parts," for otherwise that sentence would no longer hold. Thus, a basis must be found that permits distinguishing "parts" from "relations," and this is likely not simple.
