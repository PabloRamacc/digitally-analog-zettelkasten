The input/output model that operates with a __system__ of transformation from input to output will presumably have to work with more complicated __cost theories__ (or, expressed more generally: with more complicated comparison procedures) than are available in the classical cost curve models. It is not sufficient simply to juxtapose the costs of input alternatives directly against the returns of output alternatives—and thereby ignore both the richness and limits of internal mediation, that is, organization and technology. This would be a view corresponding to theory based purely on linear, simple causal laws, which has been superseded by system conceptions. See here aptly (without however taking into account systems theory and input/output model) Gutenberg → 21/3d7d13 → B709.
