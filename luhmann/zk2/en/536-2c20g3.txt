Summary, p. 47 → B1828: “The criteria that characterize the tangential response can be summarized as follows: The reply does not adequately address the initial statement. The reply is frustrating. The reply does not align with the intention behind the original statement, as perceived through words, actions, and the context of the situation. The reply emphasizes an incidental aspect of the statement.”
Ruesch is interested in → B1828 the psychopathological effects, particularly those resulting from clusters of such reactions in early childhood.
