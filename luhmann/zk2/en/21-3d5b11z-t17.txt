The function/performance/reflection distinction only comes fully into its own in functionally differentiated societies.
Its application to stratified societies is possible only as a theoretical back-projection. It does not expand their development of ideas, because these societies have other problems to solve via ideas.
Within functionally differentiated societies, however, this separation of system references is a (not the only!) essential factor in generating symbolic culture because here the problem of separating function and performance becomes dominant.
