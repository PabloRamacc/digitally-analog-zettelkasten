The __history__ of the mathematical concept of function is the history of its increasing generalization. Cf. Hobson, p. 273 → B339:
The perfectly general definition of a function that has been given above represents the culmination of an evolutionary process that proceeded largely in conjunction with the study of representing functions by means of trigonometrical series. For older mathematicians, a function was understood to mean a single formula, initially usually only a power of the variable; but later it came to be regarded as defined by any one analytical expression and was extended by Euler to include the case in which the function is given implicitly by a formal relation between the two variables.
