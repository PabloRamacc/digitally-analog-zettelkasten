A consequence of this structurally induced acceleration is the emergence of __tempo-ideologies__ that elevate the urgency structurally produced as unquestionably valid norm and apply it to any goal whatsoever. One can therefore also speak of goal-variable tempo-ideologies, or in this sense of formal tempo-ideologies. The goals may change; the urgency itself is a continuous phenomenon: university planning, atomic industry, expansion of transport networks, even the rescue of testimonies from the past are done hurriedly.
There exists a close connection with the problem circle of opportunism. Tempo-ideologies are opportunistically inclined because they can switch their goals and only foresee the tempo itself as constant. See also → 21/3a1pe9g .
