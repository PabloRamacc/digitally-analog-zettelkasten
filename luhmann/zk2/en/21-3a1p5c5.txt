__Relationship among several identities to one another__
Especially problematic is the relationship of self-presentation to other social identifications: roles, status, situational definitions, worldviews.
It seems that __consistency with one's own social self is the leading principle for the selection and presentation of other social identifications.__ This may be explained by the fact that all communications given or received are ultimately self-referential and that social identifications have the meaning of steering communicative interaction with other people. Through his identifications, and only so, can one reach another person in what he understands himself to be.
