In this context, the functions of situational structure and __normative behavioral expectations__ become apparent. They allow individuals to orient themselves toward what is appropriate and expected in each situation—rather than relying on the foresight of the consequences of their actions, which is not possible so quickly.
Consequently, it is very typical to find little instrumental rationality in social situations, and those who are relatively experienced, trained, or gifted have an advantage because they are better able to recognize the consequences of their actions and orient themselves accordingly.
