In another sense, Bales brings forth the idea of a hierarchy of problem levels. Problems are ordered such that solving one presupposes the solution of the other. Similar to here, only lacking the concept of functional equivalence, so that the order consists not in an ordering of problem-substitution possibilities but merely in a division of certain problems.
