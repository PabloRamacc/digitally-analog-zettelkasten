Goffman 1957 → B1764 treats—albeit not with complete abstraction regarding situational systems per se, and therefore with unclear implications—the __spontaneous reference to the situational topic__ as the core problem of interactive systems. According to this, the degree of immediacy and sincerity of engagement would simultaneously serve as a criterion for the success of the situation.
If that were what was meant—and Goffman → B1764 at least treats the threats to spontaneity as threats to the situation as well—then this must be contradicted; for spontaneity can very well be replaced by reflection and tactical strategy without harming the continuation of the interaction.
