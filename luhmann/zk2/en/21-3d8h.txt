__Connecting Roles__
cf. → 433/7; → 44/7; → 534/1b1; → 21/3d8h(R); → 21/3d8,7a
All system nesting is based on the multiple systemic membership of concrete actions. In this sense, the existence of connecting actions is built into the problem formulation of system nesting. Connecting actions occupy a special position among those that do not have this dual function; they can be examined in this capacity.
When this problematic becomes conscious in social life and serves as the basis for planned, function-specific organization (or expectation order) of actions, one may speak of connecting roles.
