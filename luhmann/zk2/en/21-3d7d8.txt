The Input/Output model presupposes selective standards for evaluating input and output, → 21/3d7d8a whose operation simultaneously mediates the relationship between input and output and thus fulfills central functions for the system.
See Herbst 1961, p. 85ff. → B988, who deals with two "evaluation mechanisms": Input satisfaction and performance satisfaction: p. 87 → B988: "In the case of (i) performance satisfaction, the output is judged in relation to the effort exerted and other costs in the form of supplies expended. In the case of (ii) input satisfaction, which corresponds to satisfaction with reward, income, or reciprocated service received, judgement concerns the returns received relative to the expended output."
