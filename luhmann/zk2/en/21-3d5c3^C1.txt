The property of objects is their probable __performance__ in respect to actors oriented toward them. Recall that the prototype of the actor-object relation is social interaction, in which the 'object' is also in turn an actor who does something. Thus physical objects, which do not 'act', are the limiting case of objects to which the term 'performance' is inherently __inapplicable__. In contrast with this situation lies the meaning of objects in terms of what they '__are__,' their qualities as[*] defined independently of performances, which are inherently relative to situations. The internal reference of the acting system matches interest in the qualities of objects rather than their performances, since these are presumptively more independent of direct situational exigencies."
