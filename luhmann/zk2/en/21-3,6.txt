Sense variable constructions are designed to accommodate variation of their variables in relation to one another. It may be that empirically concrete systems found "as they are" possess a (relatively strict) lawfulness in this sense on their own. To the extent that this is the case, models also serve as theories. However, it must then be ensured that every state of reality can be interpreted as an application case and every process in reality as a variation of the model. It is __not__ required – what is unfortunately often demanded anyway – that the model permits predictions about changes to the empirical system.
