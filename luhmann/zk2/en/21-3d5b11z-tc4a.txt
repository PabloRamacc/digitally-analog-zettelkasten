General Idea: Identity participates in the difference between general and particular; it is so to speak the __parasite__ of this difference (just as work is the parasite of the difference between rich and poor).
The particular is within the universal when one says, Tosca is a dog Bauschan[*] is a dog. It is and at the same time not the general, and the general is and at the same time not only itself;
