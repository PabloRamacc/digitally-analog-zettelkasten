The elasticity of an organization rests largely on the fact that it nests various coordinating generalizations and assigns them to one another such that a multitude of limited variation possibilities arises—that is, possibilities for change with adaptation through changes having only limited repercussions. Thus, for example, __offices__ are endowed with specific __authority__, establishing certain __decision programs__ based on the __organizational purpose__. All these terms denote multifunctional identifications representing variable complexes in which each other functions as a variable among others. Therefore, changing individual variables can generally be absorbed through internal balancing without calling into question the system's stock.
