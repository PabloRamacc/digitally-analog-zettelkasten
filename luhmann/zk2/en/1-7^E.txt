→ 1/7 __Group Theory as Organizational Theory__
As extensive as research on groups in organizations is, attempts to develop a theory of organization based entirely on the concept of small groups are rare. Group theory has so far served primarily as a subtheory, a tool for solving classically defined organizational problems—above all, motivation!—but not as a frame of reference for an organizational theory.
