The effective separation of front region and backstage and the differentiation of symbols and behavior in both areas presuppose, once a difference reaches a certain extent, a functioning __warning system__ that becomes operative if spectators approach the backstage. The warning system must be timed so that the scene can still be cleared up and performance initiated before the spectator arrives. Only under the protection of such a warning system is effective recovery and appropriate activity in the backstage possible. See also Goffman, p. 145 → B739.
