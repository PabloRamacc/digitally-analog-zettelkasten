The distinction between __presence__ and __absence__ affects the __structure of communication possibilities__. In this respect, the principle of self-selection and system boundaries influence the selection of topics and, consequently, the self-selection of the system.
One can only speak __with__ those who are present, not __about__ those who are present; and conversely, one can only __speak about__ those who are absent, not __with__ those who are absent.
