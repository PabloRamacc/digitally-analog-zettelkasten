The simple assertion of a connection between differentiation and generalization is insufficient. One of the most important complementary insights states: __that different [things] require social differentiation, that different levels of generalization must be distinguished and institutionalized differently, so that lower ones can be changed without feedback to higher ones__. → 21/3d5b11d10a Above all, within invariant __values__, changes in __norms__ must be possible. This can occur through the institutionalization of a distinction between values and norms.
See Smelser 1963, p. 278ff. → B658, who sees therein the structural precondition for a certain type of collective behavior, namely norm-oriented movement, as it occurs only in differentiated social orders → 21/3d5b11d11
