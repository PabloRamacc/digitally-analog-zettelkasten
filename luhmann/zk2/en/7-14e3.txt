The justification of pedagogy’s scientific nature is linked to a shift in the concept of science.
The verdict on technology, which arises from the theory of the subject (the individual), shatters the association between science and technology that applied to philanthropy.
Therefore, the concept of science must be redefined on a new basis—one that is, so to speak, metatechnological or technology-free.
This is achieved by drawing on Kantian philosophy and its certainties—which were preformulated for the field of education.
