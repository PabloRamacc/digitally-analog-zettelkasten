To the extent that a discrepancy arises in the practical execution of the representation, it must be concealed – concealment of the techniques and motives for acquiring the position from which the idealized presentation is made; the representation itself must appear as the sole motive. Or conversely: staging access to a particular mode of approach to the position (ritual introduction, leave periods, preparations and learning times) and concealing 1.) deviations and 2.) that they are essentially not required but serve only the function of highlighting and distancing; Goffman, p. 29f → B739, who in this regard speaks of a "rhetoric of training".
To these then – by Goffman, op.cit. → B739 not mentioned – come techniques for concealing concealment with the efforts required therefor and techniques for concealing subjective feelings connected to knowledge about what is concealed and thus linked to the precariousness of the representation: fear, nervousness, relaxation etc., which as such are revealing → 21/3a1p5c4aA14.
