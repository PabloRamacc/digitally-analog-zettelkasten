Here, a typology by Coleman 1971, p. 86 → B1291 is stimulating for this purpose: 1.) "__primordial differentiation__" (pre-existing, segmenting)
2.) "__modernizing differentiation__" (fragmentation; in the result functional differentiation especially in the politically-administrative area)
3.")"__compounded differentiation__" (= corporatism; the dividing lines demanded in 1.) and 2.) → 21/3d5b11z/z1a1
