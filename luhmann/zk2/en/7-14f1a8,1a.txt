→ 7/14f1a8,1 Reference: The view of education as the development of aptitudes into useful qualities—an almost Aristotelian theoretical approach, yet one that is presented as entirely new.
This leads to all the aporias (and is well suited for their study) that arise when one wishes to regard only the possible as real and self-effective.
How can something create something else? How can the possible become real?—this is discussed, according to Kant, in relation to the object!
In a theory of this type, the regulation of the educator’s behavior takes a back seat to the object. → 7/14f1a8,1b
