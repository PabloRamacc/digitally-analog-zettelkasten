It must be examined whether it makes sense – and what kind of sense – to place stock prerequisites into a hierarchical relationship so that some are more important than others. Nagel 1956, p. 272 → B252 assumes this possibility without further analysis.
For the time being only a hierarchical order in terms of gradation of functional problems is conceivable: there are prerequisites from which existence directly depends and others from which existence depends on performances that are provided for those existential prerequisites.
