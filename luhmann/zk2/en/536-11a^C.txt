→ 536/11a One of the difficulties with the concept of interaction is the failure of a purely causal approach.
Recognizing this is the central achievement of symbolic interactionism (in which it converges with systems theory).
Interaction cannot be understood as __a reciprocal exchange__, if only because of the temporal structure of human experience.
This relationship to the popular, simplistic concept of causality makes interaction difficult for the general public to understand.
