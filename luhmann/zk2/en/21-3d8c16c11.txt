It is important that the notion of scarcity can be developed and defined without reference to social contingency. This does not mean that it has nothing to do with social contingency, but rather indicates that it serves to neutralize social contingency – as a kind of functional equivalent for law.
Scarcity orientation serves the reconstruction of social contingency in a form in which it no longer appears itself, but is instead regulated away.
