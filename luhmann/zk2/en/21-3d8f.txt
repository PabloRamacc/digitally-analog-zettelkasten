The theory of nested systems includes a theory of __coordinating generalizations__. → 21/3d8f(R)
Coordinating generalizations have the function of connecting multiple action systems that are independently variable in such a way that their variations are consistent with one another.
This happens by means of symbolic generalizations that maintain the variation limits each system imposes on the other. That is to say: Those perspectives or problem formulations are fixed which arise for each system from its combination with others. And these problem formulations are utilized in various directions, i.e., as reference points relative to individual systems for functional analyses.
