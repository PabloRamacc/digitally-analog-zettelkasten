s/f
One can attempt to clarify the concept of system in a methodologically-operational manner. __System shall denote every connection whose existence is problematic and which can be made into an object of functional analysis.__ System is simply the designation for the minimum prerequisites of an existential-functional analysis. This is, of course, only a formal or operational definition, not a substantive description. But it contains quite clear indications for elaborating a substantive description. See also → 532/1c.
