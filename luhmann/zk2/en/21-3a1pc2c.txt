__On Generalization / Trust:__ → 21/3a1pc2c(R)
The essence of trust consists in a __global confidence about the future__. It does not simply consist in expectations of continuity, in extending currently conceived states into the future. Rather, trust is productive; it creates something new. Trust is the confidence that an identification will persist even in light of future changes and dangers, indeed in face of unspecified or entirely unconceived alterations. What matters for trust is that it includes and mitigates the moment of as yet inconceivable change and danger. Trust overcomes the insecurity arising from the fact that one cannot arm oneself against precisely unforeseeable changes.
