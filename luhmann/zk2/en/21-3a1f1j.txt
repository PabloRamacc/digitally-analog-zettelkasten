The critique of the stock formula must be acknowledged from the perspective of the __complexity problem__. → 21/3a1f1ja The stock formula has the disadvantage that it does not absorb enough complexity to suggest clear scientific hypotheses as such or even provide decision criteria. For this reason, Shepard 1964, p. 258 → B373 expresses skepticism about the idea of defining an optimum through survival. → 21/3a1f1jb On the other hand, precisely this disadvantage is the advantage of the stock formula: it does not presuppose complexity as already absorbed; however, initially it has greater openness to complexity – since everything can affect the stock –, but on its own – as a formula – it is unable also to absorb this complexity. Therefore: transition to systems theory.
This insight simultaneously relativizes the difference between purpose formula and stock formula into one that is more gradual → 21/3a1f1j1
