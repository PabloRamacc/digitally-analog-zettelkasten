Beyond the normal distinction between concept (as a means of cognition) and object, the thesis states that the observer must conceive __himself__ as a system in order to be able to observe systems. Self-observation as a prerequisite for observing others, and both coordinated by the __all-encompassing system__.
So Le Moigne 1977, p. 55ff. → B199 under the title système de représentation (not very happily chosen)
