Strosetzki 1978, p. 114 → B1999 emphasizes the __coherence of the concept__ of bien-séance: it promises to provide for every possible situation. Anyone who knows how to practice bien-séance—or has learned to do so—is capable of handling any social situation and is protected against the unexpected.
(At the same time, this precludes full consideration of one’s own and others’ individuality.)
