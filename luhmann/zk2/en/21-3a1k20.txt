Functionalism reaches its most radical stage when it seeks to pose __being as a problem__ – that is, with Heidegger.
Why Being and Nothingness is Nothing – this problem formulation drawn by Heidegger from the tradition reveals the possibility that everything could be otherwise.
In it is simultaneously revealed a previously unattainable possibility of understanding our history of thought from a—though remaining empty—possibility of being otherwise.
