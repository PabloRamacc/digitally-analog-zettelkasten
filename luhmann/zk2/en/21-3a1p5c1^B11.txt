Generally, following Glaser / Strauss 1964 → B810, one must inquire about the scope of consciousness in interactions: what does every participant know about the identity of the other. One should not restrict oneself a priori to situations where, as Goffman usually assumes, full commonality of knowledge is harmful. Especially in differentiated societies, where role excerpts are anyway mostly relevant, the Goffmanian situation is quite atypical. Hence the picturesque, anecdotal, situational, bohemian atmosphere of his writings.
