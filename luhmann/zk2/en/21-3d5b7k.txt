Only analytical systems can meaningfully be said to consist of variables. Concrete systems have limited possibilities for change.
The concept of variables denotes an abstraction of the possibilities for change in a system that also includes those which cannot be concretely realized at all (e.g., due to reasons of concrete compatibility). {1} → 21/3d5b7ka
This consideration shows that analytical systems contain too many possibilities. They serve to make reality appear as a selection (and thus: as comprehensible).
