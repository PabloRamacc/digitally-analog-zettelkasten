__Subject Matter as Resources:__ Systemic Linkage → 21/3d5b11z/w ; → 7/25g46,1
"Subject matter," topics, and learning objects are not necessarily identical to learning objectives (states of individuals); they are means of the learning process and have effects that cannot be precisely measured by whether or not the subject matter is subsequently present in people’s minds.
This is where the concept of resource mobilization (or liquidity) comes into play: liberation from ascriptive constraints.
