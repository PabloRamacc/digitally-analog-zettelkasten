Differentiation of the economy means that despite very different non-economic role entanglements and personal motivations of the actors, action within the economic context is conducted uniformly and predictably according to its system conditions (__that__).
See also Parsons' → B1309 contrast of the utilitarian principle of economic calculation and egoism. Economic action is not simply egoism in the sense of a personal motivational structure, but compatible with very different kinds of personal motivation – Parsons 1964c, p. 138f., 145f. → B1309 – this is indeed the basic thesis of the essay.
It is important that the differences of personal motivation are not thereby eliminated; they remain and also become effective, but their significance for the economy as a system is neutralized.
