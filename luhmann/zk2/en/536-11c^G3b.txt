In addition to the __“distortions”__ of the perceptual process caused by social systems, one must also consider the distortion caused by __psychological systems__.
Typically, this leads to the projection of excessive consonance or reciprocity: one perceives others as they “should” be in order to confirm, through their experiences, the image the individual has of themselves. → 5311/2k10 .
It should be noted that these distortions presuppose reflexively controlled experience—and thus are among the specific risks associated with this structure.
