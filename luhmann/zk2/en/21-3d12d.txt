It is not without risk to work with the majority of system references and in this way maneuver all contradictions or variations out of the system itself – especially since the assumption of a plurality of system references does not explain anything by itself. {1}
This danger is particularly evident in the case of Parsons, who masterfully handles this technique. See also the criticism by Kluckhohn – Strodtbeck (1961), p. 34. → B1346, cited in → 21/3d23b.
