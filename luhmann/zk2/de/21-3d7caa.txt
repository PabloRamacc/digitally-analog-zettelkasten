Vgl. insb die grundsätzlichen Ausführungen S. 14ff. → B993 und S. 452ff. → B993 Zum Inhaltlichen auch → 21/3d7c7 und → 21/3d7c8 . Zusammenfassende Definition S. 453 → B993: "An open system is defined by its boundaries for the selective reception of inputs (a coding process) and for its typical transmission of outputs. It is further characterized by such properties as negentropy to counteract the tendency of all systems to run down; by feedback or responsiveness to information provided by its own functioning; homeostasis, the tendency to maintain a steady state; by equifinality or the use of different patterns to produce the same effect; and by differentiation, i.e., the tendency toward elaboration of structure." (Katz / Katz 1966 → B993)
