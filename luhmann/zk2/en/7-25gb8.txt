Renewal of the concept of education, analysis after 1979.[*] → B2189
Multiple functions:
1.) A concept used to fend off political interference (as in the past). The educational system can only determine what education is from within itself.
2.) Education enables a positive self-description.
3.) The term re-establishes “canonical” elements that are a kind of “must” in education—something that cannot be omitted and must be mastered by the time students graduate from high school. A necessary component of the curriculum.
