Expectation stabilization can have various system references and be related to different systems. See in principle → 21/3a1pe12, further → 42/5d10; → 21/3a1p5b(R); → 7/10g.
Similarly, Goffman emphasizes on p. 47 → B739 (p. 71) → B814[*] that viewing a "performance" as an "expressive function extension of the character of the performer", i.e., "to see the function of the performance in these personal terms," is only a limited possibility. Often, the manner of self-presentation is also determined by its function for a further social context. Goffman → B739 considers here the subordination of self-representation to group representation (team performance). This is neither the sole case of a social function nor are the possibilities clarified system-theoretically.
Accordingly, Goffman emphasizes (p. 155f.) → B739 (pp. 242f.) → B814[*] that the disruption of a presentation simultaneously has consequences "in three levels of social reality, each of which involves a different point of reference and a different order of fact." → 21/3a1p5ba
