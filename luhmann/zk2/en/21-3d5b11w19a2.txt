Early examples in the Middle Ages: 1.) __Monastic theology__ as differentiation within the religious system of society, with the consequence that this religious system could no longer easily accommodate itself to society under the demands of this subsystem-theology, but had to elevate and specify itself religiously.
2.) __Commercial sector of the economy__: Here too with the effect that such a sub-sector promotes the differentiation of the entire functional system.
Also relevant here: Differentiation through internal differentiation.
