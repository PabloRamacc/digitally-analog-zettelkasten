→ 536/12b5 On the dialectic of finesse, see Bessel 1673, p. 245ff. → B2015 The greatest finesse is: not to resort to any finesse at all, but to __be__ a man of integrity.
Even for Thomasius in 1710, p. 123 → B2008 simulation and dissimulation are still regarded as the art of prudence.
See du Bosq 1639, pp. 141ff. → B2010: “The best artifice is to have none at all.”
Reasoning: Sincerity is easier to manage than pretense.
