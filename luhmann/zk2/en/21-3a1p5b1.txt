This is connected to the fact that social identification by no means serves __only__ those who constitute it, nurture it, proclaim it, and display it for show. This is the great error of many ideology researchers who base ideology on an interest.
Every identification implies behavioral promises that can be exploited and serve others as a guide for orientation. This can be termed the inescapably-moral aspect of all social interaction and as inevitable intersubjectivity of all symbolism. See also → 21/3a1p5c1B3 ; → 5313/2,3f.
