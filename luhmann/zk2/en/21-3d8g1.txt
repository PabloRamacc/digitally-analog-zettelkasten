Characteristic of the concept of subsystems is that subsystems can be treated as elements in the analysis of the overarching system, whereas aspects are instead systems of their own kind. This is essentially only a problem of clarifying system references, into which the ontological problem of the relationship between part and whole thus resolves itself.
