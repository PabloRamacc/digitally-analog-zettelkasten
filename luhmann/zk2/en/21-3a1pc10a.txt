Gibson, S. 340 → B632: "I would now say that stimulus generalization is simply the fact that a number of stimuli will elicit the same response."
In detail, two cases are to be distinguished within this concept (so also the literature with numerous references): → 21/3a1pc10a1
__Primary Generalization__, in which the organism identifies the stimuli without distinguishing them. Only the observing scientist distinguishes. This presupposes no term formation on the part of the organism.
__Secondary Generalization__ as a result of classificatory or categorizing term formation by the organism.
