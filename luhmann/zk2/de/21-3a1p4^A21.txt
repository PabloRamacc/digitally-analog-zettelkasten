In __one__ sense, then, symbolic action is 'substitute gratification' or even in a sense 'phantasy gratification'. But such a formula must be interpreted with great caution. We have tried to make clear that once an attitudinal system has become fully organized through internalization of patterns, the maintenance of the pattern integrity of the __system itself__ becomes the primary focus. The 'primary cathexis' ceases to have its original independent significance. Hence in a very real sense opportunity to express emotion 'symbolically' is not a 'second rate' substitute but can be the 'real thing'. Its exact significance must be judged in the context of the total action system of which it is a part. It may be that only genetically viewed should it be regarded as a 'substitute'."
