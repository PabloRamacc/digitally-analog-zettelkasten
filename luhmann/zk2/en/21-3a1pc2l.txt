Another essential aspect of the concept of trust is that it must be granted __despite freedom__ of action. A different situation exists vis-à-vis unfree action. Furthermore, even trust itself must be given __freely__. Schottländer[*], p. 21 → B572: "… interpersonal trust rests on the correlation __of two freedoms__."
This moment __personalizes__ the relationship of trust; for trust operates not in the sphere of socially preformed [behavior] but in the area of individually attributable action.
