3.) Another means of making the representation of an identity consistent and at the same time consensual is its __idealization__. → 21/3a1p5c4aA9a; → 21/3a1p5c4aA9(R) The identity and its representation are freed from defects and inadequacies and elevated towards accepted values. It is represented as unique, perfect, and universally valid. By eliminating or concealing factual contradictions, even the audience is deprived of the opportunity to contradict. The identity is polished smooth and thus freed from every point of attack.
This consideration opens up possibilities for discussing the social aspect of Platonic ideas: they are – precisely __as__ ideas – at once factually and socially contradiction-free, therefore unchangeable and therefore true being.
