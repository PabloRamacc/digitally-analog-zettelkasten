→ 536/1 __Communication / Interaction__
Communications are not simply a subset of interactions. __They are further distinguished by the fact that every interaction__ __must be accompanied__ __by__ (at least indirect “coherent”) __communications__. Without the “revelation of an intended meaning of action”—that is, without the use of comprehensible symbols—the actions of several people cannot be coordinated with one another.
Nevertheless, it would be wrong to view social contact as limited to the mutual projection of symbols. What goes beyond this is what compels us to develop a specific theory of interaction.
