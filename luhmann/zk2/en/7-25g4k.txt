The neo-humanist concept of education overturns (though not yet clearly in Humboldt!) the old antithesis of __education__ (based on leisure) and __labor__ and replaces it with the antithesis (which, however, must first assert itself against the euphoria surrounding education) of education and alienation, which is characterized by different conditions and forms of activity. __This__ takes into account the newly emerging class relations; see Fiedler 1972 → B2464.
Alienation is the exact antonym of education.
