"As individuals in our society, we know that there are a number of different levels of behavior appropriate to the degree of mutual knowledge existing between the people present. This is something rather distinct from the difference in roles. It is related more to the idea of social distance and strangeness. The more socially distant two individuals are, the more straitened their behavior is in each other's company. Restriction of behavior implies a resort to modes of speech and action that are widely accepted – a lowest common denominator of conduct. Freedom of behavior, on the other hand, is understood to allow of more possibilities – of a wider variety of actions 'in the circumstances' than would be open to us in a less known social environment.
