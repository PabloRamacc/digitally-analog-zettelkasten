One structural factor that contributes to class differentiation is the __need for parental involvement in decisions regarding their children’s educational path__, particularly the transition to secondary schools. In doing so, parents bring their own class-dependent patterns of expectation into play.
The decision itself, however, is a requirement that depends on the organizational system. Therefore, organizational innovations should aim to reduce the visibility of decisions. This is one of the primary goals of the __comprehensive school system__; see → 7/25g17f1a .
