Starting points: 1) Abolition of the ontological (?) premise that all events and stocks can be assigned uniquely to one category only. Every event and every stock must __simultaneously__ be regarded as belonging to a system and as belonging to its environment – namely, as pertaining to the environments of other systems.
2) Necessity of choosing a system reference. The application of theory presupposes a decision regarding which system the terms "system" and "environment" should apply to; cf. → 21/3d12. → 21/3d7m1a
