A fully-fledged profession requires a certain degree of independence from the “chance” of the individual practitioner’s current knowledge.
Training cannot guarantee __possession__ of all necessary knowledge. It cannot depend solely on __applying__ the right knowledge to the situation. The ability to find knowledge is becoming more important. See Parsons and Platt 1973, p. 228 → B2429 in connection with the growing importance of the theoretical structure of knowledge and the practitioner’s cognitive competence.
