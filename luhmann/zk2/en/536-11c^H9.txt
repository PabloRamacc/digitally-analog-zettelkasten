__The Problem of Relevant Uncertainties__
When discussing the reduction process, it is important to know where uncertainties arise that must be resolved within the process governed by the system.
Perhaps the most important question here is how these uncertainties relate to the systemic problems that must be resolved within the system.
Example: Court proceedings – Decision; Taxi ride – Tip → 536/11cH9,1 Exam – result; informal interactions between professor and student
