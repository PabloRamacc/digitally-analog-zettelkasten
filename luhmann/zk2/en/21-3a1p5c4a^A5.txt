Offices and buildings such as Downing Street are intended to be special pieces of ornamentation in this sense, and thus meant accordingly. The meaning of an exception turns around through this and "confirms the rule" (which is not so with G → B739).
Generally it is important that these means of expression, especially the material symbols of the "setting", are subject to control by the performer, so that no one else has the right or possibility to disturb their arrangement.
Indeed, socially recognized and observed control also brings disadvantages: The performer will then be held accountable for defects that cannot remain hidden. Symbols can convey more than they intend; cf. → 21/3a1p4A17.
On both aspects see Goffman, p. 58-60 → B739.
