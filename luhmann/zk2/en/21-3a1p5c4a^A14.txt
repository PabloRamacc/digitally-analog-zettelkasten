As a summarizing theoretical formula for this, perhaps the idea can be used that __idealization excludes revealing all functional relations of representation__, because it always contains hints at other, contradictory possibilities, whereas ideality possesses its persuasive power only as exclusive. There is presumably an intimate connection with the problem of latent functionality in general and specifically with the necessary latency of the function of feelings; cf. → 21/12 ; → 52/4j esp. → 21/12g.
In addition to concealing the inappropriate, idealization may also require a __special relationship with the audience__ – another expression of the __problem of exclusivity__, which apparently constitutes the central problem of idealization. __Idealization can require that the public be included in the special impression of uniqueness and distinctness__. → 21/3a1p5c4aA15
