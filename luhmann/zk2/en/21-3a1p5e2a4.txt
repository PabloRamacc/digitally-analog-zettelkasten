The optimum of information is not equal to the maximum of information (p. 207 → B934). Rather, it is determined by the necessities of __behavioral security__, which an image can provide through its orientation function. From there, the problem of the expansion of the factual world by science is addressed.
Another point is that images must remain interpretable, i.e., be transferred through the reproduction of the projecting attitude. If information occurs in excess, it can no longer be ordered by interpretive acts of thinking.
