The connection of the concept of respect with problems of self-presentation in social interaction is therefore so important because it conveys insight into the significance of the __diffuse__ character of respect. See also → 62,1d24e5. Respect is personal-diffuse, because it expresses itself in a generalized acceptance of another's self-presentation. By showing respect one submits oneself to an obligation in a generalized form to use the other person's self-presentation – precisely __because__ he/she deserves respect as a __personal__ matter – even in contexts other than practice of situational definition.
