The French Revolution did not yet address any specifically pedagogical issues (apart from considerations of autonomy); it was too heavily politicized for that.
Thus, the issue of educational instruction is not raised as a problem. Despite all the interest in *Instruction publique*, the question of pedagogical relevance is not viewed as a problem at all. Schools are simply regarded as instruments of human perfection.
See Rauhut 1934 → B2950 – though with the thesis that Condorcet recognized the distinction between instruction and education (p. 49 → B2950, unclear).
