The functional formulation has the advantage that it does not seem to make this coupling of temporal and substantive dimensions an unavoidable law of fate: that stronger entanglements __necessarily__ impose a correspondingly increasing work tempo, but rather that they can be compensated in these or perhaps other functionally equivalent ways. As a functionally equivalent means comes into particular consideration: better division-of-labor organization, creation of relatively autonomous subunits, generalization of leadership tasks, etc.
