In Alexander’s work, this reliance on the classics takes on a peculiar non-committal quality: everything can be deconstructed, provided it is done within the text itself.
And in this respect, then, “neomarxism” or “neofunctionalism” is a label that encompasses everything that draws on Parsons’ work.
