The distinction between system and subsystem will presumably be of essential significance for the treatment of the topic: conflict within the system. (→ 21/3d8c16)[*] It provides functional analysis with a technique by means of which it can address the problem of contradictions in the system. Contradictions are explained from their function for various subsystems and are therefore, to that extent, necessary for the system.
Bucher - Strauss → B882 form – rejecting functional analysis and without seeing this possibility – for this purpose the concept of segment in the sense of a special identification on a larger scale. See also → 21/3d8cc.
