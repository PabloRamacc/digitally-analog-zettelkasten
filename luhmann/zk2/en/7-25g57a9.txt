"Qualification System"
A debate that took place toward the end of the 19th century and during the first decades of the 20th century concerning the linking of professions to specific educational qualifications, without a precise correlation to specific subject matter.
Lit: Felix Behrend (ed.), *On the Sense and Nonsense of the Qualification System*, Leipzig, Quelle und Meier 1929 → B3283 as evidence of a public debate.
