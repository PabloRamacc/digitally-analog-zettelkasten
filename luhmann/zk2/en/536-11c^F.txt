→ 536/11c The concept of “role” is not a fundamental concept.
It must be argued against Parsons and others that social systems do not consist of roles, but rather, first and foremost, of actions.
It is not self-evident, but rather a symptom of a certain development and consolidation of the social system when it is structured by roles.
This is not found in simple, ephemeral social systems. For example, structuring based on __locations__ (one person stands here, another there)—as seen in vacation hotels, standard seating charts, deck chairs on boats, etc.
In such cases, there is a lack of objective differentiation on the scale of person / role / program / value.
