Deutsch (1958), pp. 265f. → B570, emphasizes that the concept of trust does not follow solely from the concepts of expectation and predictability (it has, in other words, not only cognitive meaning); it simultaneously involves __"motivational relevance"__. One can speak of trust only if it constitutes a __basis for action__.
See also Schottländer[*] 1957, pp. 28ff. → B572 on the moment of "commitment" in trust.
