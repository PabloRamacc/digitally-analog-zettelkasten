Only relatively complex physical systems can organize causal processes __within themselves__ in such a way that they can act as their own causal factors vis-à-vis their environment.
This rule finds its counterpart at the level of psychic (and perhaps also social) systems. → 21/3d7ka Only relatively complex psychic systems can adequately take into account their own causality in their interpersonal environment and use this conception for controlling their own behavior. Cf. Harvey / Hunt / Schroder 1961, p. 38ff. et al. → B793, Schroder / Driver / Streufert, pp. 14ff. → B1444 Good example: revolutionaries and reformers conceive themselves as executors of a foreign (higher) will.
