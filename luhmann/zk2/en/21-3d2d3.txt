An analogous, closely related problem arises from the critique of the traditional concept of role: typically – though varying by author – distinctions were not clearly made between culturally or socially imposed expectations, self-presentation of the actor, and actual behavior in a position; consequently, consensus, lack of contradiction, and continuity of the role structure as a system of factual action were frequently assumed prematurely.
