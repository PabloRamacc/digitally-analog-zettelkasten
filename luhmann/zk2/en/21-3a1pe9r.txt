Independent of tradition-determined catalogs, fundamental values can be defined as values that conclude reflection processes and thereby determine them. __Values as constraint on, and form for, reflection__.
Question: what this means in the context of other possibilities of reflection; what is thereby blocked out.
Above all important that value concepts force devaluation of counter-values, equality for example to devalue inequality.
