Herbert Krüger (1964) → B618 also emphasizes the necessity of trust as a foundation for state consciousness; see especially p. 209 → B618 and p. 987 → B618. The latter passage makes particularly clear Krüger's peculiar tendency in understanding the state: Trust refers to the possibility of uncritical execution of orders by the subject: It is meant to push as far out as possible that point at which, despite readiness for obedience, the subject must say regarding an order given to him that it needs to be checked for its correctness. …
