The demands placed on media or equivalent tools in the educational process stem from the fact that this is a case of communication involving heightened standards.
It is not merely to be accepted as a matter of course—with the personality being modified, as it were, incidentally through this acceptance—but rather, the change in personality is __singled out__ as an __intended__ goal and __the fundamental purpose__ of communication. This cannot be guaranteed under the everyday, self-evident conditions of acceptance. Rejection of self-change as a required process is, at first, rather likely.
