The connection between truth and education, which underlies the idea of __education → 7/25gb__ is constituted; it is only through differentiation that truth as a medium, on the one hand, and education, on the other, emerge. See the manuscript *Social Theory*, Draft, III, p. 41ff.[*]
It is based on the fact that in __home education__, unmediated patterns of transmission initially predominate: direct observation, imitative learning, and at most a casual demonstration of how to behave, how to handle an object, or how to respond to events. This is tailored to a world with few possibilities.
