→ 21/3d8c5 Coser, esp. pp. 20ff. → B1501, sees in the modern replacement of the concept of *conflict* with terms such as tension or strains a loss of the actual topic.
See also Herbst 1961, esp. pp. 88ff. → B988, where stress and strain are distinguished – stress as "negative input," as disruption of positive exchange relations with the environment that tax the system's forces; strain as the state of equilibrium disturbance resulting when the disruption cannot be resolved. These terms can roughly be translated by *strain* (burden) and *tension*.
A.R. Lindesmith / A.L. Strauss, Social Psychology, Rev. Ed., New York 1957, pp. 615-627 → B1588 are to provide an overview of the use of such terms as strain, imbalance, conflict etc., according to Smelser 1963, pp. 47f. → B658.
