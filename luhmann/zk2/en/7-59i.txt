Classics have their place in schools—that is, within the educational system.
This leads to difficulties when the unity of teaching and research breaks down. With regard to the classics, research would then face the problem of defining the problem it seeks to solve in relation to the classic. And often, this amounts to nothing more than speculating on what the classic might have meant.
