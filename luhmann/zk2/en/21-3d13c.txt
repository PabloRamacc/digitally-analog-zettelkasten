The concept of the horizon also denotes an element in the constitution of possibilities: it mediates between what is perceived and merely perceptible; X thus signifies a boundary between the actual and the possible. For many reasons this is nevertheless not sufficient characterization, because there exists objective reality beyond the horizon as well as mere potentiality of perception within the horizon.
