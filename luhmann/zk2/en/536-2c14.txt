The maintenance of the situational system can motivate __initiatives__ on the part of the participants; however, these initiatives usually do not openly display their latent function but rather pretend to serve other purposes within the framework of the situation. They are therefore usually difficult to distinguish from initiatives motivated by practical considerations.
Finding the right balance of initiative is a problem related to the __difficulty of synchronizing self-presentation__. (Similarly—though without the concept of self-presentation—Chapple-Sayles, p. 127ff.) → B928) Too little initiative makes it difficult to maintain the situation; too much limits others’ opportunities for self-expression.
