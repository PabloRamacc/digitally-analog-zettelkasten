__autonomy of the educational system__
at the role level → 7/25g40b ; for pedagogy → 7/14c
The autonomy of the educational system cannot be based on the knowledge that is transmitted; it is not an autonomy derived from science (as Humboldt saw it).
Others know the knowledge better—others = non-educators.
Autonomy can only be achieved by referring to the function and techniques of education (this applies generally to functionally differentiated systems). This fundamental thesis contradicts the notion of a → 7/25g52a
