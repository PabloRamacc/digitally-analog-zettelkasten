On the question of the logical derivation of inventory prerequisites
Parsons, see individually → 21/3d5c, holds that a logical derivation of individual inventory prerequisites from a comprehensive action theory is feasible and necessary for their justification. Similarly, Buckley, p. 256 → B354 – starting from the same premise – criticizes current inventory prerequisite theory because it fails to complete such a deduction and therefore actually lacks any __basis__ for assessing the relevance of individual functional performances.
In contrast, the question arises whether, as ultimately in every theoretical __approach__, a purely methodological justification in terms of explanatory performance should suffice.
