v Friedeburg 1963 → B142 attempts to break away from the focus of industrial sociology on the informal aspects of socially organized coexistence and instead to specify the social conditions of workplace cooperation as distinguishing criteria (esp. p. 10ff. → B142). The resulting very difficult distinction from business administration, which is oriented toward organizational objectives, is not elaborated upon in detail.
It is, at any rate, interesting as a starting point for a system/environment concept.
