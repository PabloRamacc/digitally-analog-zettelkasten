→ 21/3d2j2 For the functional method, systems and boundaries in social life provide clues for the formation of reference points. It treats systems as __reformulated problems__. That means: if a problem, whatever its kind, must be solved by establishing a system, this gives rise to __new__, secondary, derived problems. The original problem is split into internal and external system problems. In that lies the introduction of a boundary, numerous new possibilities for solution, but also the problem of dysfunctional consequences and thus the necessity of ever-new, derived problem formulas.
