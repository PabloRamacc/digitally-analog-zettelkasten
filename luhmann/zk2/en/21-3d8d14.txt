Part/Whole schema as an objectified, de-relativized representation of system differentiation, as an abstraction that has its function as a language for communication but is insufficient as a research language.
The part/whole language emerges as the traffic language of systems without indexical use of system references; it must indeed designate the intended systems or system parts but presupposes that doing so does not alter the respective environment. See also → 21/3d12l.
