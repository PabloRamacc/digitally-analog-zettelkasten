The methodology of problem orientation is the method for simulating a practical decision.
In this case, the decision itself is part of the problem. The problem therefore changes with the possibilities for decisions.
On a purely theoretical level this means: that theory thinks itself into its own object. The evolution theory is also to be unfolded within this methodological framework; it does not exclude design and planning in the form of an antithesis as previous tradition would have it.
