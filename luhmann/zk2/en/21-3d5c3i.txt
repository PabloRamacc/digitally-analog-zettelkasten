The pairs directed as diff / part or aff / part are problematic precisely because of their one-sidedness – specifically in relation to their negations. In order to be stable and functionally effective, identifications must always incorporate "other possibilities." That is exactly the point of identification. Diffuseness cannot be sustained without a certain degree of specific clarity and justification, and thus comparability. Affectivity can find no lasting satisfaction without a certain measure of neutralization, restraint in immediate gratification with regard to other possibilities, etc.
Only then does the meaning of negative categories become clear. They address the "other" possibilities that must be included in every stable identification according to functional identity theory.
