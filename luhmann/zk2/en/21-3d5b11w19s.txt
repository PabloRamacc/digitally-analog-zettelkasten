An important new formulation follows from the elaboration of autopoiesis theory applied to society:
The subsystems contribute to the self-reproduction of society by fulfilling their functions. They can therefore not be understood solely in terms of their function.
This means, among other things: their activity is also important outside of themselves as the readiness to provide premises for continuing social autopoiesis. Clearly observable, for example in the problem area {1}. → 21/3d5b11w19s1
