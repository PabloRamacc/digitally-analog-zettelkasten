Complexity as an Educational Factor
Schooler 1972 → B2563: Complexity measured by: “the individual (1) is called upon to make decisions based on a wide variety of factors,
(2) is exposed to numerous alternative models of behavior, and
(3) can view a wide range of goals as plausible.”
The result is: greater intellectual capacity, rejection of external constraints, subjectivism = a focus on inner qualities.
See also Hunt 1971 → B1451.
