Self-interest does not lead to stability in social interaction: “For, as M. de la Roche-Foucaut admits, the pride of the giver and the pride of the receiver, unable to agree on the value of the favor, they will always find only the ungrateful among those they have obliged, and the unjust among those who have rendered them service.”
