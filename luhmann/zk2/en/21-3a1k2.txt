This concept of the functional problem → 21/3a1k2(R) replaces—in a more formal formulation—the concept of __needs__ → 21/3a1k2,1 (__needs__) in older functional theory → 21/3a1k2a (→ 21/3a1m). → 21/3a1k2,2
The step is—judged from the factual yield of analysis—not very far-reaching. It simply restricts the starting term to its methodological aspect and thus makes it universally applicable. This does not, of course, mean that functional problems do not require empirical interpretation; but this already constitutes the next lower level of generalization. Needs are a specific type of functional reference problems. Only with this separation will it presumably become possible to index the concept of needs—relieved from its methodological function—as empirically controllable.
