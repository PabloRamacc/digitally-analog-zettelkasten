In another yet different sense Blau uses a similar concept, especially in Dynamics 1955 → B326 and, theoretically formulated, 1957 → B211 (see also → 42/2,4). Basically the approach corresponds to that of functional analysis. It is based on the notion of dysfunctions. However, Blau assumes that dysfunctional consequences of certain organizational solutions are experienced as problems in fact and secondary solutions are causally motivated by them. In this way – which is widespread – a quite justified functional question is transformed into a causal-dynamic model of organizational development, while it remains unclear to what extent this development is determined or thought open-ended, i.e., how many "multiple solution variants" for subsequent problems are taken into consideration.
