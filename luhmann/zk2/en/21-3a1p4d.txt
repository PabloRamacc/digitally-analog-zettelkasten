Identifications represent a complex of experiential possibilities that cannot be actualized all at once. This representation leads to identifications becoming __shortcuts for experience, simplifying schemata__ that orient and motivate as such – without current clarification of their "meaning" (Husserl's problem). → 21/3a1p4d(R)
With such identifications, partly perceptual, partly symbolic in nature, simplified images of social systems are also constructed. For example, every "group" is a __reification__ of a highly complex network of possibilities. Lived-in identifications of this kind often then hinder the full clarification of their meaning, especially regarding their "other possibilities," because a certain, limited usage has become habitual.
From this perspective, Simon → B88 treats such reifications as limits to rationalization. See → 21/3a1p4d1.
