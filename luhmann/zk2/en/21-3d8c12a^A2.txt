Another thesis that belongs to this context states that __external conflict limits a system's capacity for tolerating internal conflicts__. See Coser, p. 95ff. → B1501
If this is true, it simultaneously implies that other forms of environmental adaptation, such as through specific functional performances, enable stronger internal conflicts.
However, it is highly doubtful whether this thesis really holds true. Presumably, tolerance for internal conflicts depends less on the adaptation __form__ than on the degree of adaptation __difficulty__, that is, the intensity of tension a system experiences through its environmental conditions.
It is not necessarily stated that conflict constitutes a particularly difficult form of environmental adaptation.
