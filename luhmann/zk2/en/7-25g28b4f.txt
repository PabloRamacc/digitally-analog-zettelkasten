If there were no need for individual performance assessments—and especially for grading—would the teacher even form an impression of each individual student, or take care of each individual student at all?
Can one rely on a professional ethos without an organizational framework?
Ziegenspeck 1973, p. 74 → B2753 notes at least one such function of grading: “Due to the pressure to grade, the teacher is compelled to observe and compare his or her students closely and intensively.”
