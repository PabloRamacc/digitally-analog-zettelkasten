All three aspects—the fundamental reference problems of every social order—are a.) each necessary—and thus primary. None may be entirely absent, even if they can replace one another within certain limits. R → 21/3a1p1a
b.) independently – even if not without feedback on the other variables. One can, for example, improve the chance of fulfillment without improving consensus (through sanctions). Improvement in consensus does not necessarily imply better fulfillment (in the absence of factual means or inconsistency), etc.
Therefore each aspect is its own reference aspect for its own equivalence series – which does not mean that the fulfillment performances of one are also irrelevant to the others.
