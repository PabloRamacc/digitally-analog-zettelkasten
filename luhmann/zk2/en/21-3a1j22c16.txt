Self-observation in social systems → 21/3a1j22c16,1; → 21/3a1j22c16,2 is possible because observing and acting are already minimally differentiated in social situations – solely due to the fact that not everyone can act simultaneously for communication technical reasons.
Then what is observed becomes a basis of communication, becomes observable itself; active action turns into representation and concealment, and with all this runs a process of self-observation typical for social systems which cannot be reduced to the psychic.
