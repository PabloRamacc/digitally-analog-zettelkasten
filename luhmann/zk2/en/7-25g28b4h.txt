The assumption that different grading models have different motivational value (e.g., Weis 1971 → B2790), has not yet been empirically confirmed. Apparently, personality traits that vary widely within a school class are too discriminating.
See the failure of a corresponding study by Lewis R. Goldberg, “Grades as Motivants,” *Psychology in the Schools* 2 (1965), pp. 17–24 → B2791 • and the interpretation in Weiner 1975, pp. 37ff. → B2792
