__From lecture → B702__[*]: "Integration concerns the bearing of the relations to each other of actor-situation, conceived as variables, on the stability of the system, i.e. the stability of the interrelation in the system. Even if each unit is stable, these interrelations can vary. Therefore exists the problem of the bearing of this variability on the stability of the system."
