The specific function of the symbol consists in coordinating subjective and objective references, i.e., in integrating "intended meaning" with factual contexts. → 21/3a1p4A1a
Symbols are always connected to actions and used within them; linguistic symbols are an outstanding but not the only example. Their meaning consists in documenting the actor's intention, attitude, or opinion in the world, thereby simultaneously categorizing it into factual contexts and making it intelligible—especially for others, but also for the actor themselves. Symbols thus serve as focal points for integrating a personality into the world and as transfer stations between intended and objective meanings.
