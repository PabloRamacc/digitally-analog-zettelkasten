The customary __decomposition of morality__ found in tradition into: - duties towards oneself; - duties towards one's neighbor; - duties towards God.
Example: Buffier (1726), p. 25 ff., who presents this as the common good of philosophy → B1487. This corresponds in the catalogue of virtues to sobriety, justice, and piety.
Further evidence: Pufendorf, de officio hominis et civis I, IV-VI → B3407; cf. systematically p. 78 → B3407 and appeal to Cicero. Joannet 1775, Vol. II, pp. 151ff. → B3408
