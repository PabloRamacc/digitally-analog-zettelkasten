__Publicity__ → 21/3a1p5c6(R)
Publicity is to be defined as a special characteristic of the audience circle of a presentation, consisting in that the presenter exercises no control over access to the role of spectator.
The term can also be defined as a gradable concept if one focuses on the extent to which he exercises this control.
