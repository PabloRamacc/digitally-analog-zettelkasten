→ 21/3a1f1 __Survival__ (survival) as a point of reference
The survival theory is an outdated version of the stock problem; cf. → 21/3a1m. Cf. also → 21/3,11.
Proponents: Nadel 1951, p. 368ff. → B259
The theory is characterized by its use of causal causes of survival without further elaborating on the functional reference problematic. The three closely interrelated thoughts are missing: 1.) the plurality of functional points of reference in a system;
2.) dysfunctional consequences and
3.) the hierarchy of problem levels.
Therefore, a survival theorist is faced with the necessity to admit that not all cultural or social processes, institutions, symbols have stock-critical functions and therefore the functional analysis → 21/3a1f1b
