III. Objectively:
Above all: through one’s __own structure of expectations__.
In this context, the emergence of an __insider’s view__ of the system—one that is not readily accessible to outsiders (anyone)—could be used as a symptom.
It arises because a specific structure of expectations highlights events in a particular way, perhaps making them visible for the first time and endowing them with special informational value.
And corresponding to this: Expectations acquire the structuring value of unquestioned, taken-for-granted assumptions that would not have such value in general or for everyone.
