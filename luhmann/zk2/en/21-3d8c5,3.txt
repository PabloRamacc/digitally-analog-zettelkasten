Chapple - Sayles form a particularly narrow concept of tension (stress), esp. p. 133ff. → B928

This refers to the problematic situation control or self-presentation synchronization that shifts onto personal characteristics and thus (prematurely) becomes fixed.
Ultimately Chapple - Sayles → B928 tend to attribute all tensions to problems of synchronization.
