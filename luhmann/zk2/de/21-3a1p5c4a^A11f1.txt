Sofer 1962, S. 111f. → B870 mit vollem Respekt vor private and institutional privacy. Die Erforschung von Organisation darf nie zu einem spying out werden. Die Selbstbestimmung in der Freigabe von Informationen müsse gewahrt werden.
Kahn u.a. 1964, S. 78 → B624: "Each member is vulnerable. He must use sound judgment, make wise decisions, and act with skill and dispatch – or at least appear to. Therefore, each member is motivated to have at hand all the information relevant to his decisions and actions, including their evaluation by others. But he is less vulnerable if others do __not__ also have that information. His decisions cannot be challenged if no one else has an adequate basis for judging them. […]"
