A consistent criterion for __comparing__ the contingency formulas __within__ the educational system:
__What is the point of reference from which mere knowledge__ (as superficial results of the learning process) __is devalued__:
in the case of perfection: reason; in the case of education: the universal (see Schelling → B2471: Only the particular can be learned); in the case of learning ability: learning ability itself, which requires that what has been learned can be unlearned or relearned.
