The distinction between function/performance and reflection can be used to articulate the general relationship of __generalization and respecification__; cf. → 21/3d5b11d8d. [*]
Function itself is formulated abstractly. It is neither logically-deductively nor hermeneutically respecifiable. One cannot interpret inherent limitations into it and then extract them again through interpretation. That means: The problem of respecification can only be sufficiently (non-hermeneutic) solved → 21/3d5b11z/t15a
