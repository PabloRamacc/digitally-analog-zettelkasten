Selection, for its part, is problematic due to the __technological deficit__—and the rejection of selection is a parallel escape route to technological aversion.
Because one cannot reliably control the effects through the causes, one must make selections for further education __after__ success or failure has occurred—a form of feedback that leaves open whether it will develop into negative or positive feedback, but within an organizational context, it very quickly leads to the reinforcement of deviations.
