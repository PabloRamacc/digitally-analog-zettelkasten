__Subject-Specific Pedagogy__
Subject-specific didactics must be developed based on __topics__ (subject matter), in such a way that they address the topics not in terms of true/false (science), but in terms of the learning process oriented toward them. The question is not __how__ best to learn the topics, but rather which topic structures facilitate the best learning. In this context, the variety and manageable complexity of a topic are likely to be the structural factors that determine its didactic qualities.
