→ 7/25g56 On the technological orientation of the 18th century.
It is telling that, prior to Kant, there was a striking affinity with philosophy (in a very pragmatic sense). On this, Dr. Johnson, 1778 → B3250 (Boswell’s Life of Johnson (ed. Birbeck Hill, New York 1904, vol. III, p. 323 → B3250; cited in Glady Bryson 1945/68, p. 15 → B3251):
“I could write a better book of cookery than has ever yet been written; it should be a book based on philosophical principles.”
In Bryson → B3251 also a reference to Hegel, *Encycl.*, Introduction, §7, note (p. 39 in my edition → B3252).
