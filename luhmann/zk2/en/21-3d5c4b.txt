Beyond the aforementioned pairing regarding the attitude-object distinction, Parsons (WP, p. 179ff.) → B954 develops a second one that arises from the theory of phase movement. Each pair, e.g., universal/spec has a relationship to an earlier and a relationship to a later stage. Universalism is related to neutrality in the latency phase; Specificity with performance in goal attainment. This yields: 1.) Specificity – Performance;
2.) Affectivity – Particularism;
3.) Diffuseness – Quality;
4.) Neutrality – Universalism. → 21/3d5c4b(R)
These combinations resolve the isolation of individual pairs such as perf/aff, etc., place each of their members in a different context, and thereby denote a process of generalization or learning, i.e., the connection to → 21/3d5c4c
