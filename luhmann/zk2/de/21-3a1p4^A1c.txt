Zur Sinnähnlichkeit des Symbolbegriffs siehe z.B. Bertalanffy 1967, S. 25 → B770 drei Criteria: "Symbols are __representative__, that is, the Symbol stands in one way or the other for the thing symbolized. Furthermore, symbols are __transmitted by tradition__, i.e. by, learning processes of the individual, in contrast to innate instincts. The third criterion I find necessary I call __freely created__."
