This is a fundamental point: the actor is not conceived as __one__ system, which acts in relation to a situation (or environment) which is then treated as another system; actor and situation __together__ constitute the system of reference. This is as much the case for a psychological system, as a system, as for the other types. A 'personality' conceived as devoid or independent of 'object-relations' could not be called a system of action in these terms.
