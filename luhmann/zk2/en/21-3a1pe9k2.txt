The preceding considerations lead to a novel concept of __justification through values__. Justification cannot consist in the acquisition of a truth-substitute or in demonstrating that a specific action is right and not otherwise. It consists only in expanding the horizon of reference, thereby expanding meaning. The value formula establishes a very general connection between actions serving this value; justification occurs through a kind of generic logic.
