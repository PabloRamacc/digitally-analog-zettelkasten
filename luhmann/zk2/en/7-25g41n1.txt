General Prerequisite: Forms of Interpenetration and Socialization in Families.
Basic premise: __In families, all differences are individual__—at least that is how they are perceived and practiced—i.e., they are used as “differences that make a difference.” This has implications for the outcomes of information gathering and information processing. Ultimately, one always learns only about the individuals (this does not, of course, dispute the fact that a __sociologist__ can also conduct family research using other forms of difference).
