The boundary between the education system and the economy corresponds, in relation to our problem, to a __limit on total constancy__.
The __economy__ can rely on __total constancy__ in its selection practices, which is ultimately guaranteed by the monetary mechanism—e.g., government-funded positions, a willingness to pay for personnel rather than for something else. The __education__ __system__ __lacks such a principle of total constancy__.
