Among the philanthropists, there is already a clear departure from the formula of pure perfection—perhaps compelled by sociopolitical considerations.
A dual interpretation:
1.) Perfection must be conveyed in conjunction with practicality; it is attainable only in bourgeois life.
2.) Perfection is an end only as bliss—that is, only when it becomes self-enjoyment, a pleasant sensation. Thus, it is merely a means __to that end__.
See Grube 1934, pp. 72ff., regarding these two limitations. → B3190
