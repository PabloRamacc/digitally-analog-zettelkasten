It can indeed be the case, and it is even typical to expect that functional-structural differentiation will expand more than communication potential; then such a system must use personal integration in addition to complementary integration, now but in latent form if not illegal, because no longer covered by the systemic constitution. A good example are post merges between interest associations and political organizations (parties). This phenomenon is typical – a case of functionally sensible illegality – because an increase in communication potential poses different requirements than an increase in functional-structural differentiation.
