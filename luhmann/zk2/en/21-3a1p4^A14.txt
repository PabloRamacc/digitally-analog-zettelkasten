For bridging contingency and the achievement of understanding-compatible congruence in complementary and reciprocal behavior, it is now important that __the use of symbols simultaneously has instrumental and expressive meaning, i.e., fulfills a different function in various experiential contexts__. → 21/3a1p4A14(R) When an actor aims to achieve something guided by their own expectations through orientation toward and use of general symbols, they necessarily express their own attitude thereby. And this applies not only for cognitive but especially also for emotional attitudes. This makes it possible for others, by adopting the same symbols in their expressive referential sense as situational data, to align themselves accordingly and act complementarily-complementingly, and even form a congruent—though never fully matching—experience cognitively and/or emotionally. → 21/3a1p4A15
