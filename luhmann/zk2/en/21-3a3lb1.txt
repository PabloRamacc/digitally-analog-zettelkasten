→ 21/3a3lb The idea of a generalized mechanism gains precision through the concept of **double interchange**. → 21/3a3lb1(R); → 21/3a3lb1a See also Parsons – Smelser, p. 70 ff. → B370Parsons on Merton, p. 16 ff. → B955
The starting point is that all action systems are "**open**" systems that can sustain themselves only through an exchange of performance with their environment. {1}
With increasing differentiation of the system and its environment, a performance exchange that is immediately satisfying on both sides (division of labor) is no longer sufficient, since the performances and needs of the system cannot be accommodated in relation to one specific other system. The system thus requires two markets so to speak: one for the satisfaction of its needs and another for accommodating and evaluating its performances. And these two markets must be connected by an "intermediary mechanism → 21/3a3lb2
