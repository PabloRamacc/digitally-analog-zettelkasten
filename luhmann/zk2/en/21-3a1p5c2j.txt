The exclusion of the truth/reality question in the sense of a decidable being question furthermore means an abolition of the schema sincere __or__ insincere → 21/3a1p5c2j(R) ; → 21/3a1p5c2ja (truthful or untruthful) as a schema for evaluating attitudes of the self-presenting one.
Already Roethlisberger - Dickson 1939, p. 276ff. → B820 (in connection with → 21/3a1p5c1B9): "The tendency completely to believe or disbelieve the speaker also arises in part from oversimplifying the relation between what the speaker says and what he thinks. It is commonly supposed, although there is very little evidence to warrant such a supposition, that there exists a simple and logical relation between what a person says and what he thinks. On this assumption, any deviation from a logically explicit statement is taken as implying a willful and conscious intent on the part of the speaker to distort and disguise what he really is thinking." (p. 276 → B820) → 21/3a1p5c2j1
