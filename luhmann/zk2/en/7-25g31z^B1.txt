__Periodization__
Distinction between “period” and “episode” based on organization and interaction.
__Periods__ are defined organizationally. They cannot be adjusted during interaction. For example, the duration and timing of a school class.
__Episodes,__ on the other hand, are interactional units of time—whether thematically defined (larger) or arranged through turn-taking (smaller); see → 536/11h(1) .
Concept of time: __Distinction__ between organization and interaction.
