To Parsons' Systems Theory → 21/3d5,1; → 21/3d5,2
Parsons applies the __concept of function__ – in this respect quite consistent with prevailing theory – to the contribution of an "item" to a system. → 21/3d5,4 The distinctive feature of his theory – and its problem – lies in the fact that he believes it is possible validly to derive the functional requirements for every action system from a general theory of action. This derivation results in the system schema not being derived inductively, taking no account of empirical applications or paying attention to real possibilities of fulfilling functional performance, but instead being presented ready-made for further empirical and functional analysis, at least in terms of an ordering of minimal requirements.
