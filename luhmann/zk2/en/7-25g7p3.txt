In connection with an increase in publications on specific pedagogical topics beginning in the 1720s, schools, school histories, and educators also became worthy of literary attention.
Heubaum 1905, p. 269 → B2519 refers to Gottfried Ludovici, *Historia rectorum gymnasiorumque scholarumque celebriorum sive Schulhistoria*, 5 vols., 1708–1718, and similar works, until the first school journal appeared in 1741: *Acta scholastica*, a kind of professional journal.
