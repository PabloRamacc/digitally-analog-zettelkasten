Interaction theory, which sees itself as strictly scientific, begins with a __descriptive analysis of distributions__.
Who interacts with whom, when, in what form, and on what topics?
And then: How do the individual variables correlate; where are the “clusters”; what stands out?
Hardly any guidance from an anticipatory theory.
