What characterizes values is that their relevance extends to effects of action—and not to causes. From this alone necessarily follows the incompleteness of pure value analysis as a theory of action—furthermore, a distinction between values and norms.
Values refer to purpose programs; norms to conditional programs. In both cases the more abstract terms (values or norms) designate, so to speak, unfinished programs.
