Trust is, according to the above, only one of many substitute problems for complexity. Further inquiry would be where follow-up problems of this type lie in problem-solving. {1}
Here it must be distinguished according to the general inside/outside differentiation: 1.) Problems of environmental structure: How do environments pose the trust problem for systems and how do they enable them to solve it?
2.) What internal problems arise? Which structures enable systems to grant trust or cope with the "efforts of trust," possibly compensating for them → 21/3a1pc2p6A?
