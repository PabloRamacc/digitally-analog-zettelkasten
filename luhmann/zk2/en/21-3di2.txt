Ancient uses are initially found more casually, not in terminologically prominent places. Perhaps first in the Corpus Hippocraticum → B1098. Systema as a composite entity, as a structure; further Plato, Nomoi, 686 b → B1099. Aristotle, NE, 1168 b → B1100 – also interesting otherwise with regard to part-whole dominance.
In Stoic philosophy, system is explained as cosmos (Kosmos) as a system. Furthermore, from there it seems that a connection between systema and techne follows in the sense of composition as a work, which may then have influenced Roman legal thought.
