For the understanding of problems and for the relationship between problem and solution it is important to ask whether one can assume that a solution to the problem exists or not; in other words, whether this concerns only a process of searching and finding, or whether one operates here with uncertainty. Gombrich 1966 → B554 / 78, p. 77 → B555 brings up the comparison between crossword puzzles and art. The artist does not know if there is a solution.
