An important, and possibly decisive, reason for this pessimistic outlook is __uncritical thinking in terms of ends-and-means categories__—that is, the use of the ends model as the basis for analysis. Since ends are no longer capable of representing truth, they have become a breeding ground for ideological assumptions, which are then, of course, bound to be disappointed. This applies particularly to all observers with progressive, liberal, democratic, humanistic, or social reformist leanings. See Etzioni, ASQ, 1960, p. 273ff. → B26; furthermore → 532/4b6a . This highlights the need for a fundamentally critical stance toward task theory in organizational science.
