In a society of abundance, scarcity becomes the scarcity of time. The scarcity of time is __the most general formula for scarcity__. It also includes the most elementary form of scarcity in subsistence means (under the presupposition of an abundance of nature).
Or: time scarcity as the transformation of environmental scarcity into social scarcity, societal scarcity.
