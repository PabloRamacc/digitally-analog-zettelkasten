See also Moore 1963 in particular pp. 16 ff. → B1153 in the general context of questions regarding time scarcity. For this see → 7/9a(2) with further considerations.
Moore → B1153 attributes time scarcity to role specification—that is, social differentiation—at least regarding experienced time scarcity in the clock sense. Conversely, naturally, introducing division of labor on a larger scale presupposes time measurement.
Further evidence: Lübbe 1966, p. 196ff. → B1154
