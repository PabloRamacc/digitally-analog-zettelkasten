The functional concept discussed here __shifts__ the question of success/failure __to the future__ to such an extent that it poses serious problems for the learning capacity of the teacher or the school system.
Concepts that focus __solely__ on the individual or __solely__ on socialization functions have an easier time in this regard. They can already determine success or failure in the subject during the school years.
