Attribution of Actions to __Motives__ → 536/11cG5b (Experiences do not require motives!)
Lit: Mills 1940 → B1759 (photocopy); Burke → B665; → 536/11cG5a
Motives in this context are socially constituted complexes of meaning that, without necessarily having a clear correlation with specific aspects of the psychological system, underpin and account for actions in a socially understandable way (which does not always mean justifying them).
The actor himself also understands himself through his motives.
