The concept of predisposition breaks the circle of self-reference and thus forms the starting point for causal technologies.
See Brandes’s critique from 1792, esp. pp. 36ff. → B2027: Philosophy had erroneously assumed that all diversity was merely a matter of education, which need only presuppose general perfectibility. But in order to begin self-education, one must presuppose an innate __capacity__ on the part of the student, and this capacity is present to very varying degrees.
