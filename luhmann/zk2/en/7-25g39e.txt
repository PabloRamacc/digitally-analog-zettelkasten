Exams provide only limited guidance for teachers in assessing their __own__ effectiveness.
According to a survey by Lortie (1969, p. 37, Tables 1–3 → B3050) only 13.9% of teachers assess their own effectiveness based on “objective examinations and various other tests,” whereas 60.4% do so in a rather vague manner based on “general observations of students ….” (n = 3,056).
