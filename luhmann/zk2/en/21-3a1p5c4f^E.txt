Goffman, S. 60ff. → B739 distinguishes between the direction of performance at prominence as a star before the audience. → 21/3a1p5c4fE(R)
The __direction__ is not a role in the representation itself; it has no relation and especially no counterpart within the audience circle. It must remain invisible to the spectators, otherwise the "artificiality" of the show would be revealed. See also → 21/3a1p5c4aA11.
The administration can refer to all necessities of the practical production of representation. The director thereby acquires a special relationship with the other members of the team. Whether the director must appear on stage or remain completely behind the scenes depends on the requirements of the performance.
The requirements of good direction can be so demanding and rigorous that they are incompatible with the leading role in performance (__in__) but must instead be separated from it; this may then lead to the disempowerment of the → 21/3a1p5c4fE1.
