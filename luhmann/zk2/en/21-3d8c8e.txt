Coser, S. 43f. → B1501 distinguishes between __displacement of means and displacement of goals__, depending on whether the form of conflict behavior is maintained but the object changed, or whether the conflict behavior itself functionally replaced by wit, theater etc.
The adoption of terms from the realm of rational behavior is however not very fortunate, since for conflict-related behavior an explication according to a means-end schema by no means is necessary.
