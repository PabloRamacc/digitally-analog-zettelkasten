The special danger of defining the functional reference problem as a need lies in that one immediately thinks of a __motivation__, thus of a causal cause for remedial services, without further methodological control – i.e., and thereby tacitly attributes causally explanatory meaning to a purely functional analysis; cf. → 21/5a1c.
Cancian, S. 820 → B349 (cited in → 21/3a1k7a) criticizes the stability assumption in the need concept, while the functional reference terms are problematic; not convincing: It must be distinguished between the (inevitable) stability of a problem formulation and the stability of the solution to the problem.
