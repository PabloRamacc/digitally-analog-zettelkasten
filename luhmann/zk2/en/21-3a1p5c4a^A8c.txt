Following this, it must be considered under which system conditions the "representation problem" can be minimized and a functionally specific goal pursuit with rational use of all means maximized accordingly. What types of social systems lead to relieving the purpose structure from representation functions?
For this to happen it is certainly necessary that the purpose [Zweck] be freed from the motivational tasks—and in this area: from the problem of environmental adaptation—and that this problem be solved in another way. For this, see → 525/8c9 et seq.
However, this alone is insufficient; it must not merely address the problem of motivation but rather consider the entire order of environmental adaptation of a system.
