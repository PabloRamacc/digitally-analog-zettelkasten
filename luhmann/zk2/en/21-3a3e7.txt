If one drops the prerequisite of a determined system, one opens up the possibility for an explanation within causal science that the persistence of an effect is due to compensations in the causes and thus slides over into an equivalence functionalism which thematizes such compensating mechanisms only as a special case from another perspective. For it is on the assumption of a determined system that the possibility of compulsion prevails, namely that following a variation of a certain type – and only one compensatory variation follows or does not follow. Without this prerequisite one can merely search for possibilities of compensation.
