Just as the individual must not show any doubt about his presentation, so too must the team—the officer corps, the doctors involved in a "case," the court, and the ministry—show no sign of dissent or disagreement. This includes concealing processes of consensus-building and sanctioning (→ 21/3a1p5c4fC), but even more: every open contradiction, every inhibiting or opposing action must be avoided, not only for the sake of smooth presentation but especially for the sake of a corresponding impression.
Of course, it is also conceivable that in special cases doubt and dissent are displayed—but this is an exceptional case conditioned by the nature of the identification presented.
