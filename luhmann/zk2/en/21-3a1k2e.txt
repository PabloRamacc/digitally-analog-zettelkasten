Even today is still spoken of *needs* (in a highly formalized sense) when functional reference problems are meant.
Blau, 1955, p. 11ff. → B326 and passim: Blau → B326 defines needs not as prerequisites for survival – which cannot be empirically tested during the existence of the system – but as experienced "conditions" in need of improvement that arise as a consequence of functional or dysfunctional patterns, thus as secondary problems (albeit without systemic reference, but rather in terms of value; cf. → 21/4,4 - → 21/4,5). Cf. also → 21/3a1h6a.
Johnson, S. 52, 71 → B273 clearly opposes the use of the need concept as a term for causal explanation. Essentially, his argumentation leads to a limited type of explanation, namely as an explanation of __a__ kind of functional performance, not specific individual performances; however, this does not come out clearly. → 21/3a1k2e(R)
p. 52 → B273: "The concept of 'social need' is certainly not meant to account for the existence of cultural forms. → 21/3a1k2e1
