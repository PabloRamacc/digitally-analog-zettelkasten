__The Temporal Structure of Education__
See also → 7/14i5 ; → 7/25g61 .
A distinction must be made:
(1.) Education takes time. → 7/25g59A
(2.) Children and adults differ in their temporal structure. Children are more oriented toward the present.
(3.) The child must learn temporal integration; must learn to orient themselves toward the past and the future (Prudentia).
