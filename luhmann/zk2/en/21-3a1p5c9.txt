__Uncertainty__
The problem of social self-presentation is closely linked to the issue of __personal uncertainty__. Such uncertainty essentially stems from awareness of the problematic nature of one's own self-presentation.
This explains the __reinforcing feedback__, which, as is well known, occurs when one's own uncertainty becomes visible and it is realized that this visibility has occurred. At the same time, it is then noticed that symptoms of the questionable nature of one's own self-presentation have become apparent, and now additionally bears the burden of this obviousness.
