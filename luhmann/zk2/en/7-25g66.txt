__Self-legitimization of Education__
An important factor contributing to stability in education is the knowledge (and the resulting sense of security) __that__ one has been educated.
Monsieur Teste, p. 59 → B3340
The same cannot be said of socialization, since it takes place unnoticed.
Self-legitimization requires only broad constraints. The fact that one has been raised must, of course, be recognizable and acceptable.
