As perhaps the most important component belongs to the institutionalization of conflicts that the disputants recognize each other in roles consistent with their self-presentation – in other words: treat one another tactfully. They must then cast all conflict behavior into a form that conveys an image of the opponent corresponding to his own self-conception and confirmed by him in his actions.
See a related remark on the expression of dissent in social relationships by Goffman 1955, p. 216, note 5 → B815.
