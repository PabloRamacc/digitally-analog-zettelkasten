Ackerman/Parsons associate a dysfunction with respect to necessary respecifications with the terms __anomie__ and alienation (p. 38ff. → B1220) → B1220.
Anomie is given when generalized norms are not sufficiently re-specified with regard to the question of what I should do—that is, with regard to norms.
Alienation is given when generalized norms are not sufficiently re-specified with regard to the question why—that is, with regard to values.
