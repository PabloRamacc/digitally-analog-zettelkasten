Some implications for the transition to the theory of highly complex systems:
When greater complexity is available, the problems of perceiving others can be resolved in two ways: a.) Neutralizing personal characteristics in “impersonal” relationships (indifference);
b.) individual selection of partners based on projection (love).
It is characteristic of simple systems that __both of these__ avenues are closed to them.
