Resistance as a Test for Reality
Usually (especially in transcendental theories) it is overlooked that the reality of those operations which encounter resistance can also be problematic: will and cognition.
See, however, regarding this "paradox" Miermont 1995, p. 262f. → B463
This problem can be solved elegantly if one starts from the resistance of a system's own operations against the operations of that same system.
