The two-level structure of the technology problem was already recognized by the philanthropists. It arises from the short attention span of the teacher in the classroom. See also → 7/14f1d .
See, e.g., Trapp 1913, pp. 38ff. → B2516; see also the general characterization in Schrader 1928, pp. 104ff. (p. 104) → B3185: “Educational scientists are the technologists of education. Teachers and educators are mere technicians.”
This connection is expected by academia and conveyed through books and teacher training.
