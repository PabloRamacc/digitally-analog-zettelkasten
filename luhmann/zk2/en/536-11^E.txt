→ 536/11 __General Remarks__
Interaction systems are __primarily__ characterized by functions in the social dimension.
They __inevitably__ arise whenever several people come together under the condition of double-contingent selectivity.
At the same time, they achieve—especially in social reflexivity—high levels of selective coordination that cannot be matched or surpassed by other types of social systems.
Whatever else is accomplished—as a prerequisite or side effect, in terms of content and time—remains relatively simple in complexity.
