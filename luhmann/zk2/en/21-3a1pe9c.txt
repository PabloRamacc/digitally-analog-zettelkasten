Parsons relates the generalization of values to the differentiation of a system and defines Value as "__pattern of normative orientation defined on a level of generality independent of differentiation in the system__", more precisely as "conceptions of the desirable type of system which are stated independent of differentiated functions in the system and of the particularities of situations" (Lecture 1960, p. 4 → B702).[*]
This definition is to be understood relative to systems. The subsystem has its own values; that subsystem again has its own values etc.
