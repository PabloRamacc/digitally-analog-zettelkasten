Conflicts are transformed into social systems of a special kind through the form of dispute.
Especially acute conflicts tend to lead to the formation of social systems with highly complementary expectations and action.
The problem of these systems lies in __excessive interdependencies__ at too concrete a level of meaningful experience and acting.
Society is interested in preventing or channeling the formation of conflict systems without relinquishing the structural advantages on which the possibility of conflict rests.
