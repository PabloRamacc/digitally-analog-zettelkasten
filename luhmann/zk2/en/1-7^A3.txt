__On the Concept of Organization__:
Bakke in Haire, p. 37 → B72: “A social organization is a continuing system of differentiated and coordinated human activities that utilizes, transforms, and integrates a specific set of human, material, capital, ideational, and natural resources into a unique problem-solving whole engaged in satisfying particular human needs through interaction with other systems of human activities and resources in its environment.”
