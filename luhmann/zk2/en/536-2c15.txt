Like all social systems, situational systems also have a problematic relationship with __the purposes__ of action. More on this below → 532/4b6 .
Rules of conduct __can__ structure situations, but they cannot guarantee their success.
When purposes are discussed in situations—when they must be discussed—the situational interest is usually very clearly separated from the purpose. The purpose runs the risk of being talked to death. See Leavitt 1962, p. 217ff. → B832 discuss “problems of objective” in committee meetings, yet still with the aim of “clarifying objectives.” In general, however, it is then difficult to move from talking about the purpose to actually pursuing it. The situation, with its systemic interests, is such that the purpose remains the topic of discussion.
