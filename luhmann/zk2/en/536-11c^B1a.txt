Analysis of the Transition from Binary Relationships → 536/11cB1a1 systems comprising three or more people.
Two-person relationships are also social systems, but they do not regulate themselves based on their systemic nature; they are not aware of this nature as an impersonal, overarching unity—as a point of reference for establishing demands and substitution processes—and therefore it cannot serve as a bridge to broader social contexts.
