Organization requires a language specific to it that __can convey something unambiguous__ in __decision-making situations__.
This language is only gradually being developed. It rejects pedagogical intentions, appeals, and theoretical concepts.
The school regulations of the 18th century do not yet distinguish their language from that of pedagogy. Nor is there yet a distinct public administrative law. In this respect, the prerequisites for an anti-bureaucratic sentiment are also lacking.
