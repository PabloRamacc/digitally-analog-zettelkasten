Besides the inevitable contradictory communications that are necessary for creating a desired impression and mere slips or mistakes, there is another type of __communications that__, likewise but as it were __voluntarily__ do not stand in agreement with representation.
Goffman → B739 discusses pp. 107ff. → B739, without systematically clarifying and summarizing their distinctiveness under the overly broad title "Communication out of Character" with four types: "treatment of the absent, staging talk, team collusion, and temporary realignments".
The essential point seems to be that the tension of a problematic and perpetually endangered representation awakens the __need for contrasting behavior__ – so to speak as relief. In this lies also an awareness of oneself and possibly others regarding the representation as mere representation, which facilitates and relieves, refreshes.
