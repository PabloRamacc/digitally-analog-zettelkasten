__Hagen → B338__ also uses the distinction between statics and dynamics; regarding his concept of a static equilibrium, to which he also counts moving equilibria (moving equilibrium) with changing parameters, see in particular → 25/1,4; → 25/1oa.
By dynamic analysis he understands the tracing of the path along which changes to parameters within the system are captured – presupposing that the system requires a certain period for this, often one that is very long.
