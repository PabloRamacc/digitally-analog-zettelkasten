Possible interpretations of the origins of __deviance__
Based on suggestions in Lemert 1967, pp. 51ff. → B1783 (without reference to the time problem) the concept of “drift” by David Matza, *Delinquency and Drift*, New York 1964, may be of interest → B1441. One is swept into situations and can only define oneself as a deviant in retrospect.
