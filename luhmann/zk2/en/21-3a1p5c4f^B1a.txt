→ 21/3a1p5c4fB1 __Confidentiality__
A typical byproduct of such loyalty regarding external behavior is internal confidentiality. Its essential moments are: 1.) the __grounding in the security of expectation-compliant public conduct__. This basis provides a certain freedom and informality to internal communication. One knows the limits of the scope of validity for this tone, feels relatively secure that they will be observed, and thus feels safe against misuse. This presupposes the possibility of "regional" (cf. → 21/3a1p5c1C ) separation of the Internal Role and the External Role. Cf. also → 21/3a1p5c4e.
2.) The restorative informality has a certain __exchange value__ and compensates for public discipline. Whoever demands discipline in public appearance for the team, limits their demand to this aspect, → 21/3a1p5c4fB1b
