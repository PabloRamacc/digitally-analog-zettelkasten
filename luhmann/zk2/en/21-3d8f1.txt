From the specific functional definition of such coordinating generalizations follow precise indications as to how such generalizations must be constituted. "**Coordinating generalizations**" is a designation for a class of functionally equivalent performance possibilities.
This represents a fundamental advance over the simple concept of generalization, which as such does not yet contain a criterion for the direction of generalization and can therefore easily be interpreted merely as classificatory generalization, thereby bypassing the problem of contradictions and dysfunctional consequences; cf. {1} → 21/4e1 .
