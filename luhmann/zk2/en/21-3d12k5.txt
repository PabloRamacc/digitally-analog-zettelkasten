The problem of reductionism is the problem of an observer. It rests on the fact that something can be observed with the aid of a difference, distinguishing system and element, after which one or the other side can be designated as a connection point for further observations – but not both sides at once.
If one uses this distinction, then one is thus forced to a problem of this kind. But must one use it?
And above all it matters that one does not use them incorrectly, for example by saying that the human being is the element of social systems and then [psychological] reductionism. → 21/3d12k6
