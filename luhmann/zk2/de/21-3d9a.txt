Wichtiger Gesichtspunkt der Kritik:
__Systeme "bestehen" nicht aus Dingen (Personen), sondern aus Zuständen bzw. Veränderungen von Dingen (Personen)__ → 21/3d9aa ; → 21/3d9ab
Vgl. auch Kuhn 1963, S. 50 → B645: "The elements, or components, of a system are __not__ the __entities__ in the system, but qualities or states of those entities [die, was später (S. 50f. → B645) gesagt wird, variabel sein müssen]. […] the elements of systems are states or conditions of things, not the things themselves. In systems involving persons, it is not the __person__ who is an element, but his state of hunger, his desire for companionship, his state of information, or some other trait or quality relevant to the system."
