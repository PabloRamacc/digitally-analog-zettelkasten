The system/environment theory does not exclude conceptions of overarching relations – neither a concept of the world – cf. → 21/3d18c27 – nor notions of an encompassing system – for instance in social theory. However, it alters – and complicates – the manner in which one conceives access to higher-order systems regarding system/environment relations. Access can no longer be conceived as command-like or normative as state determination, but rather as control over selection relations. → 21/3d5b11w4
