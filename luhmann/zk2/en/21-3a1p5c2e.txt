Perhaps a functional analysis of social identity allows for a more precise representation of this problem. The constitution of a social identity is, in a certain sense, always the construction of a generalized system with at least some degree of differentiation: it assigns various (!) expectations.
However, the stabilization of generalized systems always requires the dissemination of a minimum mass of contradictions within external or internal stock prerequisites. See in particular → 3411/5 (→ 3411/5d); → 21/4 (→ 21/4e).
