The problem of separating system and stock presuppositions is connected to the necessity of __abstract__ reference points. In relation to concrete worldly things there are naturally no functions, because every function belongs an __open__ (supplement-requiring) equivalence point.
This further connects with the fact that the __identity of the system__ is problematic and must be maintained over a certain range of concrete shifts. If this presupposition is not made, it makes no sense to speak of stock; it is implied in the concept of stock.
