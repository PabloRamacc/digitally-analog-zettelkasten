Blalock, S. 85 → B385 see the demarcation regulated solely by the purpose of investigation: "Depending on the purposes of analysis, we arbitrarily make a distinction between a system and its environment." However, it is presumably assumed that the relative invariance of a system provides the guide for demarcation. Just as Blalock → B385, so also Hall-Fagen, p. 20 → B386.
