To the extent that relations between systems assume a systemic character, __institutions of conflict avoidance__ are established to serve the preservation of the system-to-system while taking into account the power situation. The institutionalization of open conflict action can be regarded as one possible case of such conflict avoidance; for this → 21/3d8c11.
Another is the redefinition of system needs in terms of compatibility with satisfaction possibilities, which simultaneously reduces power tension. See Thibaut-Kelley, p. 111ff. → B657
