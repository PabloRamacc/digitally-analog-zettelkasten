→ 536/2c20g [*] logical structure of a dialogue through mutual responsibility:
For mutual interaction, as it occurs in dialogue (as opposed to bilateral monologues), essentially transforms the partners into a single system that allows for mechanical analyses and predictions, so that – with regard to specific actions, each individual becomes unpredictable and logically unforeseeable.
