The separation of the team from spectators is normally ensured by distinct closed connections regarding function, information possession, and accessibility of action spaces. Goffman, p. 90 → B739 speaks in this sense of "congruence among function, information possessed, and accessible regions", meaning that the function of a teammate grants access to backstage areas and secret information, whereas the function of a spectator only grants access to the front stage and its presentation.
However, this connection is rarely absolutely secure. At points where it is breached, danger zones generally arise, at least problems for the presentation. Roles of some typicality that breach this connection are discussed by Goffman, p. 87ff. → B739, under the designation 'discrepant roles', e.g., 'informer' (traitor or spy in the team) p. 90f. → B739, 'shills' or 'claques' as members of the team → 21/3a1p5c4e6
