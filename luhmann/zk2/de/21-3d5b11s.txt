__Auswirkung der Differenzierung auf die Motivationsstruktur__
Zusammenhang mit der Theorie des Rollenkonflikts. Siehe dazu Moore - Feldman, S. 64 → B1311: "There are two conflicting hypotheses concerning differential commitment and participation. The commonly accepted one may be summarized as the principle of 'frustration and diverted activity.' According to this principle, the frustrations inherent in specialized work and possibly other roles lead to search for alternative modes of expression, representation of interest, or reform. The second is the doctrine of 'differential level of positive affect,' according to which both dissatisfactions and satisfactions are mutually reinforcing, and thus individuals' levels of participation and involvement are generalized rather than compensatory.
