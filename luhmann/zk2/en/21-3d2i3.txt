Another argument from intellectual history: arguing with stock and its preservation presupposes a relatively abstract terminology. The switch to thinking about stocks in the early modern period is itself evidence of an increase in abstraction in thought. {1}
See the __old__ argument Thomas Summa Theol. I, II, qu. 2, Art. 5 → B1178: the captain does not have as his final goal to preserve the ship entrusted to him, because the ship serves a purpose outside of itself, namely navigation. (Otherwise he would leave it in port!) Only the concreteness of the analogy convinces one of this. The conclusion is: so also man.
