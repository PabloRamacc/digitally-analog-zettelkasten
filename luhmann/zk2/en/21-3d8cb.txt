→ 21/3d8c Relationships between systems are only necessary and possible within the framework of a higher-order system. As soon as one speaks about or identifies such relationships, one implies a higher-order system. In this sense, conflict theory always also has an internal-systemic aspect. Whether it is internal or external is merely a question of systemic reference: which system one means in mind.
See also Sjoberg's proposal on p. 206 → B397 to regard the fact of contradictory functional requirements as "an essential ingredient of any theory of conflict".
