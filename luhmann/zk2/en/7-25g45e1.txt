"relationship between two items, in which the relationship between the two items is influenced by the state of a third item."
In the case of the teaching-learning process, the third factor is:
1.) __either__: __subject matter__ and, in cases of increased contingency, __science__,
2.) __or__: __organization__.
A differentiation of the educational system increases the contingency in the teacher-student relationship to such an extent that these two third-factor safeguards have been called into question since around 1800.
