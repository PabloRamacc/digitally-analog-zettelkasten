This ambiguity initially stems mainly from a premature transfer of the survival idea to the social sciences, without taking into account that biological death as an essential empirical criterion is absent here; cf. → 21/3a1f1.
It must be fundamentally questioned whether this problem constitutes a reasonable objection against the __method__ at all; or perhaps it is a problem for which no generally correct solution can even be found on the abstract level of methodological consideration, but rather one that necessarily has to be decided only upon concrete __application__ of the method. It probably concerns the problem of designing a __theory__, which as such naturally cannot be pre-decided within the method itself.
