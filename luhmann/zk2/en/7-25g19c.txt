A more sophisticated form of differentiation exacerbates the discrepancies with the environment (especially with the family) in three respects:
1.) __In terms of subject matter__—regarding __the__ __topics__ covered;
2.) __in terms of time__—with the educational system’s own rhythm: school hours, the pace of graduation, the strain on the family’s time management (schoolwork), and vacations;
3.) __socially__—grouping by age; a principle entirely foreign to the family.
