See also the thesis to be distilled from analyses of Simmel 1890, esp. p. 237ff., → B1238 that in a system/subsystem order only __one__ system can truly differentiate itself: either the system or the subsystem, because differentiation of one disturbs the other depending on whether it becomes too specific or too indeterminate.
Simmel → B1238 concedes factual compatibility if differentiation is not pursued excessively far. Beyond this, perhaps we must also distinguish qualitatively better and worse types of differentiation that are compatible with each other.
