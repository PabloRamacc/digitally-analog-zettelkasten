Differentiation between the state and the economy as a condition for the differentiation of the educational system: the __economy__ made the __requirements__ evident, and the __state__ made it __possible__.
This non-identity reappears later as the input/output difference.
Initially, it functions as a __genetic__ non-identity. This occurs within the terminology of bourgeois society (with a newly emerging sense of “citizen”), which still suggested unity. Hence, despite an economy that was already largely international, there was an economically motivated political education program. This becomes particularly clear in Iven 1929 → B2256.
