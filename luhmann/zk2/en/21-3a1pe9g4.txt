This somewhat arbitrary list of problems can be systematized under the general guiding motif that every rational precision and specification generates follow-up problems. I.) Values are not given: they __must first be identified__. (1) This identification, however, leads to a plurality of values (2), so that the generalizability of value orientation (in particular transitivity) becomes problematic.
2.) The generalization of value orientation is problematic in three dimensions: a.) temporally – the stability problem (3);
b.) substantively – problem of conflict (4);
c.) social – problem of dissent (5).
III.) An equivalent for the Aristotelian generalization thought of equality is methodical, prudent opportunism.
