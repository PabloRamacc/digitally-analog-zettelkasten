A fundamental function of __jurisdiction and its procedures__ seems to be deferring the action-relevance of conflicts and transferring the conflict entirely into the sphere of experience. This restructuring with corresponding representational compulsion leads to it appearing that, even after the self-presentation of the parties involved, matters are no longer about interfering actions but only about discovering what is right.
The legal mechanism forces all conflicts into the form of dissensus and thus acts as a conflict-mitigating force in this regard.
So Aubert 1965, p. 98f. → B1496: "When a conflict of interest is turned over to the law it must from now on be formulated as a dissensus, a conflict of value or belief. → 21/3d8c17c1
