The input-output schema presupposes system boundaries and an inside/outside distinction. To the extent that the concept of boundary gains significance in systems theory, so too does the importance of the input/output schema increase. In a systems theory dominated by this notion of boundary, maintaining a positively functional transformation process becomes a substitute for the old stock formula: A system exists if it can transform input into output such as to secure the continuation of the input stream.
