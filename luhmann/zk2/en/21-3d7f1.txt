Systems theory overcomes old causality primarily through its principle of linear explanations. See in this sense a brief remark by D. Moore 1962, p. 188 → B1439.
Similarly in cybernetic systems theory, which is primarily interested in highly complex, probabilistic systems where causal explanation also fails. See Beer 1962 → B69, explicitly e.g., p. 71 → B69; the idea permeates the entire book; similarly Marney / Smith 1964, p. 112 → B983.
