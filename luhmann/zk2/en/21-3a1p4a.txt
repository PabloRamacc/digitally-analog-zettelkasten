All identification is a transcendence of the spatio-temporal immediacy of experience, or more precisely: of the spatio-temporal proximity of the current content of experience. So much is certain and undeniable.
The application of the functional method to this "achievement" must relate it to a __functional problem__. The problem is the narrowness of finite actuality in experience, which forces __selection__.
The actuality of experience can be seen as such as a selection principle; its principle is proximity to experience. It reaches for spatio-temporally proximate possibilities of experience. Thus, however, the narrowness and contingency of current existence are not overcome; it merely makes their facticity into a principle.
