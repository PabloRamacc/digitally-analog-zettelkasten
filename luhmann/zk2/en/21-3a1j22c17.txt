Self-observation is less arbitrary but also less informative than foreign observation – more secure and tighter.
It is less arbitrary because here the Element/Structure level (e.g., consciousness, communication) in self-__used__, thus nothing can be chosen arbitrarily (a self-observation of psychic systems cannot abstract from consciousness; cf. → 7/93a2 ).
But it is also __more secure__ due to the immediacy of connecting observation with what is observed. → 21/3a1j22c17a
