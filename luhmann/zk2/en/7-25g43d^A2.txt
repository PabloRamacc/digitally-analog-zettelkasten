The formula of perfection/bliss does __not distinguish between the regulative idea and the goal__. (Criticism of this “shortcoming” can be found in Schrader, p. 33ff. → B3185)
This means: the level of the contingency formula is not distinguished from the purposes of instruction.
E.g., Trapp 1780/1913, p. 141 → B2516: Not truth, but ultimately happiness is the “purpose of instruction.”
