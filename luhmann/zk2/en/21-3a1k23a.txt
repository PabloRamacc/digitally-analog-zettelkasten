The law of the conservation of problem quantity states that there is only one way to trigger problems – namely __the passage of time__. As long as structures are relevant and confer permanence on the present, so too will associated problems be dragged into the future. __Permanence of problems__ goes hand in hand with __generalization of structures__. Only through generalization do structures make problems an object of __work__.
