From this fundamental formulation, Parsons's theory of double interchange between subsystems must be examined; see → 21/3a3lb1. Due to its entanglement with causal conceptions—especially the motivationally causal conception of exchange—it fails clearly to express the essential issue. Moreover, mere exchange can also exist between systems that are not subsystems of a total system.
Nonetheless, in Parsons—at least implicitly—the idea is involved that through mutual exchange the subsystems are enabled to continuously render their performance for the total system, fulfill their function, and address their systemic problem → 21/3d8g10.
