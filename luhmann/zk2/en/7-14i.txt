__Details: 18th century__
1.) “Education solitaire” as an experiment (the tradition dates back to Herodotus, II, 2). Cf. Mercier 1960, p. 400ff. → B2157
2.) The shift in education from repression to attraction at the beginning of the 18th century; Mercier, p. 215 → B2157. Perhaps as a result of the abandonment of natural teleology, which could only be threatened in a privative or phentical sense (against which repression was set).
However, this was already very consciously applied to the Jesuits in the second half of the 16th century.
