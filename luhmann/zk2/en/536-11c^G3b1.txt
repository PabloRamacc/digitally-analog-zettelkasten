Perception and understanding of the other must be structured in such a way that they sustain the flow of reflexive consciousness.
This requirement forms the basis of __my__ interpretation of the other, and it implies that the other’s experience of my actions and perceptions can serve as a basis for his or her own actions, and that the sense in which this is the case can be inferred from his or her behavior. Only to this extent do questions and answers make sense; only in this way is the other person included in the interaction as a subject. → 536/4c
