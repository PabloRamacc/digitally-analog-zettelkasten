Buffier, IV, p. 27ff. → B1487 Maxim:
“Do not do to others what we would not want done to ourselves.” → 536/12b2,1 ; → 536/12b2,2
This maxim fails in cases of functionally necessary asymmetries or in cases of morally meaningful self-denial (asceticism). Buffier → B1487 mentions: (1.) the judge who sentences a criminal; (2.) the educator who must reprimand, correct, and punish children.
The maxim therefore does not apply to all cases; it can only be applied with restrictions.
In general, only the more general rule applies: “to seek in all things our own advantage, combined with that of the people with whom we must live” (p. 32 → B1487)
