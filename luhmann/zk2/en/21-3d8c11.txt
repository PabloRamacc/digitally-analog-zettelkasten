__Institutionalization of Conflicts__ → 21/3d8c11,1
The institutionalization of conflicts means that the fact of a lasting conflict is perceived socially and that certain roles for its treatment are institutionalized, i.e., finding consensus. → 21/3d8c11,2
Here arises the interesting question how these two components of meaning stand to each other: what significance the development of forms of treatment has for the social consciousness of the conflict and vice versa. Where does the motor element lie?
