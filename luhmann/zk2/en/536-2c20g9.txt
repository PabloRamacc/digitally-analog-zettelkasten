The most important prerequisite for all of this: __at least a provisional neutralization of the question of truth__.
In the social system of discussion, true and false communications must be structurally permissible. – At the very least, a level of communication must be attainable in which the truth or falsity of a communication can be treated as open, “thoughts” in Frege’s sense can be expressed, and questions can be asked. There must be meaning that is indifferent to truth and falsehood. See Eley 1969 for further discussion → B1722.
