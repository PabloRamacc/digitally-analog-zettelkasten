→ 21/3a1p __Repeated experience (time dimension) as a factor in strengthening expectations__ → 21/3a1pC(R)
One of the most obvious and perhaps earliest observed perspectives. In behaviorist learning theories, for example, studied as frequency of need-satisfying responses in relation to generalized learning from expectations.
Today there is instead the insight that reinforcement of expectation is something more complex than mere frequency of response. Stogdill, p. 61 → B321 citing Hilgard → B559 and McGeoch-Irion → B560 (→ 21/3a1pe).
This also enables an understanding of the problem of particularly impressive individual prior experiences. Cf. the concept of __Paradigmatic Experiences__ in Karl Mannheim, Diagnosis of our Time, 1944 → B561 and Gouldner, Wildcat, p. 165-7 → B369. → 21/3a1pC1
