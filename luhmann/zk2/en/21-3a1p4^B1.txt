New formulation of the concept of form on the basis of the concept of double selectivity (see ms., Society 5.III, p. 3).[*]
Forms are relations that have already been selected within complex configurations and then again filtered with regard to their relevance for another system, condensed once more, and reconstructed. Thus selections of selections, reduction of complexity. {1} {2} {3}
This formal concept has the advantage that it also anticipates a (also limited) background area with respect to which problems of form resolution can be addressed.
