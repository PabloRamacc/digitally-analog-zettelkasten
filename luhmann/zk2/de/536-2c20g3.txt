Näher J. Ruesch, The Tangential Response, in: Hoch / Zubin (Hrsg.), Psychopathology of communication, New York 1958 → B1828 ✓
Zusammenfassung S. 47 → B1828: "The criteria which characterize the tangential response can be summarized as follows: The reply inadequately fits the initial statement. The reply has a frustrating effect. The reply is not geared to the intention behind the original statement as it is perceivable through word, action, and context of the situation. The reply emphasizes an aspect of the statement which is incidental."
