__The Reality Shock of the First-Grader__
Starting point: a temporal boundary issue (initiation rites) and an inevitable difference in the quality of experience between the beginning and everyday life.
One cannot treat the beginning in isolation; one must view it __in contrast__ to the everyday life that follows.
It is the difference from the beginning that then counts as everyday life; it is this difference that gives everyday life its distinctive quality.
