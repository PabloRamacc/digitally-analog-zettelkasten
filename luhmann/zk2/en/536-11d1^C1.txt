context-dependent “indexical expressions”; cf. → 21/3d18d35c .
p. 353 → B1965: “We take the critical phenomenon to consist in this: With ubiquitous prevalence and insistence, members devise formulations as remedies for the problematic features that the properties of indexical expressions present to their attempts to satisfy the aims of distinguishing between objective and indexical expressions in actual occasions, and, in actual occasions, providing objective expressions as substitutes for indexicals.”
Translated into systems theory, this means that formulations seek to objectify and thematize the system reference (without abolishing it!).
