All communication conveys information in two ways: about a factual matter and about the communicator.
This gives rise to a number of structurally important questions, in particular: 1.) What kind of information guides the further development of topics within the social system?
2.) Can these two aspects be separated? This latter question is addressed from a psychological perspective by Rokeach (1960) → B1841.
It should be incorporated into a sociological theory.
