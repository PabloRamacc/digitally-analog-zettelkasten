__Environment as an Interrupter of Interdependence__
Parallel: Time as an interrupter of interdependence → 7/9z3. → 21/3a1j16,1
The environment is already always and abundantly present.
Then the system can use the __environment__, in a __system-internal__ manner, to stabilize itself despite self-reference. It uses the environmental reference as an interrupter of interdependence, for introducing independent variables into the system-internal circularity.
In this way, the instability of pure, basal self-reference is interrupted and prevented. The system determines itself. → 21/3a1j16a
