From this basic perspective, new light is shed on the extensively debated problematics of __conflicts resulting from division of labor__. In previous approaches to organizational theory or bureaucracy theory that focused on internal problems, these conflicts appeared as pathological phenomena—as unintended and unwanted consequences of division of labor; cf. → 432/4a .
In contrast, system/environment theory can make visible that this is not a deviation from the ideal but rather an initial stage in a process of problem-solving, already reduced to a smaller formulation of the problem—thus components of functional performance—notably not yet the final stage of the absorption process, but a formulation that enables further internal strategies for conflict resolution.
