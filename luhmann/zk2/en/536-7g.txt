__Special Features of Communication__
Subject to the following conditions: meaning, limited attention, and presence (see also → 536/2c18g ).
All communication relies on a framework of prior understandings from which the participants can __supplement__ their messages.
This also involves each participant being able to assess what the other can contribute and what they need to be informed about.
Question: How specific must such a prior understanding be in order to function as such?
Abstraction is likely only achievable by simultaneously limiting the group of participants and controlling recruitment.
