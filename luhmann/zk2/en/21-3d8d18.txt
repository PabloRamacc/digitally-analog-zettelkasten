The last cycle of the Whole/Part schema around 1800 is connected to the newly elaborated concept of the Individual.
The individual as a parasite of the difference between whole and part holds this fixed as that schema from whose logically insoluble problems it feeds itself.
Not coincidentally does the new concept of individuals begin in aesthetics and in the theory of the living organism, which then still equip Kant's Critique of Judgment → B714. See Baeumler, p. 244ff. → B1656: "The individual whole".
