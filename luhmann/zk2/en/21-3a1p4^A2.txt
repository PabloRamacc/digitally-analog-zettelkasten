The acting agent means and opens up, at the same time (__zugleich__), factual orientations, access to other possibilities in the world. See more closely → 21/3a1p4A14.
Additionally it must be noted that the complication arising from the inclusion of other fields of experience has to be taken into account. The term cognitiv is therefore ambiguous. It must be distinguished between 1.) __cognitive__ and __emotional__ meaning of an ego's experiencing;
2.) symbolic __expression__ of the cognitive and/or emotional attitude of the ego;
3.) __understanding__ of this expression by alter in its cognitive and/or emotional referential sense;
4.) cognitive and/or emotional __attitude of alter__ towards the cognitive and/or emotional attitude of the ego.
