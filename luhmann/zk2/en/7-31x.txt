Sociology as second-order cybernetics—__as the observation of an observing system__.
Explicitly Giddens 1984, p. 284 → B2961 in accordance with his emphasis on the “knowledgeability of actors.” Quote: “The concepts that sociological observers invent are ‘second-order’ concepts insofar as they presuppose certain conceptual abilities on the part of the actors to whose conduct they refer. But it is in the nature of social science that these can become ‘first-order’ concepts by being appropriated within social life itself.”
