The focus of the comparison and the reference range for grades should be limited to the respective school class that receives the same instruction. Any evaluation that goes beyond this is questionable. See Flitner 1966 → B2751X.
This would make it sensible to include the class average in parentheses next to each grade, also to effectively dispel the notion of a normal distribution with a __fixed__ peak.
See also • Ingenkamp, “Are Grades from Different Classes Comparable?”, in: Erziehung 2/3 (1969), pp. 13 11–14 → B2789.
