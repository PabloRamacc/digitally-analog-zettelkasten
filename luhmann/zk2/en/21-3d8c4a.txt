When using the concept of conflict as a functional reference problem, it is __not__ to be assumed that this refers to an __experienced conflict__. That would constitute an unnecessary restriction on – which is also legitimate and meaningful – a narrower line of inquiry.
Accordingly, not only consciously practiced – functionally transparent – techniques for resolving conflicts come into consideration as solutions to the problem.
The point of this further line of inquiry lies primarily in expanding the realm of consciousness. There are achievements that have latent functions for solving latent conflicts.
