It is reasonable to assume that new scientific disciplines are established only when they remain within the scope of a single researcher. They emerge through a process of separation when parent disciplines become too complex, too specialized, or too confusing.
It is, however, almost never achieved through the fusion of perspectives from multiple disciplines that are, in any case, too vast for any single mind to grasp!
This limitation can, at best, be overcome by a strong theory.
