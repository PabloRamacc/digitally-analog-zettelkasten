__Conflict as an Autopoietic System__
Conflicts arise somehow within pre-given structurally determined systems. On the one hand, they are a continuation of the autopoiesis of these systems; on the other hand, they form their own autopoietic systems. When such processes are underway, a triggering event is interpreted as an initiation. Punctuation.
__Because__ the conflict system is active, something that fits must be held responsible for being the beginning – and typically the question 'who started it' becomes incorporated into the dispute.
