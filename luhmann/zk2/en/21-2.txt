On Determining the Concept of Function → 21/2(R); → 21/2,1
Nagel, esp. pp. 251ff. → B252 limits the concept of function to **self-maintaining** systems. To this end and against it → 21/3a1a1 .
It concerns – at a further level of abstraction – the same error that Malinowski also made: namely, characterizing the concept of function by specifying a particular type of effects. Only that Malinowski remained much more concrete and presupposed as a reference point a system of basic needs of the human organism; cf. → 21/3a1k2a .
