Coordination via input and output costs a lot of time; it therefore becomes unfeasible when the processes themselves take too much time, so that the environment does not remain constant in the meantime.
This concerns on the one hand the educational process, on the other hand __structural change__ processes in systems which are already coordinated at the process level via input/output but still have to be aggregated at the structural level. Example: legal system and legal dogmatics.
