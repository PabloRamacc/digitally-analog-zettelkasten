Terminologically, a distinction can be made between centralized and non-centralized forms (systems) of conflict dampening.
The insight that there are indeed non-centralized forms of conflict control – which are mostly experienced and handled without functional consciousness – is mainly due to ethnological research. Cf. LeVine 1961, p. 10ff. → B1587, who in this regard particularly refers to M. Fortes / E.E. Evans-Pritchard, African Political Systems → B1616.
Of course, international law must also be taken into account here. For example Karl W. Deutsch → 21/3d8c11a2 → B1617
