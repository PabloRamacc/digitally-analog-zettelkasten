The issue must be examined in all __three analytical dimensions__.
An increase in group size means, for example, a decrease in the __time__ available for individual activity within the system (if the shared focus of attention is to be maintained).
An increase in size means a __social__ decrease in the participant’s chance of becoming the focus of the group discussion with his or her personal problems. → 536/11cK1a
