__Reference__ is always an empirical operation; that is to say a referable (observable, signifiable) operation.
__Accordingly__, in every reference it must be clearly distinguished __which__ operation refers __to what__. Fuller 1988, p. 42 → B499 calls this a functional rather than ontological distinction: "there are things __with__ which and __to__ which one refers."
Important for demarcation against transcendentalism.
