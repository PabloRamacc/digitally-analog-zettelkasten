A good measure of the degree of functional differentiation is the emergence __of different system histories in subsystems__, e.g., different traditions in religion and politics.
Furthermore, it is not only necessary to consider the emergence of different individual traditions within subsystems, but also that differing typologies and selection methods in individual systems lead at all to __different relevance of system history__. Not all systems have the possibility to free themselves from their own history through revolution or reprogramming.
