The emphasis on person-to-person relationships may be related to the fact that the school system is differentiated from the __family__ and must build upon it.
Within the family—especially in the course of its own differentiation beginning in the mid-to-late 18th century—the human element must be emphasized as the central focus (whatever that may also mean: as a perspective on its failure), because there are no public roles for the family; cf. → 7/5d6 .
