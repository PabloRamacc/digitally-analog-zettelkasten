Contingency formulas are __expressions of unity__, function-specific reconstructions of the unity of a manifold.
They therefore tempt us to overreach in our demands for uniformity, e.g., in the form of postulates of equality or progress.
The “value” expressed in them is experienced as __capable of improvement__ and in need of improvement; the differences are classified along the dimension of more or less. On this point, the educational formula “human perfection and education” is instructive: Rang 1968 → B3193 (photocopy).
Differentiation may only take place sequentially, in accordance with the “escalation of the concept”—as per → 7/25g43g1
