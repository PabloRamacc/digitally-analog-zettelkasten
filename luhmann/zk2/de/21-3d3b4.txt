Autonomie ist Systemleistung und nicht spezifischen Ursachen zurechenbar.
Vgl. Deutsch 1963, S. 207 → B962: "Autonomy in an organization is thus a function of the whole system. It is not located at any one point in the system, but there may be one or more points of particular importance for it. These crucial points for the autonomy of an organization are the points at which a flow of data recalled from the past enters the flow of data used for the making of current decisions."
Vgl. auch Karl Deutsch, Autonomy and Boundaries According to Communications Theory, in: R.R. Grinker (Hrsg.), Toward a Unified Theory of Human Behavior, 1964[*], S. 278-297 → B1186,S. 354-357 → B1187[*]
