__Aversion to technology__ as a way of coping with the technology deficit.
This consequence of the concept is clearly evident in Moog 1925, p. 8ff. → B3264
Unlike with technologies, one must not __presuppose__ purposes in pedagogy, because __doing so__ would mean treating the child __merely__ as a means to an end.
The structure of the argument presupposes that the child is considered a means at all in the context of pedagogy, and this is inevitable given the child’s self-referential structure.
