Only when one abandons the definition of society as stratification and replaces it with a more abstract theory of system differentiation can one gain meaningful access to a history of ideas. For only then can ideas be addressed as a structure of the social system in their relative autonomy (and not merely in terms of their function within social strata)—which, __moreover__, presupposes a critique of Parsons’ distinction between the cultural system and the social system.
