WP, p. 50 → B776: "This, as we know, concerns not the scope of cathectic significance of the object, but the __kind__ of the cathectic significance with reference to one fundamental orientation-decision, namely whether or not to seize opportunities for gratification at a given moment in a given situation."X Here too, the definition is too narrow to claim completeness. There are undoubtedly affective relevances of attitudes that do not lie on the line of satisfaction; e.g., precisely the expression moment emphasized by Parsons otherwise. Instead, it would be better to refer to consciously formed feeling (which can naturally only be stabilized if the corresponding expectations are satisfied).
