See note → B739: "Individualistic modes of thought tend to see processes such as self-deception and insincerity as characterological weaknesses generated within the deep recesses of the individual personality. It might be better to start from outside the individual and work in than to start inside the individual and work out. We may say that the starting point for all that is to come later consists of the individual performer maintaining a definition of the situation before an audience. The individual automatically becomes insincere when he adheres to the obligation of maintaining a working consensus and when he participates in different routines or performs a given part before different audiences. Self-deception can be seen as something that results when what were originally two different roles, performer and audience, are compressed into the same individual.
