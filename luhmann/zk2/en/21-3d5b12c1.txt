A good relevant representation of the problem without using the term can be found in Simmel, *Religion*, 1922, p. 7ff → B1343: disturbances and conflicts at a small scale can thus be resolved by absolutizing and relativating a form principle respectively. "What could not reconcile itself with other elements of life as part and relative magnitude into which it entangles itself can win an organic, satisfied relationship to them as the Absolute and Ruling one" (p. 7 → B1343). Carried out in the case of religion. What remains unclear is the relationship among a plurality of such totalizations toward each other.
