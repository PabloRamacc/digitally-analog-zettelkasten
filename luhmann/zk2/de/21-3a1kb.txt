__Hemphill, S. 89 → B506__: "A problem is here defined as a state of affairs that is perceived with dissatisfaction." Unbrauchbar, da es auch im sozialen Prozess nicht wahrgenommene Probleme gibt.
Donald M. Johnson, The Psychology of Thought and Judgment, New York 1955, S. 63 → B507: "A person may be said to have a problem if he is motivated toward a goal and his first goal-directed response is unrewarding."
George Humphrey, Thinking, London 1951, p. 312 → B508: "A problem is a situation which for some reasons[*] appreciably holds up an organism in its effort[*] to reach a goal."
