Trapp 1780 → B2516 / 1913, p. 15: “Education is the formation of human beings for the sake of bliss.”
Bliss is then characterized as “a state of pleasant sensation” (pp. 15ff. → B2516) and “pleasant sensation” is introduced as an indefinable basic concept.
Consequently, one can speak of bliss when perfection becomes a pleasant sensation.
