Nicolai Hartmann (1924), in particular pp. 161 ff., → B512, deals with the difference between systematic and aporetic problem-oriented thinking.
The former defines problems according to a previously adopted system standpoint and treats everything else not as wrongly posed questions.
The latter starts from the problem and can be guided by it; while it does not reject system formation, it holds that only a limited degree of it is achievable.
