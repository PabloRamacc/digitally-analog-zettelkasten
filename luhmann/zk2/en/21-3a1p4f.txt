In the definition of identity through its function for ordering expectations lies a __dynamicization of the concept of substance__. Identity is something unfinished and unclear, hence capable of development and renewal (cf., e.g., → 532/4b8b1 ). The change in what remains identical despite identity occurs through changes to individual expectations (not by changing all at once).
Here one should think not only about an objective change in things but primarily about shifts in their meaning that also react back into the past; reinterpretations of the past are required, so that the past of the identical acquires a different sense than it had as present. Cf. much on this by Strauss 1959 → B666.
