The neo-humanist critique of the principle of happiness (as a category of reflection in philanthropy) encompasses
1.) A critique of the moral and pedagogical value of pleasant sensations;
2.) A critique of implied self-interest as opposed to __pure__ morality; → 7/25g43dA1ia
3.) Critique of the focus on consequences rather than on pure ideas or a priori principles.
See Schuderoff 1792, esp. pp. 43ff. → B2073 Instead, a kind of socialization of the a priori is conceived.
