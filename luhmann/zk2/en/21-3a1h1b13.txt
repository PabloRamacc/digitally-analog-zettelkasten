With regard to the continuous functions of multifactility, one cannot represent the evolution of society as a development from functionally diffuse to functionally differentiated without specifying more precisely in what relation functional differentiation stands with respect to social systems (primary subsystems!).
It is rather correct to say, similarly like for the continuum concrete / abstract, that complex systems have __both__ possibilities and can choose between them, and that their rationality advantage lies therein.
Simple systems can therefore only afford multifunctionally used facilities against this background, due to reasons of institutional economy.
