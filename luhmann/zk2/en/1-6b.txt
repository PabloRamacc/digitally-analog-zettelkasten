This conceptual approach also provides the ontological __concept of structure__. Structure is the order of the relationships between the parts, which causes them to constitute a whole. → 21/8a7a(1)
See, e.g., Kosiol, G., p. 16 → B41: “Such an organization, which progressively divides the whole into subunits that are themselves wholes, and in particular places the individual elements in coherent, reciprocal relationships of co-ordination, superordination and subordination—[…], and such a hierarchical order of the elements of a whole is generally referred to today as __‘structure__.’”
This concept of structure differs significantly from the functional concept of structure—more on that → 21/8 .
