→ 7/25g56 General characteristics of the concept of __technology__: → 7/25g56,5,1
Technology presupposes accessibility and transparency of the relational structure to accommodate changing modes of access—that is, a multi-level system.
In this respect, the concept is system-dependent.
It is a secondary question whether access is carried out by the agent themselves or by another, and whether it serves to adapt to an environment or to learn according to one’s own criteria.
