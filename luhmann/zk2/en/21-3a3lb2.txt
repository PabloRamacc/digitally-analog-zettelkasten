__The generalization in a system like this double interchange lies essentially in the fact that__, (or leads to), __that in each of the involved systems inputs are estimated as conditions of possible outputs and vice versa__.
Parsons, Lecture → B702[*], formulates with reference to "Alienability of factors of production: They are inputs valuated as conditions of outputs." However, therein lies a quite general principle. The generalization refers to the character as "condition": It may remain open what one buys for money or what concrete political trust is used for, etc., and precisely this range of usability is considered essential for the input.
