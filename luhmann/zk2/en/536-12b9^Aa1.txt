Article “Conversation” from the *Encyclopédie ou Dictionnaire raisonné des sciences, des Arts et des métiers*, Vol. IV, Paris 1754, reprinted Stuttgart 1966, pp. 165ff. → B2021:
“Conversation generally refers to any mutual discourse that revolves around whatever it may be, whereas ‘entretien’ refers to a mutual discourse that __revolves__[*] around a specific subject. […] The rules of conversation are generally not to dwell on any one subject, but to move lightly, effortlessly, and without affectation, from one topic to another.”
