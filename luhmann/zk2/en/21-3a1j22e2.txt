Observation of self-referential systems is __observation of more complex and opaque systems__.
This leads to: 1.) the thesis that only cognitive interaction itself can be transparent (Gordon Pask, Glanville);
2.) a reformulation of "psychological" terms as __cover terms__ for the __unobservable__ (Zopf 1962, p. 327ff. → B456). → 21/3a1j22e2A Unobservable both to external observation and to self-observation.
