__Observing as an Operation__
Observing is only possible as an operation, can gain reality only as an operation, and indeed as an autopoietic operation.
Operations proceed blindly; they do what they do, practice what they practice. How then is the blindness of observing to be understood? See also → 21/3a1j22j3.
As an operation, observing is __the deployment of a distinction for designation__, execution of the instruction draw a distinction.
