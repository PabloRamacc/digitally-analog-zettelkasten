The theory of coordinating generalizations is to be connected with the functional interpretation of causality. This connection is of fundamental significance for linking communication theory with bureaucracy theory; cf. → 35/3. Cf. also Parsons' quote → 21/3d5c1C3 in his → B956.
Generalizations can refer to individual stages of the causal process. In essence, these stages are always generalized in this sense if they serve as functional reference problems.
