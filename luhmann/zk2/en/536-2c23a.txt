For a detailed discussion of ending interactions at a bar, see Cavan, esp. pp. 54ff.; 21f. → B1477
The ease of initiating contact is correlated with the rule that contact can be terminated at any time. → 536/11eA1a
One must therefore consider the rules for initiating contact and the rules for ending it, along with the degrees of freedom in both areas, together.
In between lies the system of interaction, which can modify this relationship: If one accepts a drink but does not buy one in return, ending the interaction becomes more difficult.
