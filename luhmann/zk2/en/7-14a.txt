return to the philosophical presuppositions of thought.
The golden age of Western pedagogy was the era of ontological metaphysics, in which influence on human beings could be conceived as both rationalization and socialization, because right action itself was capable of truth—and not merely statements about the relationships of (correct) action to their incongruent, actually unintended factual circumstances. This made it possible to demonstrate true action, from which a rational human being—to the extent that he realized his essential human nature: reason—could not escape.
