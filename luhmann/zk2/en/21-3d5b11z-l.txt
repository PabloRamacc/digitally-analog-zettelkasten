__Context with: Meaning – Reproduction – Communication – Media__
System differentiation facilitates the reproduction of meaning by channeling it into subsystems with specific thematic areas. This also makes __evolutionary selection mechanisms__ (= reproductive communication) independent to a certain extent from one another. Law can develop without immediate dependence on what happens in religion. This is connected to the fact that system differentiation follows the differentiation of media, namely facilitated reproduction; the justification for this thesis lies here.
