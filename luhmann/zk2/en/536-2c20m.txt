In highly complex decision-making systems, it becomes necessary to differentiate the decision-making structure in the form of reflexivity—in particular, to distinguish between allocation decisions and substantive decisions (see → 35/5h4aa ).
The question is: Whether such a differentiation can be achieved and made explicit in discussions; what degree of rationality abstract allocation decisions can possess; and how the exercise of authority over them correlates with the status structure of the discussion system.
