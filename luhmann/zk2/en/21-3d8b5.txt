Under compatibility is to be understood __possibility of coexistence__.
The term is thus, like all possibility terms, only to be clarified through a closer characterization of conditions of possibility.
This difficulty one often saves oneself by taking an indirect route via negation, i.e., by defining compatibility as __non-necessity of a contradiction__.
