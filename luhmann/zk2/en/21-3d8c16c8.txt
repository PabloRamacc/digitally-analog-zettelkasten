Scarcity is a form of __reconstruction of contingency__ that must be seen as a functional equivalent to law. That other things could also exist will be conceded under specific limitations, and these limitations are pushed into the matter itself from which they develop.
See here Sartre's development from L'etre et le néant → B821 to Critique de la raison dialectique (esp. p. 200ff on: rareté) → B1633. Furthermore Klaus Hartmann 1966, p. 86ff. → B1634. For Sartre scarcity appears as contingency of being, which is also socially relevant.
