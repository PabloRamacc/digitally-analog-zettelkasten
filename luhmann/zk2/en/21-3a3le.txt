Assuming a concept of equilibrium based on __latent causes__ that become effective in case of disturbance, the built-in nature of such specific latent causes is the core idea of restorative or stabilizing mechanisms.
Parsons' assumption seems to be that the human organism's capacity for generalization is such an embedded mechanism that enables the equilibration of social systems. → 21/3a3le(R)
See also SS, p. 6, note 2 → B348: "A mechanism as the term will here be used is an empirical generalization about motivational processes stated in terms of its relevance to the functional problems of an action system."
Latent causes must be empirically specifiable → 21/3a3lf
