Very illustrative in this regard and precisely on the same line are Wildavsky's remarks from 1964 → B150 on the function of trust in the process of bureaucratic-parliamentary budget preparation; see especially p. 74ff. → B150 The problem is consciously functionalized with respect to the question of complexity.
p. 74 → B150: "The sheer complexity of budgetary matters means that some people need to trust others because they can check up on them only a fraction of the time."
p. 171 → B150: "Clientele and confidence strategies are desirable as well as inevitable in a democratic society.
