The risky aspect of the process of becoming known lies in the necessary initiatives that are always self-revelations and yet can rightfully – namely on account of the status quo – be rejected.
This is a general phenomenon that is not only characteristic of the process of becoming known but also, especially for boundary roles. Cf. → 44/7h.
