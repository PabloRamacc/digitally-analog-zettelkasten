__Images are social identifications with a specific function__: They are simplified, thereby stabilized and action-enabling conceptions that enable a system to make sense of its environment.
Image theory belongs in the context of the system/environment problematics.
Images serve to stabilize a system–environment relationship (through which they simultaneously become subject to targeted possibilities of influence). The own stability of images, whose conditions can be further researched, thus serves to stabilize a larger connection.
