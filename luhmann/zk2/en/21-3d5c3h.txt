The above schematic of pattern variables also offers approaches to the elaboration of certain functional problems and possibly a theory of variation that does not fully coincide but shares some similarity with Parsons' system-problems and Phase-Movement Theory (cf. → 21/3d5c4).
The guiding idea is that every identification, every solution of the orientation problem contains characteristic weaknesses that can be understood as functional problems.
For the two weak or negative pairs spec / univ and neutr / qual this is evident: taken by themselves they do not provide a sustainable world-orientation but require supplementation through stronger identifications with each other group respectively. Thus specificity presupposes affectivity, quality particularism etc., for example.
