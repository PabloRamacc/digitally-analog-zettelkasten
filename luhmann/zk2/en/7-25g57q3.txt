2.) → 7/25g57q __Discrepancy Between Assessment and Diagnosis__ → 7/25g57q3,1
The typical, everyday teacher’s judgment assumes that it can combine both: assessing a student’s performance level and identifying the reasons for underperformance.
As the basis for assessment __becomes increasingly scientific__ (and thus also in line with the educational establishment), __assessment and diagnosis drift far apart__; this requires entirely different assessment tools, etc.
The collection of mere cross-sectional data does not allow for a diagnosis.
