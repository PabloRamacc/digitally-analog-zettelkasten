# Luhmann archive material

The German Zettelkasten transcriptions in this directory are from the [Niklas Luhmann-Archiv, Universität Bielefeld](https://niklas-luhmann-archiv.de/). The archive states that its online material is licensed **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. These files are adaptations of the transcriptions and carry the same license. See the [license deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

The English box is an automatic translation from German using DeepL and Ollama (`qwen3.5:9b`), without human review. It is a derivative and is shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as well.

The files are adapted to this app's plain text format: underlining is written as `__text__`, links as `→ number`, and scans are not included. No claim is made over the archive's original material.

The archive's suggested citation for an individual card includes the author's name, box, card number and date, archive name, identifier, location, version, and stable card URL. For example:

> Luhmann, Niklas (1963–1997): ZK II Zettel 9/8 (undatiert), in: Niklas-Luhmann-Archiv (2021): ZK_2_NB_9-8_V. Bielefeld: Universität Bielefeld. [Permalink](https://niklas-luhmann-archiv.de/bestand/zettelkasten/zettel/ZK_2_NB_9-8_V).

The archive's pages provide the current version and full citation for each card. Its [online collection](https://niklas-luhmann-archiv.de/bestand/) is continuously maintained.
