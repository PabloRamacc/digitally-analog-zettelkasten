not applicable to all members of society; situational conditions determine their application and sanctions. __Collectivities__, a level lower down, denote purpose-oriented, concrete action systems governed by values and norms (see also → 21/3d5b5), which assign societal support and enable the specification of purposes – and thus: functional specifications in overall society. __Roles__ are finally the last unit, individual behavior units governed by higher "patterns". Collectivities consist of roles, not persons.
For evaluation see also → 21/3d5b11e1 → 21/3d5b11e2.
