This schema corresponds to a differentiation of temporal perspectives: – Function as (inexorable) __present__;
– Performance as __future__;
– Reflection as the system's __past__.
Here, both with regard to the future and with regard to the past there arises a problem of choice: promote or prevent; continue or discontinue. Only the present of social existence remains inevitable by necessity; but this is conceived as an interim- and decision situation and thus freedom[*]
