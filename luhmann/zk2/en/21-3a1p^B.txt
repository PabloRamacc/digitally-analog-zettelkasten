→ 21/3a1p __Strengthening expectations through factual consistency, associative proximity, etc.__ → 21/3a1pB(R)
For this see Stogdill, p. 69 → B321 (cited in → 21/3a1pc ), pp. 71f. → B321: "A confirmed expectation provides a set of reference experiences for evaluating related expectations. The reinforcement of one expectation may also act to strengthen other expectations." etc...
A special case of logical inconsistency occurs when an expectation cannot be fulfilled without disappointing another. For this see Gouldner, Wildcat , pp. 135f. → B369 (cited in → 21/3d8c5b ) with the example "seniority and efficiency as criteria of advancement".
