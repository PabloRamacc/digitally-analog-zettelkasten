s/f
Application of the input/output model to the performance domain of the education system.
For both boundaries, there are overarching considerations, which, however, are not identical:
- For the __input limit__: __equal opportunity__, particularly by neutralizing class-based criteria; - For the __output limit__: __quantity__—the right amount for each category of education.
These considerations are __not__ reversible. It is not possible to sort the input quantitatively (numerus clausus), nor is it possible to produce class-neutral results in the output.
