s/f
To emphasize sharply the __extreme formality__ of the term in line with Spencer Brown's logic (see also Fritz Simon 1988 → B432).
The term also goes beyond the distinctions 1.) knowledge/object,
2.) significator/signified,
3.) Recognizing/Acting,
4.) normative/cognitive
in advance; for it is about distinctions.
Therefore the term is also suitable for a sociological (possibly psychological, etc.) grounding of logic, including the special form of Spencer Brown's calculus itself.
