Social planning of a __differentiated educational system__ → 7/25g7,1 can probably only be conceived in connection with the educational mechanism becoming reflexive. In this respect, differentiation and reflexivity are interrelated.
Planning must be limited to the first phase of the reflexive process—namely, the education of educators—and can thus rationalize itself without anticipating the future state of the system that it brings about.
