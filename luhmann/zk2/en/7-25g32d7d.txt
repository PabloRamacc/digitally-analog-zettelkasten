To what extent is early withdrawal a consequence of selection policies (i.e., something that can be influenced by them)? By no means necessarily!
See Gerstein 1972 → B2786: 42% of early leavers had never been held back (55% girls, 35% boys) p. 115 → B2786.
Grades alone are not a sufficient explanation either, although, of course, the dropouts’ grades tend to decline as they approach the time of their withdrawal.
However, 80% of dropouts have the same __grade point average__ in German as high school graduates. p. 122 → B2786.
