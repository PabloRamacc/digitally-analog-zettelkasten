The semantic career of the concept of value since the 19th century can be traced back to the now necessary acceptance of __contingency__ in all social structures.
Much more than ever before, one must expose oneself as a decision – or submit to the question why something is not done against it through decision.
Consequently, new "inviolate levels" are required (Hofstadter → B434); and the concept of value provides the answer.
