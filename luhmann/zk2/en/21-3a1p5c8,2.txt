According to the distinction between → 52/8j, a differentiation must be made here between changes in self-identification (while maintaining continuity) and situation-bound shifts in social self-presentation, as noted in → 21/3a1p5c8.
Strauss (1959) → B666 deals with both forms of exchange without a clear distinction, primarily however with the former. For this see → 52/8k.
Above all, it concerns quite different events when one relinquishes a role because the situation is no longer suitable for that purpose, partners are missing, etc., or when one gives up a role because one has outgrown it and it no longer corresponds to one's self-conception. One can speak of micro- and macro-dynamics with regard to this difference. It concerns a change in relation to various identities, which certainly cannot be thought without connection to each other.
