One of the characteristic consequences __of greater interdependence is a faster pace__. → 21/3d2b13,1 Increasing behavioral entanglement translates into increasing rates of behavior, one of many signs of an entanglement in both substantive and temporal dimensions. Another would be, for example, the connection between problem awareness and progress.
Behind this lies a principle of functional equivalence between the time dimension and the matter dimension, which is valid only for specific functional reference points. This means that certain problems of the matter dimension—namely those arising from increasing interdependence—can be solved by acting faster. Put more simply: more appointments compel an acceleration of the work tempo.
