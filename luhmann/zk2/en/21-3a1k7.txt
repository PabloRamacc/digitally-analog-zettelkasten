Determining the reference term as __problem__ and not as ontic facticity is crucial for the meaning of functional analysis. → 21/3a1k7(R) This sets it against any teleological explanation. It remains important that the perspective of reference nevertheless stays "__ground__" of functional analysis – even if no longer ground in the sense of ontological metaphysics.
The insight that the unit of reference of analysis is a stabilization problem – and not a hypothesis of constancy – penetrates; → 21/3a1k7a but it does not become clear that the unit of reference retains its function as basis for explanation, so one falls back into a purely causal explanation of solving the problem of stability of this unity through functional performances. For example Nagel 1956 → B252. → 21/3a1k8
