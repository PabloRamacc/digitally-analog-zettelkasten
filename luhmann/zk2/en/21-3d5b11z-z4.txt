The change in the form of differentiation (or the corresponding centralization of evolutionary achievements) is the explanation for why evolution does not proceed continuously but occurs in leaps that realize new possibilities relatively quickly.
See the concept of "__punctuated equilibria__" (between population and eco-system with Stebbins 1982, p. 17ff → 21/3d5b11z/z4,1 → B3463) → 21/3d5b11z/z4,2.
New forms of differentiation opened up new possibilities for updating possibilities (which were already prepared latently; otherwise the step would not succeed; pre-adaptation).
