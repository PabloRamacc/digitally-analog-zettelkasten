The perspective on whether the "basis of significance" primarily concerns the individual object and its relational system or not: under the aspect of dominance in the motivation system—or its relative irrelevance in orientation—the group aff neutr / perf qual should be grouped together. If one wishes to formulate an overarching category for this, it would have to go beyond Parsons perhaps by asking about the stabilizing function of identification → 21/3d5c3b1; → 21/3d5c3b7. Primarily if an object is to be stabilized, a stance becomes diffuse; the object is identified particularistically → 21/3d5c3b3, otherwise the stance may become specific and the object universalistic. If primarily the experiencing self as motivation system is to be stabilized, the stance becomes affective, the object identified in terms of its performance value; otherwise the stance remains neutral or the object primarily qualitative → 21/3d5c3b4; → 21/3d5c3b(R).
