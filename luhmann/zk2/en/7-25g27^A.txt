→ 7/25g27 Teacher learning—despite the complexity of the classroom interaction system.
For conceptual background, see the suggestions by Amidon and Flander (1971) → B1995.
In principle, this is merely a categorization of the behaviors of the teacher and the students.
The assessment of whether the teacher’s own categorized behavior needs improvement is left to the teacher (p. 45ff. → B1995). Some research findings using the same categorization framework provide a certain degree of guidance in this regard.
