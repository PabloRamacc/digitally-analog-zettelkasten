It is important to note that a discussion __must__ be a social system because mutually influencing systems can no longer logically be conceived of as separate entities. MacKay, 1964 → B1793.
This means that the systemic nature of a discussion within the discussion is no longer a matter of choice, or can only be decided by terminating the discussion. At the same time, this results in certain inescapable limitations for discussions.
Connection to Parsons’ problem of double contingency.
