3.) The Person as the Environment of Society (systems theory conception).
This sequence can be understood as a process of __dialectization__ in the history of science, as defined by Bachelard: development from what has gone before through carefully placed negation.
For all three conceptions, one could explore the question of how they relate to __the__ __stratified structure__ of society and how they reconcile human equality and inequality.
It is very easy to incorporate into the concept of perfection the idea that one allows nature to take its course at the levels at which a person is born.
