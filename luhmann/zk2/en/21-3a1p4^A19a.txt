→ 21/3a1p4A19 The characteristic of the ritual → 21/3a1p4A19aa is that it symbolizes an attitude that does not fully reproduce real feelings and therefore serves primarily in interaction with others to overcome or suppress these feelings. This can happen in various ways, for example by providing a socially legitimate (socially harmless) form of expression for a problem and thus helping to suppress other forms of emotional expression that might be personally more immediate (as solutions to the problem). Example: mourning ceremony.
Another possibility is that the feeling pressing itself upon one personally (e.g., fear) is not expressed at all as an attitude, remains without social confirmation, and withers away (example: war ceremony).
