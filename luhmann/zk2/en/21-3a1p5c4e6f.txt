The scope of a colleague circle is determined by shared identification and the task of representation. → 21/3a1p5c4e6fa The term "colleague" must be understood relatively in this context.
This simultaneously explains the peculiar nesting of relationships within collegiality. In a narrower sense, only members of one's own organizational unit are colleagues; in a broader sense, all people in corresponding positions or all professional peers.
See also Bucher – Strauss, p. 330 → B882 for further distinctions between wider and narrower circles of collegiality.
