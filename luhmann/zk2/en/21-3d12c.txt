The fact that concrete events cannot be conceptualized with the help of unambiguous quality designations is connected to the majority of possible system references. __All__ terms of systems theory denote functions that enable concrete events only within specific systems and possess them solely in relation to these systems; thus, they never concern "inner" qualities per se.
This system relativization remains, as noted, limited to concrete events. It does not preclude the formation of general terms for several or even all systems. It certainly does not imply that a separate language of concepts must be created for each system.
