(e.g., situations, roles, persons, but especially familiarity and intimacy of the encounter) a variable minimum measure of respect for one's own self-presentation as a condition for continuing contact, at least in natural action not distorted by sanctions. This is for him a generalized normative expectation through his own self-identification. With reference to this fact, the concept of __respect__ → 21/3a1p5c1B4(R); → 21/3a1p5c1B4a; → 21/3a1p5c1B4b; → 21/3a1p5c1B4c must be defined. Respect is the acceptance of the presented (not necessarily identical with the "real" self-interpreation of the other along with its assigned system of social identifications). Respect in this sense is the foundation of every free, natural, i.e., not distorted by reward or punishment, social interaction. The meaning of sanctions is – under this aspect of significance for general social interaction –, to make respect as a basis of communication entirely or partially dispensable.
