The decisive moments of a genealogy of social conflicts probably lie in __attribution divergences__, partly systematic ones (over-attribution, under-attribution), partly socially structurally contingent ones (e.g., hierarchy-dependent). Cf. Watzlawick et al. → B1582 on punctualization. See also the analysis in MS. Society, section Experience and Action [*] → B1583.
In attribution divergences there is a tendency to establish attribution consensus with regard to diverging action – i.e., to shift the divergence from attribution (constitution) onto the action itself.
This disrupts the transfer of selection achievements.
