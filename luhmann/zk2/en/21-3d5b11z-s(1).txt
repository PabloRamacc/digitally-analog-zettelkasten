In more highly differentiated systems, conditions of compatibility among subsystems replace concrete constraints of the total system. In short: The constraints of the total system take the form of compatibility conditions or mutualities of interchanges.
Parsons 1969, p. 493 → B1368 regarding Polity: "Generally, the most fully differentiated system is to be regarded as that in which all three of the principal interchange systems are relatively 'free' from constraints other than the mutuality of the functional 'interests' of the polity on the one hand, each of the other primary functional subsystems on the other.
