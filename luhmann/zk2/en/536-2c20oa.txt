→ 536/2c20o Thinking involves not only coming up with an idea, but also relating it to the framework of thoughts already formed; developing it further, and putting it into practice.
When an idea strikes, you have to quickly devise a plan for how to develop it further.
And you can’t also pay attention to what others are thinking at the same time—let alone how you appear as a thinker to those who don’t think.
