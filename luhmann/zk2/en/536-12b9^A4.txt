The primary purpose of conversation, at first: __self-presentation__
See Du Bosq 1639, p. 52 → B2010: “The main purpose of conversation is to give the impression of having wit and judgment.”
See also pp. 112 ff. → B2010 on reputation.
__This very point is later disputed__. See de Villiers 1697, Vol. I, p. 210 → B2024: “A man should only be considered a good conversationalist when he knows how to hide who he really is.” Do not give prominence to one’s profession, habits, or personal skills!
