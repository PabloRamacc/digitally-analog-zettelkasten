This dependency does not only refer to the avoidance of unintentional errors; its core lies in the problem of __preventing indiscretions__. Once the problem of indiscipline arises within a group, one has secure grounds for assuming that the group exhibits team characteristics. Conversely, if indiscretions are inconceivable, it is usually about different kinds of groups. The problem of indiscipline can therefore be used as an index for the type of representation team treated here.
