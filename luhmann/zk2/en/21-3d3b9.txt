__Autonomy / Autarky__
The term __autarky__ refers to the exchange rate of system/environment. A system is autarkic insofar as it can throttle its exchange relationships with the environment.
The term __autonomy__ refers to the control of this exchange by the system, through system-own selective criteria; thus presupposing such an exchange itself. Even under intensive exchange, a system can maintain the autonomy of its selective criteria.
