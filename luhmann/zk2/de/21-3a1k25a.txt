2.) Die __Definition__ des Problems ist insofern __ausschlaggebend für den Problemlösungsprozess__. Sofern das Problem well-defined ist, stehen die Lösungskriterien fest, sofern sie ill-defined sind, handelt es sich nur um "open attributes", also nur um "open constraints" (S. 292ff. → B541). Open constraints sind charakteristisch für sehr komplexe Probleme. So erleichtern deren Lösung S. 293 → B541: "… that frequently it is just exactly the openness of certain constraints that makes a solution of the problem possible. These constraints provide definitional slack. They allow the problem solver to take a new tack on his problem, not by violating his constraints and setting up new ones (though he may of course do that too), but by adjusting those parameters of the constraint that are open to him."
