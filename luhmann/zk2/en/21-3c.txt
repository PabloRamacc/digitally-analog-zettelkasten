Following → 22/2b, the role of empirical systems as units of reference for a causally functional analysis can in principle be discussed.
The "stock of the system" is a formula for selecting certain effects as functional reference points for causal-functional analysis. Against this approach, the __charge of one-sidedness__ → 21/3ca is frequently raised in various respects: as an accusation of too abstract selection vis-à-vis concrete life fullness, as an accusation of being "conservative" → 21/3cd and unattentive to change regarding system-referentiality, or as an accusation of cultural one-sidedness and inability for comparison – charges that largely exclude each other in any case. → 21/3cb; → 21/3cc
