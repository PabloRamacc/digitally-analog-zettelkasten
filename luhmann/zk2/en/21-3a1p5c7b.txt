Under the title __Realigning Actions__, Goffman discusses, S. 120ff. → B739 __communications__ "out of character", i.e., in contradiction to the presentation made to present spectators.
There can arise from various reasons a need also for the spectator to recognize an contradictory attitude, e.g., when both sides feel their official version as a straitjacket but cannot get rid of it without risk, as a sign of rapprochement, etc. In doing so one then makes use of indirect communications which are discussed in → 352/9.
Further application cases are → 534/3e9; → 42/1A11A15; → 21/3a1p5c8: Necessity for formal recognition of a status difference, a legal situation, an obligatorily hostile attitude, despite a factual-contrary tendency towards collusion. Goffman, S. 124f. → B739 treats particularly fraternization between opposing specialists (S. 125 → B739: "the point is not that the secrets of the teams will be disclosed → 21/3a1p5c7b1.
