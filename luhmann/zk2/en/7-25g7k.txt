Undifferentiated education can only reproduce (though it does bring about change), even when it is intentional. Only differentiated education has the choice to be progressive or conservative.
See Geiger 1930, p. 416 → B2221 (in the language of that time): “Spontaneous education is thoroughly bound to the prevailing group spirit. By its very nature, it is rooted in the past and conservative.”
