Every generalization of expectations presupposes a minimum degree of self-identification and certainty of continuation → 21/3a1pc.
From this perspective, the topic of __trust__ → 21/3a1pc1a ; → 21/3a1pc1b ; → 21/3a1pc1(R) is to be addressed in general. Trust is tied to self-confidence and the stability of one's own personality. The more generalized trust is, the less susceptible it is to disappointment. Therefore, the generalization of trust—for example, in a very tolerant lived love, but also even more so in blind passion—can arise from fear of disappointment: One fixes onto the object to which one binds oneself, making it secure against disappointment. However, this does not contradict the finding that trust presupposes self-confidence: Self-confidence can be achieved and yet still be problematic. → 21/3a1pc1(R)
