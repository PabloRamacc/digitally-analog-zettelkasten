Communication of values occurs indirectly, alluding to them, implying them.
That very thing signifies their taken-for-grantedness. Their validity must be presupposed. Were values a direct object of communication, that would constitute an invitation to the partner to examine whether one wishes to accept or reject them.
This is connected to the peculiarity of *inviolable* levels within a differentiated communication system.
Values are systematically withdrawn from decision-making through communication, for that would automatically create new *inviolable* levels → 21/3a1pe9w1
