French Reform Movement 1760–1790
Entirely focused on planning a public school system without incorporating any significant pedagogical ideas:
La Chatolais[*], Abbé Coyer, Diderot, Condorcet, Mirabeau.
The reform cannot, it seems, be burdened with pedagogical reflections and controversies at the same time.
Apparently, pedagogy as such is not yet a socio-politically relevant reform movement; its ideas for improvement lie at the level of interaction.
