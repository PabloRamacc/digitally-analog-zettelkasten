It would be important to trace the connections between systems theory and __historicism / evolutionism__ – that is, by means of Cuvier's *Vorlesungen über vergleichende Anatomie*, 4 vols., Leipzig 1809-1810 → B1110.
The insight into functional shifts in interdependencies internally and interruptions of continuity externally provide the elements that can later be arranged in a historical sequence, replacing notions of an enduring typology of being for nature and its possibilities.
For this see Foucault's presentation 1971, p. 322ff → B1111.
