__Form concept__: Adoption of a thesis from Whitehead 1929, p. 44 → B805: "it is not 'substance' which is permanent, but 'form'" – to another system-selective form concept.
Systems constitute permanence through their reductions—and thus time.
Also evaluable for the connection between system formation and temporality.
Time is not independent of forms of thinking. (How far does this represent a new thought compared to structure?)
