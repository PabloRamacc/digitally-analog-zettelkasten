For the preservation of equality, a __professional ethics__ is essential that regulates competition among colleagues against each other. Fundamentally this applies to → 52/10f; see also → 534/3e15d and → 534/3e15f.
This holds for free professions especially with regard to behavior toward the "client" (restrictions on advertising, not taking away clients, etc.), and for organized activities with regard to behavior toward superiors.
For professional ethics as competition control, cf. some remarks by E. Gross 1958, p. 230f. → B883
A special problem is competition especially because of limited but nonetheless existing opportunities for advancement. For this see → 523/3c6; there also more detailed information on remedies.
