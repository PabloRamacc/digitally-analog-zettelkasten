Individual characteristics of "natural valuation situations": 1.) __Majority of values__ – See Braybrooke - Lindblom, p. 23ff. → B509 Also see some points in → 532/4c with regard to the concept of purpose.
2.) __Difficulty of identification and isolation of individual values__, dependence on formulation. In this too, apart from the plurality of diverging interests, a reason for the appearance of a majority of value perspectives may lie, a reason that can be remedied not by rational analysis but only through decision-making. See also Braybrooke - Lindblom, p. 23ff. → B509
3.) __Instability of values__; Braybrooke - Lindblom, p. 26ff. → B509 This is practically the time dimension of a majority of values. Also see above on situational dependence.
