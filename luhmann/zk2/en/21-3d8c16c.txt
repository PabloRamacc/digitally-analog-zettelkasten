In this context, the theory of __scarcity__ → 21/3d8c16cf; → 21/3d8c16cg can also be integrated – scarcity understood as a schematization of a value conflict. See also → 7/9(2); → 532/11b; → 535/13f(1); → 32/3g8f4 (syst Ankn). → 21/3d8c16ce
Scarcity is a general schema for system-internal processing of inconsistencies among the potentials offered by the environment → 21/3d8c16cd – and not, in any case, a property of nature.
Therefore, it is also incorrect to assume that scarcity decreases with increasing complexity and performance capacity of systems – though equally questionable is the opposite view that scarcity would increase during civilizational processes as complexity grows. → 21/3d8c16ca Rather, one must distinguish: what increases? → 21/3d8c16c1
