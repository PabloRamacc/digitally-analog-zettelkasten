The terms self-description/other-description refer to the descriptive subject.
They stand therefore crosswise to the (more common) distinction of internal/external descriptions (e.g., R. Rose [*] 1973 → B450), which refer to the object of description.
The comparison leads to the question: can a self-description be an external description? Precisely this is denied by the theory of autopoiesis. Why?
