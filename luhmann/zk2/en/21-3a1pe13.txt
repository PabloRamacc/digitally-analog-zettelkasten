A particularly interesting problem of expectation theory lies in the possibility of __negative expectations__. → 21/3a1pe13,1; → 21/3a1pe13,2. There is presumably a close connection with the system problem (necessary membership and the problem of generalization).
Negative expectations express at the same time an indifference towards factual events and a prevention against disappointments.
In this context it may also be interesting that there are hardly any negative expectations in the realm of tangible values or nature; they are possible but rare. One expects, for example, when arriving as a tourist in Iceland, that there will not be trees because one has read about this beforehand.
