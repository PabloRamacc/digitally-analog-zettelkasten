Losano 1968 → B1085 criticizes the usual conflation or negligence in distinguishing between "external" and "internal" system concepts – external understood as extrinsic to the matter itself, analytical, terminus ad quem of scientific performance.
The history of the general system concept initially shows a development toward an external system concept. In jurisprudence, this shift was initiated by Jhering (though formulated problematically).
The external system is the result of legal theory; the internal system are the norms themselves.
