As __criticism__, one would point to the inadequate logical elaboration, which is essentially due to the lack of the equivalence idea. The inventory prerequisites are not always formulated independently of each other (e.g., E., F.), partly equivalents for each other and therefore not general enough (e.g., H., J.), partly too general (various directions of exchange, e.g., A.). There lacks any logical order of the relationship to one another, and in particular the idea of a hierarchical ordering is missing, as becomes clear from the elaboration and exemplification of the individual inventory prerequisites.
