Historically, the problem of selection can only arise with the transition of education from the family to the school. The family does not select; it fosters.
The first phase of this school-based pedagogical awareness of selection was presumably a __motivational__ concept. The prospect of the difference between success and failure was intended to motivate students to seek success and avoid failure—as we now know, these are two distinct motivational constellations that cannot be reduced to one another.
