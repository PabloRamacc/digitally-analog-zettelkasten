→ 21/3a1 Here, it must first be determined what is meant by "system" at all, and which identifying (persistent) features of a system should stand in contrast to the insignificant (changing) ones. This determination can take place on various levels of generality: for all social systems, for those of a certain (more or less comprehensive) type, or for concrete systems. See also → 21/3a1f1d.
Therefore, it is very problematic to compile a universally valid list of prerequisites: it remains always relative to the chosen system.
The prerequisites of the more general type can however be understood as a minimal list for its more concrete elaborations. This thought leads → 21/3a1n1
