Second-order cybernetics must take into account the fact that __it cannot form a hierarchy itself__, i.e., __cannot offer a complete description of itself__: neither from above (from a highest point) nor from outside (in the sense of an internal description of an external position, from which the whole would be observable.
This consequence is also drawn for type theory or for the level hierarchy, which then wrongly is called a hierarchy (e.g., Löfgren in Jantsch 1981, p. 134f → B477 with reference to Russell himself).
