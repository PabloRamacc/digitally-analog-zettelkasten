__Routine__ sets in during instruction anyway. Relieving oneself of effort through the formation of habits, celebrated as experience—since time immemorial; see, e.g., Bock 1780, p. 124 → B2673:
“Experience shows him the tricks of the trade for teaching the learner everything with less difficulty and in a shorter[*] time,” etc.
The problem is to what extent the development of routine should be left to mere experience and how it can nevertheless be kept open to learning.
The relationship between technique and technology is a facet of this general problem.
