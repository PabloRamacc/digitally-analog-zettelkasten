Between the differentiation concepts of system/environment theory and cybernetics lies a fundamental difference of presumably high practical (and political) significance. The theory of intermeshed feedback loops uses a concept that identifies systems as system/environment regulatives and therefore treats differentiation as system overlap. As a result, systemic differentiation is not emphasized so sharply as in a theory working with the terms complexity and reduction.
