Further elaborations and applications of this idea can be found with Blau - Scott → B203, under the designation "__organizational dilemma__". For the concept itself see esp. p. 222; 250f. → B203.
Blau - Scott → B203 derive from it an internal, dialectical dynamic by attributing to the dilemma a kind of causal driving force for the search for new solutions, in strange contradiction to the sociological method that deals with latent functions.
Also unclear is the relationship to the functionalist method. See esp. p. 222 → B203: "The concept of dilemma greatly contributes to an understanding of internally generated processes of change in a social organization. If we conceive of social systems as meeting basic needs and requisities, the implicit assumption is that adjustment prevails and change does not occur unless new __external__ conditions → 21/3a1f7b.
