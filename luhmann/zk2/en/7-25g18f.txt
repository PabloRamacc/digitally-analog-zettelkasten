Didactics lacks an equivalent for what “cases” accomplish in other professions, namely: 1) facilitating learning through the transmission of practical experience; 2) serving as a starting point for the development of a teaching tradition and, ultimately, a body of doctrine.
Consequently, a causal technology must be taught—or at least presupposed in teaching—that does not actually exist in reality.
