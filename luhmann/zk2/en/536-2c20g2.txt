Part of reciprocity and the rhythm of the discussion is responding to the other person’s intention. Under what social circumstances is this possible, impossible, obvious, etc.?
Put negatively: Avoid “tangential responses”—that is, those that use communication merely as a pretext to pursue one’s own goals. See Laing 1961, pp. 93ff. → B1827 with the example: “Mummy, look what a big fat worm I have got. She: You are filthy—go and clean yourself immediately.” Taken from Ruesch 1958, p. 43 → B1828.
