The thesis of functional primacy is to be connected with Gouldner's → B1345 (polemically developed against Parsons and Marx) proposal of a stratified system model. Gouldner, Crisis 1971, p. 230f. → B1345
Gouldner → B1345 starts from the thesis that interdependence is a variable which can assume different values with regard to individual parts of the system. From this it follows that there must be parts that are relatively more __in__dependent (functionally autonomous) than others. This can be connected with the notion that functional primacy would fall upon the subsystem with relatively greatest own complexity.
