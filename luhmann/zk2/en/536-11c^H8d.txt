The relationships between personality structure and the perceptual process have not been sufficiently researched—(nor have those between social structure and the perceptual process). Presumably, the connection must be established with the help of the concept of selectivity.
See, for example, B. Kempler / M. Wiener, “Personality and Perception in the Recognition Threshold Paradigm,” Psychological Review 70 (1963) → B1943.
According to Abrahamson → B1750 K and W distinguish → B1943 three approaches: 1.) Perceptual avoidance theory;
2.) Hypothesis or expectancy theory;
3.) Response avoidance theory;
depending on which personality theory underlies it.
