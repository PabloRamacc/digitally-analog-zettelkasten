__Self-knowledge__—secularization of the old motif and theme through a shift toward an interactive function.
Self-knowledge makes it easier to __see through__ __others__.
See Thomasius 1710, p. 102 → B2008, Wisdom in Dealing with People—A Single Rule Instead of All Kinds of Tricks: “It consists of a single rule: The more you come to realize your own folly, the more you will recognize other people’s wisdom and folly without any other rules; and the less you know yourself, the less even a thousand rules on how to judge others will help you.”
