→ 536/11cB3 Follow-up analysis of the function of __the__ __greeting__.
The purpose of the greeting is to avoid entering into a social system despite shared presence. It presupposes that there is a possibility—indeed, a suggestion—in this direction. It wards off this possibility __without causing offense__, that is, without precluding the possibility of forming such a system at a later time.
Through its distinctive form, a greeting enables rapid, so to speak, system-free communication regarding this complex intention.
