In both respects, interruptions in interdependence are __initially__ present in the teaching/learning process. They are what make it a definable system in the first place. The question is: to what extent can they be __reduced__ in the interest of
1.) greater autonomy in relation to the environment;
2.) greater predictability with regard to the future.
These questions are directed at the system’s self-regulatory mechanisms—and thus, ultimately, at pedagogy.
