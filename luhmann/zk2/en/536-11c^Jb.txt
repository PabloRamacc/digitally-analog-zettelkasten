→ 536/11cI Starting point: In all thematically focused gatherings, communication is forced into a __serial__ order. This entails a considerable expenditure of time; see → 7/37 .
Time scarcity thus __arises__ only from the basic structure of thematic concentration and from the conflict __this__ triggers with other dimensions (time spent on clarifications; the compulsion to synchronize).
Any assessment of the issue of serial selectivity must proceed from the assumption that it is a form of meaningful experience and action that is inherently selective due to the flow of time itself.
