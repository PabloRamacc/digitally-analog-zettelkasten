Symbols can fulfill their function only by summarizing a differentiated system of references; they thus presuppose generalization and organization (cf. also → 21/3a3lb5).
This means that in terms of time dimensionality, they presuppose the capacity for memories and expectations. In temporal perspective, their coordinating performance consists in the crystallization of expectations and memories (or inferences about what has been).
See also Parsons, WP, p. 33 → B776: "Sign behavior of any sort (involving either 'signals' or symbols in Morris → B644' sense [ → 21/3a1p4A9(R) ]) implies a certain differentiation of the most elementary form of action system, and also the extension of action process in time, in such a way that the concept of expectation (and memory) becomes relevant.
