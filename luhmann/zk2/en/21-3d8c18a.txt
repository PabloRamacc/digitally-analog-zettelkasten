For non-differentiated notions of scarcity, it seems typical to be—according to Foster 1965 → B1635, photocopy—a) an invariant notion of __constancy in quantity__, with the consequence: one at the expense of the other;
b.) a subsequent __moralization__.
Going beyond Foster → B1635, one might conjecture that quantity constants make moralization inevitable, precisely because of the consequence: at the expense of the other, although this too still allows for __very different__, e.g., cooperative and competitive __moralizations__.
