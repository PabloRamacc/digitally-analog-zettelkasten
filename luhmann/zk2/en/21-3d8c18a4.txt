Scarcity behavior under the premise of constant sums leads to __direct__ action against nature and thus to attempts at production maximization within the system. See Röpke 1971, p. 452 → B1479 for details.
This means that this behavior is not calculated based on systemic rationality in terms of the __ratio__ between input and output.
Only the full abstraction of the scarcity principle, made independent of quantities of goods, enables shifting rationality as purpose/means-rationality into the system.
