Conversation with Parsons, Feb. 10, 1961[*]
__Fundamental connection between the methodological concept of functional equivalence and the concepts of organization and control in differentiated systems__.
Parsons believed that a theory of organization should be developed based on the leading models of economics (macroeconomics). There, management and control occur at levels of generalization where one can disregard specific details of how variables are filled in without it making a difference to the system’s existence (as viewed at this level). The question of how deeply one engages with the differentiation of a system is not a matter of right or wrong, but rather a matter of the chosen level of abstraction.
