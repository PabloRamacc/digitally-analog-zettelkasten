As long as collegiality also requires representations vis-à-vis the common superior – and this is most often the case – communicative barriers of discretion must likewise be erected vis-à-vis the superior.
In this sense, there exists a widespread institutionalization of a collegial norm against "snitching," "ratting out," etc., to the detriment of colleagues, which is frequently regarded as core elements of good collegial behavior.
See also Jaques 1951, p. 281f → B895 with reports on mutual suppression of criticism among colleagues before superiors. If so, then either after prior notification of the colleague or "confidentially" in expectation of the superior's discretion.
