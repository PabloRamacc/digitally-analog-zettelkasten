The concept of __conflict resolution__ must be conceived as correspondingly complex. → 21/3d8c2(R)
It is inherent in the concept of a prerequisite that it either has to be fulfilled or else the system collapses. In this sense, every conflict comes to an end fundamentally, either through the destruction of one involved system or through an adaptation of the involved systems to each other. The adaptation takes place such that equivalents are found for the disputed functional performances. These equivalents can in turn burden the involved systems with subsequent functional problems and so on. The __entire__ system must adapt itself – along with a wealth of internal consequences – to the conflict situation. [Therefore, it is expedient to conceive conflicts as relations between systems.] Then u.U. → 21/3d8c3
