The thinking of ontology regarding wholes and parts is surpassed and left behind by the stipulation of the problem of contingency (questioned).
This becomes particularly clear in the relationship between Hobbes and Aristotle, as well as to the Aristotelian tradition, specifically regarding the style of justification. See Riedel 1969 → B1672 for this.
The question of the __beginning__ no longer played a role in the distinction between whole and part; it is no longer sufficient to explain or conversely, the parts of society by reference to their beginning in the natural whole. Rather, justification transcends the whole along with its parts toward an antecedent privation, an absolute chaos that must first be ordered.
