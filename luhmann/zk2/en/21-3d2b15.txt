As a general and perhaps most important consequence of increasing interdependence is an __intensification of the communication process__ with its sequelae: generalization and specification of communications, increase in __mutual__ influence (in generalized form: power, influence, authority, leadership!).
Stimulating remarks on this by Riggs 1957 → B1140, which however suffer from the fact that they are not ultimately reduced to systems theory or communication theory. See also → 352/1,12b1.
