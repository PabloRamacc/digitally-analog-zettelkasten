Another important idea is the __connection between boundary maintenance and tension management__.
See Goffman 1961, pp. 41ff. → B1729 On “Case and Tension,” esp. p. 43 → B1729: “This concept of tension is crucial to my argument, for I will try to show that just as the coherence and persistence of a focused gathering depends on maintaining a boundary, so the integrity of this barrier seems to depend upon the management of tension.”
The basic idea here is that in tense situations that cannot resolve their problems, boundary violations, inappropriate behavior, etc., are inevitable.
