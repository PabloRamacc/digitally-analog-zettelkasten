The relationship between the home and school in the 18th century was highly problematic and characterized by a lack of respect for the school and by excessive demands. Stephan 1891, p. 152ff. → B2091 with references; likely because there was no universal compulsory education and because the upper classes educated their children at home.
Lieberkühn 1785, pp. 43ff. → B2438: Complaints about parental interference in lesson planning, too many excused absences for trivial reasons. Wealthier parents demanded more leniency for their children, etc.
