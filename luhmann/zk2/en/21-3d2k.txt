→ 21/3d2 The popularity of the concept of stock seems to be based on the fact that it makes a bivalent propositional form appear usable. When asked about existence, beings can assume only two states: being in stock or not being in stock.
See also → 7/15e for advantages of such binary calculation.
In contrast, it must be taken into account that no real system can afford an all-or-none principle for the whole; doing so would mean foregoing the advantages of differentiation and specification. See especially Ashby 1954, p. 136ff → B980 – more closely → 21/3d8g13 ff. → 21/3d8g16a.
This means: The theory of stock is only tolerable and realistic if it is refined into complexity through a theory of system differentiation. In this sense, the theory of stock and the theory of differentiation are inextricably linked. {1} {2}
