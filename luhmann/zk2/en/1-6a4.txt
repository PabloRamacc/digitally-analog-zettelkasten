On the relationship between __the__ __doctrine of justice__ and the whole-part schema—and thus also the law itself—see Cajetan’s commentary on the *Summa Theologiae* (Thomas) in II II q. 61. a. 1 → B55; in I II q. 60. a. 5 → B56 → 1/6a4,1
According to Giers, p. 25 → B57: There are three types of justice: 1.) The ordering of the parts to the whole: justitia legalis;
2.) The ordering of the whole in relation to the parts: justitia distributiva;
3.) The ordering of the parts in relation to the parts: justitia commutativa.
There are only these three forms in this schema.
