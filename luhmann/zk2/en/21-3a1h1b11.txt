The disadvantages of multifunctionality can be reduced to a certain extent if one provides rank decisions between the functions or manifest/latent differentiations, so that it comes about as __primacy__ of a function or an official purpose determination which does not effectively exclude other functions but yet drives them into the background of the less important or the unconscious, from out of which changes can offer less effective resistance; cf. → 21/3a1h1b2 ; → 21/3d5b12.
