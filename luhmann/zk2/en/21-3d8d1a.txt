Either the consideration is expanded by treating what was previously part of a whole and only addressed in analytically fragmentary relevance as its own action system in full concreteness; then the previous analysis becomes merely partially relevant. Or one retains the partial aspect without expanding it; then, with increasing detailing, the analysis itself becomes more abstract, and the capacity to perceive equivalences is unnecessarily cut off by a preconceived perspective.
The question is thus whether changing the reference of the system carries forward or nullifies the insights gained for the new analysis.
