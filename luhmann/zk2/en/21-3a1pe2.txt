The central position of the concept of expectation for __learning theory__ rests on the fact that learning is essentially seen as strengthening expectations (reinforcement of expectation). See Stogdill, p. 60ff → B321 citing Tolman → B661, Mowrer → B662, MacCorquodale / Mehl[*] → B669, Rotter → B663. Also see → 21/3a1pc10d.
Important here is that the term reinforcement is seen as complex and not merely related to one dimension, e.g. frequency of response. From Stogdill → B321 it is not entirely clear how far clarity generally exists in learning theory beyond this.
