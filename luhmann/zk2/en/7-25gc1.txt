The problems involved in distinguishing between the scientific system and the educational system deserve a detailed and nuanced analysis.
The convergence at the role and organizational levels is counterbalanced by clear distinctions in the conceptual framework:
1.) Critique of pedagogical conceptualizations from a research-methodological perspective (Bachelard);
2.) Differentiation at the level of the traditional complex of experience and learning (Buck 1969 → B2191).
