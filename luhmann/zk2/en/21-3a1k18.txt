For Merton 1961 → B513, the term "social problems" is synonymous with a discrepancy between standards and actual behavior – despite a much more comprehensive understanding of the structural problematic of social systems.
E.g., p. 702 → B513: "The first and basic ingredient of a social problem consists of any substantial discrepancy between socially shared standards and actual conditions of social life."
There are also latent social problems (p. 708ff. → B513), but their latency lies solely in the fact that the __discrepancy__ is unknown. Even latent problems are only possible with regard to __manifest__ (institutionalized) social expectations.
