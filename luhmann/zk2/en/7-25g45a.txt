Main point of comparison between media systems and the educational system: How are partners’ other possibilities treated?
In media systems, binary schematics are available for this purpose, through which the “other possibilities” can be expressed in a highly generalized, abstract manner. In the teaching/learning process, they must be specifically discouraged or downplayed with the help of teachers and instructional materials.
