Marney / Smith 1964, S. 113 → B983 definieren:
"a system is an __adaptive__ system if and only if its behavior maintains its essential variables within the limits of their respective norms. Here 'essential' variables are interpreted as those measures of an environment to which the survival of the system is sensitive." Dafür berufen sie sich auf Ashby, Design for a brain → B984, und auf die Entsprechung zum Begriff der Homeostasis.
