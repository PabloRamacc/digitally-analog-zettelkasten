This section deals only with the relationship between the differentiated education system and __other areas of society__; it by no means covers the entirety (or the defining characteristics) __of all__ system/environment references that occur in the field of education.
__Nevertheless__, the fact that the educational system is internally differentiated a.) into subsystems and b.) into levels (individual/organizational/societal) is also relevant to this external relationship.
In effect, this means: decisive responsiveness to environmental changes.
