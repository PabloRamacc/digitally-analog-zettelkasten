__Conversation__
A special case of discussion whose distinctive feature lies in the social obligation to keep __any__ topic moving. The motives lie beyond specific topics—primarily, it seems, in the social realm: in the mutual affirmation of goodwill and interest in one another.
This means that in such a system, there will be rules designed to facilitate changes of topic; rules against delving too deeply into topics or providing overly detailed answers to questions, etc.; and furthermore, rules regarding the avoidance of lecturing in both tone and content.
