→ 536/6 On role-switching in general, see → 5313/2f .
Here, we address only the specific problems that arise when a role must be switched in the course of a single interaction.
This gives rise to a host of problems, especially when the roles are contradictory and transitions must be found—without interrupting the interaction—that do not make the contradiction too obvious.
The role change by no means needs to be institutionally regulated in the sense that it is precisely determined when, under what circumstances, and how it is to be carried out. If such institutionalization is lacking, however, there must be certain forms and rules of communication regarding the intention to switch roles, which can be institutionalized as such without the specific occasions for their use being precisely defined.
