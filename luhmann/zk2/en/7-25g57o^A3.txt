Reference to constant / variable.
Praise and blame do not refer to fixed abilities, but rather to variable aspects—specifically, to achievements made through effort and to missed opportunities.
For an old tradition, see → 536/12b4 , Buffier 1726, pp. 196ff. → B1487 Praise should refer only to __voluntary__ actions, not to natural characteristics.
The argument has a binary structure: Otherwise, one would also have to be able to criticize the lack of these characteristics.
