Certainly, the concept of a prerequisite for existence includes that its fulfillment is required for the continuation of the system. This can be demanded for each individual prerequisite for existence. {1} {2}
Another question is whether, additionally, it should also be required that the continuation of the system __be secured__ when all inventory prerequisites are met. [This requirement naturally makes no sense with regard to individual inventory prerequisites but only in relation to them taken together; so too Hempel 1959, p. 294 → B258.] This requirement implies a higher degree of systematic connection; it only makes sense in relation to a closed and comprehensible group of inventory prerequisites and is therefore currently hardly fulfillable in the social sciences. → 21/3a1e5
