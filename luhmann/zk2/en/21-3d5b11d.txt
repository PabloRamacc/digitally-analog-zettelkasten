__Differentiation / Generalization__; see also → 21/3d5b11l
A central idea of Parsons' is that differentiated systems must develop generalizing mechanisms, e.g., interchange mechanism, or on the normative level general norms. Example: the traditional village with its family businesses (China), which receives industry work outside the family and therefore must develop a new, generalized unit no longer guided solely by the family (Parsons conversational remarks, 24.4.61)[*]. → 21/3d5b11da See also → 21/3a3l and in principle → 21/3a1pc. → 21/3d5b11db
A close connection exists with the thesis that generalization only makes sense as bridging contradictions; see → 21/4e.
Further evidence: Establishment of this connection between the requirement for generalized symbolic equipment and system differentiation by Goffman, p. 16ff. → B739; see → 21/3a1p5c4aA. Goffman → B739 → 21/3d5b11d1
