One of the characteristic features of the insecure is taking refuge in illegitimate means that are not accepted by the audience, attempting to play with false cards and thereby regain problematic control over the situation. Because the insecure feels uncertain, he believes it appropriate to employ means that others do not use. In this sense, insecurity always simultaneously constitutes compensation for a presumed social inferiority (inferiority complex).
For the illegitimacy of the action of the insecure see also Burns 1955, p. 474 → B919, cited in → 532/5d6aA5.
This contains at the same time the starting point for group formation based on shared insecurity.
