Superficiality is something one must be content with, because otherwise social interaction would be impossible.
Concluding statement: the other is too distant from us, and we are too close to ourselves to fully understand them (p. 133ff. → B2035).
Complete knowledge of human nature would confer superiority in all matters and is, for that reason alone, hardly desirable.
Specifically against examining strangers too closely, pp. 72ff. → B2035: They would disappear, and with them the pleasure we derive from them.
“Je ne sant jamais d’étruire son plaisir soi-même.” (p. 73 → B2035)
