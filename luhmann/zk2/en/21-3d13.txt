Without recourse to the concept of a __horizon__ → 21/3d13,1, the system concept cannot be fully clarified. The difficulty lies in the concept of __context__.
A system hangs together as a __complex of preferred possibilities__, which at the same time must be exclusive to some extent, i.e., come at the expense of other possibilities. How does such a referential order arise?
See already James, *Pragmatism*, p. 94ff., for systems in this sense of a referential context → B1716.
