__Identity – Order__ → 21/3a1p6(R)
The relationship between these two terms needs to be clarified; this is a task that can be undertaken once the ontological definition of identity and its presuppositions for thought have been abandoned.
__Identity cannot be positively defined absolutely, order cannot be negated absolutely__. One cannot assert or establish identity without keeping something non-identical in view. And one cannot negate order without presupposing something ordered.
This strange relationship suggests that identity and order are essentially the same thing (provided it makes sense to speak of sameness with regard to identity at all). Seen from the perspective of possibilities for negation, this is identity; seen from the perspective of possibilities for positive definition, this is order.
