Furthermore, it must also be noted that in each of these reference problems there are fundamentally two possibilities for establishing stable expectations: one can change the expectations to adapt them to the reinforcing connection or vice versa; one can e.g., adjust one's own expectations to the chances of fulfillment or conversely adhere to certain expectations and motivate others toward fulfillment. The same applies to consensus and consistency. Both possibilities are functionally equivalent __in general__ from the perspective of the overall problem of stable expectations, not so if one wishes to adhere to a specific order of expectation: then one has decided for __one__ of these alternatives and moved oneself onto an already more concrete level of analysis that leaves fewer possibilities open.
