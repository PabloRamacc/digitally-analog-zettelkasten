Introduction to: __Essays__ → VI (3)
The current state of research does not permit a systematic, dynamic presentation: there is a lack of completed research with definitive results in almost all fields.
Furthermore, the functional method contains a series of groundbreaking ideas that call into question both deductive and classificatory systems, though they cannot yet replace them. Therefore, the presentation must remain loose in its form and structure. This also addresses the general problems of presentation (linearity, repetition, cross-references).
On the other hand, this does not mean that the individual works are conceptually independent.
