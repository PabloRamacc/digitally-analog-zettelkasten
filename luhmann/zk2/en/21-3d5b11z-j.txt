__Adequacy__
S. adequate complexity → 21/3d18c35.
The criteria and control concepts for the concept of adequacy must be developed from the theory of system differentiation. Precisely therefore is adequacy not identity, equality or similarity.
The term refers to system/environment relations, thus to reductions with respect to an excessively complex (but in a certain sense self-ordered) environment.
Adequate are reductions not as shortened representations but as operation manuals, → 21/3d5b11z/j1
