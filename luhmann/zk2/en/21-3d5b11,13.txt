→ 21/3d5b11 Very important is a clear distinction between functional specification and system differentiation.
The distinction shows that it is rather an exceptional case when a system differentiation follows the principle of functional specification.
For functional specification as a general law of development, see Tatje / Naroll 1970 → B1257. See also as pioneering publication: Raoul Narroll, A Preliminary Index of Social Development, American Anthropologist 58 (1956), pp. 687-715 → B1258.
