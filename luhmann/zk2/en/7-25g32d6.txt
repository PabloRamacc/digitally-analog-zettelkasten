An important consequence of the internal differentiation of the school and higher education system is that, through its selection processes, the threshold of adulthood is pushed back for a __portion__ of the population, a transition that is further delayed by the start of their professional careers. See Parsons / Platt → B2429.
This gives rise to problems within the education system, as well as in the economy and politics—pure consequences of the external differentiation, internal differentiation, and autonomous selectivity of education.
