It must be noted that one can also __be surprised by their own actions__ and – whether positively or negatively – disappointed. For example when something succeeds better or worse than expected. See Strauss 1959, p. 32 → B666.
Therefore there must also exist special forms of expectation correction towards oneself. Presumably one corrects faster and more elastically compared to others because one continuously experiences their own acting in its factual conditions, possibilities and reasons.
From here out the problem of norms for oneself is then also addressed.
Finally disappointment through oneself naturally has significance for self-objectivation, the development of one's own self-conception.
