Functional differentiation presupposes the __repression__ of possibilities available to the total system that should not come into play within specific subsystems.
This already applies at the level of organic evolution. See e.g., Bullough 1967, p. 8ff. → B3452 following Jacob - Monod. Possibly see F. Jacob, J. Monod, Genetic Regulatory Mechanisms in the Synthesis of Proteins, J. Mol. Biol. 3 (1961), pp. 318-356 → B3453.
