system concept
__Def__ Ackoff - Emery 1972, p. 18 → B1002: "a set of interrelated elements, each of which is related directly or indirectly to every other element, and no subset of it[*] is unrelated to any other subset.
__Critique__: This definition does not exclude the environment but includes it, since it also maintains relationships with the system.
