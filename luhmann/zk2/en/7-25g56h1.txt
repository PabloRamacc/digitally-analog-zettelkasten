The adaptation concept has two related concepts:
__Cognitive adaptation__: The material must be comprehensible given the students’ current cognitive abilities—e.g., self-evident, elementary, and illustrative.
__Motivational adaptation__: The material must be attractive, must capture attention, be “as engaging as possible,” free and enjoyable, and incorporate play, etc. See Schrader, p. 102ff. → B3185
