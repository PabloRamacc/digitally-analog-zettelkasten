__Resistance to Sociology__
This seems to stem primarily from the fact that sociology’s right to a say cannot be entirely disputed; yet the __adoption of__ sociological __theoretical proposals__ in other disciplines __would result in__ a paradigm shift—__a Kuhnian revolu____tion__; even in fields where no consolidated paradigm exists, such as jurisprudence.
Sociology seems to expect other disciplines to undergo groundless revolutions.
