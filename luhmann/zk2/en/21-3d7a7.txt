The relationship between causality and systems theory is to be developed not from concepts of causality but from system concepts. That means: these, not those, have primacy. Not the causal efficacy of a system, but the use of the causal schema as a strategy for problem-solving deserves scientific interest. See also → 22/10.
The possibility of such a shift in the horizon of inquiry is given by the fact that we have abandoned the causal-scientific interpretation of functionalism and thereby can make the causal relationship itself its function into the topic.
