3.) The operational definition of an expectation also includes a precise characterization of the situation in which it should occur __and high certainty of occurrence__ – in Stogdill's → B321s terminology: Certain probability estimates. This goes hand in hand with the fact that certain expectations are more easily disappointed and more easily extinguished than uncertain ones, as if they half-anticipate disappointment themselves. In a sense one can say, when ignoring or equating the objective chances of occurrence, that __uncertain expectations are more stable than certain__ ones. These → 21/3a1pe8b3
