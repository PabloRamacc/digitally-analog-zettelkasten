→ 21/3a1p5e2a The selective character of the image is important in multiple respects and thus particularly suited to elucidate the interconnection of various aspects: 1.) Selection is a condition for the coherence and persuasiveness of the image, a condition of its imaginal nature itself, its vividness.
2.) selection is a condition for the stability of the image, the elimination of contradictory information.
3.) Selection is a condition for the instructive quality of the image, its suitability as an orientation- and behavioral basis. In this sense Dreitzel treats on p. 207ff → B934 the danger of flooding images with facts by science, an "overabundance of information" p. 207 → B934, which undermines the normative structure → 21/3a1p5e2a4.
