The gain of insight through incongruent perspectives presumably rests on an __expectation disconfirmation__ that makes expectations explicit and forces their classification into new contexts.
Interpreted roughly in this sense, psychoanalytic therapy must be understood. One becomes aware of one's complexes using scientific terminology and thereby acquires a new reference system for their object. However, the method has the disadvantage – as Burke points out on p. 176, note 1 → B665 – that the new reference system lacks sufficient social support other than through the therapist himself: "The weakness in the various individualistic schemes of cure offered since the breakdown of the religious rationalization seems to arise from the fact that the resultant socialization is but partial.
