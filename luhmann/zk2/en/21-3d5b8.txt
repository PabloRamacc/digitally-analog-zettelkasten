Regarding the concept of "Subsystem," Parsons' thought is important: in principle any unit can be interpreted as a subsystem, and its boundary can be drawn with reference to an action frame of reference; the interpretation must make sense in terms of this system's frame of reference (Structure of Social Action, p. 737ff. → B1229).

What is a unit in terms of one point of reference may always be treated as a system from another; this view of macroscopic-microscopic relations is fundamental to our whole treatment of action.
