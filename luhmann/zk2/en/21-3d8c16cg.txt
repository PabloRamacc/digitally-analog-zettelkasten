The term → 21/3d8c16c scarcity must be defined and distinguished from: 1) absence of things, 2) feeling of lack.
From legal theory: Scarcity is a calculative assumption that schematizes a conflict of values and brings it into the form of an alternative about which one can decide without value judgment.
For this a medium of exchange is required.
The fact that scarcity is not determined by shortage can already be seen in the destruction of goods for the sake of scarcity.
