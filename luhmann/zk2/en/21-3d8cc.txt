Critics of functionalism sometimes claim that it contains a harmonizing prejudice and does not adequately account for the actually existing conflicts and contradictions in the system. For example, Bucher - Strauss, p. 325f. → B882 → 21/3d8cca; Horowitz 1962 → B1500.
This is closely connected to the criticism of functionalism as conservative – and is equally unfounded. → 54/2a
Both criticisms overlook the abstractability of functional reference points and the possibility of working with multiple system references. In particular, the elasticity inherent in the System/Subsystem schema. → 21/3d8ccb See also → 21/3d8g3; → 21/3d8c16f.
