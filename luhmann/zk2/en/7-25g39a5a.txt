From an organizational perspective, the “uncertainty absorption” function of examinations is crucial. Uncertainty is transformed into certainty that can be built upon.
Consequences of Examinations:
1.) Scholarships, etc.—particularly in the Middle Ages and up through the 18th century; corresponds to a stratified society;
2.) “Authorizations,” i.e., qualifications for performing tasks, correspond to a functionally differentiated society.
In principle, the same mechanism underlies both connections.
