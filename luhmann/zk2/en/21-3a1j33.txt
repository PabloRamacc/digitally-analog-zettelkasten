The thesis of autopoietic systems states with regard to the environment two things: 1.) that everything which appears as a unity in the environment is produced as a unity by a system;
2.) that the system uses itself as difference that makes a difference, thus employing self-reference for the establishment of distinctions.
From this it does not follow either that the system exists without an environment or that the environment is entropically or chaotically ordered.
