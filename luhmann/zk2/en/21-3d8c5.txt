The burden on a system by a conflict in the preceding sense is to be termed __tension__. See also → 25/1n. → 21/3d8c5,1; → 21/3d8c5,2; → 21/3d8c5,5; → 21/3d8c5,7; → 21/3d8c5,8
Within the framework of general theory, which makes the problem of expectation strength into the reference point for the functional analysis of action systems (cf. → 21/3a1p), it must therefore define __tension as uncertainty in expectations__ and at the same time empirically index it. A conflict consists in the disappointment of a system's expectations by another system; that is to say, in a lack of complementarity of expectations; cf. → 5311/2; → 532/1a.
In this sense → 21/3d8c5(R) Gouldner, Wildcat, p. 124ff. → B369 sketches "Rudiments of a General Theory of Group Tensions" on the basis of the notion that action systems come into conflict with one another through their behavioral expectations, disappoint each other, react to disappointments and thus live in continuous tension.
