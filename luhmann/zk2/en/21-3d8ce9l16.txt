Protest movements combine, as long as possible during ongoing operations, self-reference and other-reference. The self-reference lies in the form of protest; the other-reference lies in the topics and occasions.
Here, the __combination__ – that one sees topics as occasions for protests – is an internal achievement – just as already the terms self-reference / other-reference presuppose a system reference.
In another version one can also formulate: the system is open with regard to topics and closed with regard to the form of protest.
