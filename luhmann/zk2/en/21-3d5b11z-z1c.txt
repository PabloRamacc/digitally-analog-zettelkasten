Question of the __relative dominance__ of subsystems.
The possibility of dominance varies with the type of differentiation: - in segmentary differentiation: no domination; - in stratificational differentiation: regulated domination; - in functional differentiation: no domination.
Apart from that, there are situations of transition involving a kind of irregular dominance, e.g., particularly wealthy families in archaic societies or retrojected function primates in individual highly cultured societies. → 21/3d26g67
