→ 1/7 Weiss, p. 5 → B82: "The organization, as a social form that achieves its goals through the coordinated effort of individuals in offices, faces three basic problems: (1) the problem of allocating responsibility for specific functional activities to specific members of the organization;
(2) the problem of members of the organization accepting responsibility (referred to as the problem of __adaptation__);
(3) the problem of __coordinating__ the functional activities of the members of the organization.
