→ 21/3d7d Dorfman, S. 824 → B1431 referierend: "It [input-output analysis] conceives of an economy as divided into a number of industrial sectors each of which is analogous to a process as the term is used in mathematical programming. It then takes either of two forms. In 'open models' an input- output analysis starts with some specified final demand for the products of each of the sectors and calculates the level at which each of the sector-processes must operate in order to meet this schedule of final demands. In 'closed models' final demand does not appear but attention is concentrated on the fact that the inputs required by each sector-process must be supplied as outputs by some other sector-processes. Input-output analysis then calculates a mutually compatible set of output levels for the various sectors."
Vielleicht sollte man dies besser output-input analysis nennen im Unterschied zum innerbetrieblichen Aspekt.
