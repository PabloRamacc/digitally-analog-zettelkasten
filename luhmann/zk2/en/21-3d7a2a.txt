The system's performance of giving small causes large effects is important among others in connection with structural changes; cf. → 21/8o14c.
In this context, Buckley (1967), p. 47 → B996, discusses small causes of large effects in complex systems by showing that with __little__ energy important information can be provided which brings about changes in behavior.
However, it must be paid greater attention to the fact that structural change is a special case.
