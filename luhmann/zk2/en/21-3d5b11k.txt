On the adverse consequences of differentiation: → 21/3d5b11ka 1.) Differentiation impairs __the interchangeability of parts.__ They can no longer stand in for one another. Frequent observation. → 21/3d5b11kb Cf., e.g., Simmel 1890, p. 133f. → B1238 This means that in a differentiated system functional stabilization becomes problematic and must be consciously regulated. Special mechanisms of backup provision become necessary. One can no longer crumple the system like a freshwater polyp with the consequence that then the digestive organs become skin and the skin becomes digestive organs (example from Simmel's → B1238).
The thought is evaluable for the problem of emotional / functional stabilization: With identical affective disposition, members of a system are highly interchangeable if additionally regarding memory content and abilities → 21/3d5b11k1
