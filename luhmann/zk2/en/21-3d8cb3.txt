Seen from outside it is a specific experiential theme; for members it is the presupposed framework of conduct. It is precisely in this that the meaning of "entry" and the difference between experience meanings lies, which corresponds to the inside/outside distinction. Within the system itself – among members with one another – conflicts are therefore only possible in such a way that the system as a conflict frame is accepted; i.e., only according to the rules of the game of the system. Whoever attacks the system as a whole thereby steps out. This is essentially the same thought underlying also the definition of formality through the membership rule.
