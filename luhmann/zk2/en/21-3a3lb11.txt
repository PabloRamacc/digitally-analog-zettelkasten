Crucial for the concept are primarily Parsons' → B348 remarks on "situationally generalized goals" in *Social System*, p. 236ff. → B348 The determination of what a system strives for, wherein it sees its action fulfilled, is at the same time a mechanism of adaptation to the environment if it succeeds in generalizing this definition so that it does not relate solely to the specific needs of the system in certain situations. For example: orientation toward professional success, academic achievement (in whatever sense), money, power, pleasure.
From this arises simultaneously the more precise meaning in which for Parsons → B348 goal-setting is environmental adaptation. Furthermore, generalization contains the possibility of normative integration within larger systems.
