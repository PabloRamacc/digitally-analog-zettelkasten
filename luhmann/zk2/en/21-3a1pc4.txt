Simultaneously with the generalizing identification, __the precision (unambiguity) of expectations becomes problematic__. → 21/3a1pc4(R) One might perhaps call unambiguity __the problematic aspect of generalization__ (or rather, the almost unavoidable lack of unambiguity as a dysfunctional consequence that raises secondary problems).
Unambiguity can perhaps be defined as __the intersubjective and intertemporal repeatability of selection__. However, this definition already presupposes identity in elementary experiential possibilities, so it is only applicable to more complex generalizations. This seems unavoidable and at the same time denotes an unsurpassable limit of every precision.
