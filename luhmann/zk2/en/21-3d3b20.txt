At the level of second order cybernetics, autonomy can only be described through the double postulate of __autonomy__ and __inclusion__; Heinz von Foerster 1985, p. 28f. → B947
Even the concept of a __boundary__ requires this two-sidedness. One can see a boundary only if one sees both sides. Therefore, every second-order observer arrives at this double postulate when he realizes that first-order observers see only with the help of a boundary, only with the help of a distinction from themselves.
