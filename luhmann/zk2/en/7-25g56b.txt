Dreeben 1970, pp. 87ff. → B2551 proposes not analyzing the problem of the technology deficit in a uniform manner, but rather subdividing it into four areas, namely:
1.) The instructional process (= teaching-learning process—in the narrow sense), p. 88ff. → B2551;
2.) Motivation, pp. 91ff. → B2551;
3.) Classroom Control, pp. 94ff. → B2551;
4.) Changing social arrangements within classrooms and developing new arrangements, both within and outside the school, as well as structural issues, pp. 100ff. → B2551.
The list is deliberately inductive in nature and, admittedly, incomplete.
