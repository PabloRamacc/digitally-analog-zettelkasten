This formulation of the concept of form supersedes theory of ideas, which formulated but did not grasp forms.
Progress lies, according to early modern understanding, in the introduction of subjectivity of consciousness and thus in the dependence of forms on processes of cognition.
It lies, according to systems-theoretical understanding, in the introduction of system/environment difference as condition for form selection.
This development correlates with societal developments insofar as the last formulation of the problem of form overcomes the others and is only possible in highly complex societies structured by self-reference. → 21/3a1p4B3
