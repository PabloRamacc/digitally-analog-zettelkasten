→ 21/3d5b11 The following theses must be noted regarding the fundamental significance of the theory of social differentiation: 1.) Differentiation and system formation are essentially the same thing, because the first act of differentiation is the inside/outside distinction in system formation; hence the fundamental rank of this problem.
2.) The starting hypothesis of non-differentiated systems is—for all practical research purposes at least—a methodological fiction, comparable to a.) the theory of the state of nature in human society, cf. → 81;
b.) the starting problems of psychoanalytic functionalism, such as Freud's assumption of a satisfied primal libido before birth. See also this thought developed by Rank → B1252. In addition to that → 28,10f9p(1) [*].
