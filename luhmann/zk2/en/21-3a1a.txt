__Preconditions for existence must not be confused with functional performances__. The confusion is sometimes close at hand and often seems to involve interchangeable formulations → 21/3a1a1 (cf. well-known tautological formulations regarding needs and instincts) → 21/3a1a9. In such cases, however, we are dealing with __correctable theoretical errors__. The criterion of distinction is that preconditions for existence are always __unique__ → 21/3a1a3, in the sense that they have no functional equivalents; i.e., unlike functional performances, they do not serve a function for the maintenance of the system but merely formulate the conditions of its existence.
