__Aggregating Identification of Society__
With the form of differentiation, the possibility of aggregating identification in the social system varies.
One can no longer summarize layers or even functional systems as easily as families or villages, because aggregation must bridge differences.
Therefore, stratified societies additionally employ pars pro toto abstractions based on a function system or the uppermost layer, while functionally differentiated societies again require additional processes of reflection. → 21/3d5b11z/z1f1
