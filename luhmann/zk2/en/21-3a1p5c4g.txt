The identifications on which a team can agree as "common" must be consensual for the team, i.e., socially generalized. In this sense, the representation of a team will typically be more "__abstract__", general, less individual, and colorful/imaginative than that of an individual. The team cannot afford eccentricities; even a Bohème style appears in its function as a team performance as blatant and polished. Formalization of identities and ambiguity are also to be valued in their function enabling team performances.
