The term of __stock prerequisite__ as well as that of the __reference problem__ is deliberately kept free from __any empirical connotation__. These terms are not intended to simulate an immediate empirical fact. Their application to real systems remains open and problematic.
This represents another step forward compared to the older concept of need, which also failed to achieve this but did not make it explicit.
