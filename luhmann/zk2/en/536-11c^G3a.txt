It should be noted that a great many problems and solutions within the __social__ system are attributed to the perception of people and their “characteristics”—that is, they appear phenomenally in other people and are localized there in a way that prevents them from being resolved, or at least resolved in a sufficiently lasting manner.
One of the ways sociological thinking penetrates everyday life is by dispelling this “appearance.”
Examples can be found, for instance, in the context of attributions and explanations of disappointment; see → 3414/3a .
