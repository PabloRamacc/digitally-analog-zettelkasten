__Exclusion__ as a correlating term to inclusion → 21/3d5b11z/u36,1
Without the possibility of exclusion, inclusion cannot be maintained. Geremek 1976, p. 11 → B3441: Existence of non-integrated groups and individuals as a condition for social cohesion.
This also means that it is an __internal__ societal distinction. It does not exclude communication with the excluded group – or only in cases of expulsion in tribal societies.
The internalization of the difference between inclusion and exclusion can therefore be regarded as a variable of societal evolution.
