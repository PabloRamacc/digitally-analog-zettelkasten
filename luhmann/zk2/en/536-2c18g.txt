Since a shared stream of time is involved, communication among those present presupposes __a willingness to wait__.
This involves not only waiting for an opportunity to act oneself, but also waiting for the meaning intended by the other person to become clear as they present it. See a remark on this in Garfinkel 1967, p. 3 → B1784: “Over the course of their delivery, accounts tend to require that ‘auditors’ be willing to wait for what has been said so that the present significance of what has been said becomes clear.”
