With regard to the structuring of contingency, value orders have a peculiar double function.
As mere "lists" of preference aspects, they generate a surplus of possible goals and reference aspects. At the same time, the character of preferring in value contains an indication—albeit incomplete—of possibilities for reduction.
This double problem and the corresponding ambivalence of the concept of value are a clear indication of its position in the context of "**steigern**" (increasing) and **reduzieren** (reducing) complexity.
