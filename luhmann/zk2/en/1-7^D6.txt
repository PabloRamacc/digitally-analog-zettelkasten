This theory does not provide a selection principle for the actual division of labor (formation of departments), but rather only a framework of possibilities based on the fundamental idea of __grouping similar elements__ __together__; given the multitude of dimensions, there are several possibilities.
Acker 1961, p. 144ff. → B84 mentions: "1.) Subtasks of the same substantive nature, a.) that have the same object, b.) that involve the same action;
2.) Subtasks of the same rank; a.) management tasks of the same level; b.) operational tasks of the same difficulty;
3. Subtasks in the same phase; a.) planning tasks of the same level [See also → 432/5cA .]; b.) monitoring tasks of the same level."
