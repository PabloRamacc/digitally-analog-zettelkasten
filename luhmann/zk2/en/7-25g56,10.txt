→ 7/25g56 [*] Expansion of instructional technology—in the sense of equipment-based resources—toward a general technology of teaching, or technological didactics, as described by Flechsig in 1969 → B2682.
An important aspect of the theory’s genesis is the question of the functional equivalence of devices and teachers and the overcoming of this difference within a general technology of teaching.
Initially, the question “Can devices replace teachers?” brought with it a new imperative for precision.
