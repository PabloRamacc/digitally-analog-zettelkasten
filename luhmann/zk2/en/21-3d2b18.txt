The interdependence that can be borne within a system is closely related to the structurally permitted contingency and complexity. Ceteris paribus – especially with constant selection speed – must interdependence be reduced as contingency and complexity increase, otherwise every change triggers untenable internal coordination burdens.
One can take this context into account by incorporating interdependence among the dimensions of a multidimensional concept of complexity: an increase in interdependence would then be regarded as a form of increased complexity; cf. → 21/3d18ck.
