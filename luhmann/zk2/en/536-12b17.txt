__Naturalness as a rehearsed lack of affectation__
It originates from Italian court literature, but the effort to conceal that effort seems to be more pronounced among the French.
See Du Bosq 1639, p. 272 → B2010: “… (Si donc) la Bonne Grace se remarque à faire tout [p. 273] comme par nature et sans estude” and it follows that “la Naïfueté est bien meilleure que la Contrainte.”
