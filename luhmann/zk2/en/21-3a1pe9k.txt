Values are a highly abstract form of factual generalization of behavioral expectations (cf. → 21/3d14g1) and insofar hold particular significance for the __integration of systems__.
Fixation on values is compatible with a high degree of isolation and non-fixation (delegation) of decisions, and thus constitutes an integration mode also suitable for __very complex systems__.
Translated into phenomenological language, one could also refer to values as very open-ended references. They point to decisions that are indeterminate but determinable once values are related to situations. A value order serves as the __most general establishment of a connection horizonal → 21/3a1pe9k1__
