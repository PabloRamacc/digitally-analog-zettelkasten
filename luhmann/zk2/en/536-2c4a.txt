→ 536/2c4 It must be noted that this structure itself is in a state of flux and changes as the situational system evolves. What appears appropriate in any given situation depends on the history of the system—that is, on what has happened previously.
And this is not merely in the sense of selecting norms that are currently under consideration from a fixed set of possibilities, but in the sense of a permanent transformation of the structure itself.
See, for example, Garfinkel in O.J. Harvey 1963 → B576.
