→ 21/3a11 As long as reactive mechanisms are demanded as components of the system itself, only certain types of sensitivities of the system (namely those that it can eliminate itself) receive consideration. This does not mean that the considerable disturbances thereby become definitively determined in all respects (every type of skin injury brings blood to coagulate, regardless of how individually caused). But they are indeed so schematized that only an excerpt of the overall conceivable disturbances is taken into account.
