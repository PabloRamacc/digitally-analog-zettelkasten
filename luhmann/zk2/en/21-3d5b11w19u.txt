A starting point for the analysis of semantic correlates:
The socially __necessary__ no longer lies in the __nature__ of the social body and not anymore in the __essence__ of a human being oriented toward society, but rather in the __coupling__ of subsystems: in interferences, in relations. In doing so, relations assume contingent forms; the necessary allows for various exclusions and is thereby capable of evolution.
But this is a question of system description!
