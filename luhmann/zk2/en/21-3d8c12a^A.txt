→ 21/3d8c12a It is one of the oldest theses in social science that conflict produces unifying and binding effects, leading to the formation and consolidation of groups.
Evidence: Coser, p. 33ff., pp. 27ff. → B1501, following Simmel → B1621; William Graham Sumner, Folkways, New York - Boston 1906, pp. 12f. → B1622; Georges Sorel, Reflexions sur la violence → B1623.
