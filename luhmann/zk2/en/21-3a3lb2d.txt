The thought of the *double interchange* can presumably not be explained and understood from the notion of exchange; exchange is a prototype that becomes over-abstractioned and therefore meaningless.
For understanding the function of distinguishing two levels, a different foundation must be sought. Approaches to this in constitutional rights work → B958[*]: One level of the problematic: that of non-explicit but implicit exchange performances, serves preserving the differentiated overall structure in its ability to allow relatively autonomous, functionally-specific oriented subsystems to exist. The other deals with the follow-up problem of mutual performance dependence arising from it.
