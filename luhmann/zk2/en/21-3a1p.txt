→ 21/3a1 Attempt at a General Theory of the Stock Conditions of Any Social System → 21/3a1p(R)
The starting point is two assumptions: 1.) that the system is not universal, thus having an environment. This follows from the concept of identity.
2.) that the stock of the system consists in the stability of expectations (esp. behavioral expectations); → 21/3a1pe this follows from the concept of interaction (Durkheim). → 21/3a1pa
Together these two assumptions state that the stock of a stable order of expectation and thus of social order per se is problematic. → 21/3a1pb
Expectations can now become problematic in various directions → 21/3a1pc, namely → 21/3a1pf; → 21/3a1pg 1.) through inadequate fulfillment → 21/3a1pC;
2.) through lack of consensus → 21/3a1pD;
3.) through inconsistency of their factual connection → 21/3a1pA; → 21/3a1pB; → 21/3a1pE.
