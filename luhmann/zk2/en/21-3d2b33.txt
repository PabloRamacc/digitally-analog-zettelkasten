The term "semilattice" as a concept for non-complete interdependencies that cannot be pooled either but still exhibit an order.
See Christopher Alexander, A City is not a Tree, The Architectural Forum 122 (1965), 4, pp. 58–62 and 5, pp. 58–61 → B1172, Gabor Szasz, Introduction to Lattice Theory, New York 1963 → B1173, M.F. Friedel, Organizations and Semilattices, American Sociological Review 32 (1967), pp. 46–54 → B1174
