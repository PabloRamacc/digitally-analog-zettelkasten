On the fundamental nature of expectation attitudes for the constitution of experiential possibilities, indeed human ways of living altogether, see Claessens 1962, p. 77ff. → B563
According to Claessens → B563, this is a pre-emotional life necessity (expression of self) that also underlies the distinction between positive and negative expectations (p. 80 → B563). The significance of expectation as such (that something specific follows from something at all) is understood in Gehlen's sense as a mechanism of relief, specifically as relief from too many impressions through pre-orientation, through order specification.
Kelly 1958 → B564 also emphasizes very fundamentally the role of expectations for the channeling of experience. For the connection see → 21/6m2.
