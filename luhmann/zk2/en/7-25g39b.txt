Exams are institutions of __organizational systems__ within the educational process. → 7/25g39ba They cannot be directly derived from the function of education; thus, they are not necessary in all areas where education takes place—for example, not in families.
This alone implies that assessments can always be calibrated within processes of reflection on the “actual” function of education. They require a shift in the level of system formation and the type of system—a transposition from society to organization.
