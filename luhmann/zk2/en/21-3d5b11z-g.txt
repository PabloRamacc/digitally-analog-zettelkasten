A favorable starting point above all for determining parallels in conceptual history is the __antonym__: continuum.
See Aristotle, Metaphysics X, 1069 a 5 → B3391, regarding συνεχές. Translation by Lovejoy 1950, p. 55 → B1649: "Things are said to be continuous whenever there is one and the same limit of both wherein they overlap and which they possess in common.
