__On the theoretical connection between conflict concepts and system.__
Two possible constructions must be rejected: 1.) __The concept of conflict is not a sufficient reference problem for a theory of social systems__, i.e., the function of a social system as solving conflicts is not sufficiently defined. With Parsons, contradictory statements can be found. He defines *social order* in itself from the perspective of the problem of conflict following Hobbes. Order is for him a major contingency and __not__ an empirical fact. See Mitchell 1967, p. 10f → B1203.
