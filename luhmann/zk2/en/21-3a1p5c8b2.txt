What is important is that one always comes to know people __always__ in socially defined and typified situations and __therefore__ always in __a__, albeit vaguely outlined yet still limited role: as a local who shows the way, as a fellow traveler, as a fellow theatergoer, as a new boss, etc. The process of becoming known takes place as an exploration of other roles that do not straightforwardly result from the initial role socially-structurally – and only thereafter → 21/3a1p5c8b2(R) gradually personal acquaintance in the sense that individually tailored behavioral predictions become possible. The perceptual recognition of individuals is still no index for "personal" being-known in this sense, but a differently ordered process that also serves the purely specifically focused interaction in the initial role.
