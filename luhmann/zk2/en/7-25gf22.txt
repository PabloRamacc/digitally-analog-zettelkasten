The environmental dimension of the educational function distinguishes it from all system-internal functional definitions, such as those proposed, for example, by Fend (1974) → B2416 lead to a multifunctional model, esp. pp. 64ff. → B2416 with three functions of reproduction:
1.) cultural reproduction;
2.) role and status allocation;
3.) integration and legitimation.
All of these are functions that cannot be fulfilled solely through education or socialization and therefore cannot be distinguished as education.
