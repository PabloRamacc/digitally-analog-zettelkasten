An important application and a good example of the discrepancy between being and seeming discussed above is the divergence between normatively expected, symbolically expressed attitudes and those actually felt emotionally. For this see also → 5311/4b11a (cf. also → 42/5l2; → 524/3e; → 52/4b2).
This discrepancy is clearly a problem of the adaptation of personal and social action systems. (See also → 21/3a1p5c2e1.)
See also Daniels (1960), p. 266 → B816, who explicitly refers to Goffman → B739 for this problem of a "frequent disjunction of affect and expressive pattern".
