The system-environment theory can interpret a number of characteristic features of images. 1.) __The necessity of mediation__ arises from the peculiar systemic performance, namely the invariance of the system in relation to its environment. → 21/3a1p5e2a1
2.) __The composition of the image out of system-determined and environment-determined factors__. → 21/3a1p5e2a2 The image is not attributable solely to the environment or the system. It is – precisely for this reason – neither pure imagination nor exact copy.
3.) __The selective character of the image__, through which conditions of contradictory origin are combined into a unity. → 21/3a1p5e2a3
