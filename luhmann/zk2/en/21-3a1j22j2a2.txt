Important here is the concept of archetype with Locke, or rather the humanization of this term.
The archetype as the idea with which a manufacturer anticipates its work and through which another can observe __what and how__ the manufacturer observes.
See Tully 1980, pp. 8ff., 16ff. → B465 for an introduction, possibly also John W. Yolton, *Locke and the Compass of Human Understanding*, Cambridge 1970 → B466.
