This simultaneously clarifies that the pattern-variables denote a gradient of intensity. While identifications in the case of *diff*/*part* are strong and evident, they are in the case of *spec*/*univers* weak up to the limit of convincing coordination performance; correspondingly so in the case of subject stabilization. From this follows: The combinations *diff*/*affect* or *partic*/*perform* result in very intensive connections between subject and world, experiences of strong constancy and expectation security. Conversely, the combinations *specif*/*neutral*, *univers*/*qual* are weak up to functional uselessness; indeed, from a conceptual perspective, almost contradictory. Typical and frequent will instead be combinations that pay for a primary identification interest with an attenuation of the other, in particular *spec*/*aff* and *univers*/*perf*, but also *diff*/*neutral* and *particul*/*qual*.
