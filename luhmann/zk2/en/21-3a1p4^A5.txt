__in [S. 33] action__ is to say, not only that in terms of cultural standards it must be 'appropriate' __to the relevant object or objects__, but it must also at the same time, as an expressive symbol, __express the appropriate attitude__. And vice versa depending on which side the cultural regulation primarily starts.
In contrast to Parsons → B776, I believe not the opposition between cognitive and cathectic (expressive), but that between subjective (experienced in relation to oneself, experienced as experience) and objective (objectively experienced) is decisive. With Parsons → B776 however only the terminology is unfortunate. In his argumentation he compares cognitiv with sachlich and expressiv with intentional, einstellungsmässig, subjektiv.
