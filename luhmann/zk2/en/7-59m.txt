Classical texts in comparison to canonical biblical texts
One can abandon two premises: 1.) that the texts are divinely inspired; 2.) that they possess eternal relevance.
Classical semantics benefits from the increased credibility that can be achieved by making these omissions.
And at the same time, it attempts to reverse the effect of these omissions as much as possible. A kind of hagiography on a secularized basis, driven by the unavoidable imperative to reduce complexity as a consequence of the printing press.
