7/62[*] __Curriculum__
Curriculum—taken in the broadest sense, a term that sets limits on the dominance of the social dimension in the “pedagogical relationship.” Within the educational system, the curriculum represents the substantive dimension of meaning and therefore has its own relevance.
The problem seems to be that the complexity and contingency are so high that the social dimension can offer superior and more effective ways of simplifying the material. Agreement is reached on the selection of subject matter.
