Values are a schematization of experience that serves the reduction of complexity.
Values presuppose an open situation, and ultimately an open world, and are indispensable as means or mechanisms to organize needs in light of this openness.
It must be noted here that this openness is not a property of the world but rather a relation to humans—an excess of their possibilities over what they can actualize. It is precisely from this overload of humanity by its own world that the specifically human way of organizing needs emerges. See especially Gehlen 1958 → B716 or Vickers 1959, p. 48 → B717, with emphasis on it as fundamental → 21/3a1pe9j1.
