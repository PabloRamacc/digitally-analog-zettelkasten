Simmel 1890, p. 117ff. → B1238 highlights three reasons for the saving of force through differentiation: 1.) Avoidance of unnecessary friction (→ 21/3d5b11f3);
2.) Avoiding detours;
3.) Avoidance of superfluous coordination of means.
Behind this lies the basic idea that differentiation allows for more precise goal orientation because relevance can be defined more precisely.
