b.) __neutrality – affectivity__; → 21/3d5c3A
2.) primarily concerning the objects themselves: c.) __universalism – particularism__ → 21/3d5c3B
d.) __quality – performance__. → 21/3d5c3C
Since actor and situation are always interrelated, they naturally also involve a.) objects and b.) subjects as well; among the multitude of possible connections across the attitude/object line, especially important are the pairs → 21/3d5c4.
