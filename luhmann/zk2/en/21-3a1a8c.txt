Strikta rejection to the question of functional prerequisites for a society Homans 1961, p. 384 → B364: "There is no functional prerequisite for the survival of a society except that the society provide sufficient reward for its individual members to keep them contributing activities to its maintenance, and that it reward them not just as members of that society but as men."
Thus in essence already a – albeit quite problematic – formula for the existence of society is given and the question about the prerequisites of this rewarding by no means excluded.
