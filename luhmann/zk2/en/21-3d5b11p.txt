At the most elementary, action-proximate level, social differentiation can perhaps be understood as __differentiation of communication spheres__. Within this conception, which underlies basic rights work → B958[*], the question of system formation (subsystem formation) is a variable, no longer a definitional criterion.
The social differentiation of communication spheres is a complex process that breaks down into a series of variables, namely 1.) differentiation of preferred partner roles in communication – subsystem formation;
2.) differentiation of special languages;
3.) differentiation of rationality criteria;
4.) differentiation of sanctions. → 21/3d5b11pa
