For Parsons' concept of interdependence in social systems, two things are characteristic: 1.) P conceives interdependence as mediated by (role) expectations and thus stabilizable (i.e., not simply through the immediate interaction of linear variables).
2.) P brings the category of exchange (exchange as opposed to barter) onto the same level of abstraction as the concept of interdependence and means that __all__ interdependencies in action systems are conducted in an exchange-like form.
