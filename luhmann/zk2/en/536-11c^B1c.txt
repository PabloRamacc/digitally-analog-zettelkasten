__Further considerations__: 1.) Is there a sense of continuity despite changes in membership? Among all members, or just some?
2.) Are there positions such that, if left unfilled, the organization’s identity remains intact but a sense of vacancy arises?
3.) Are there ways to treat the system as such as a unit of action, or to represent it if necessary?
All of these developmental stages can be translated into internal, structural requirements.
