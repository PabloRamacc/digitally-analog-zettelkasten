What is characteristic of functionalist theory in general is that it views problems not only as factually given, disturbing data – like pure behaviorism; cf. quotes → 21/3a1kb – but already as a __cognitive achievement__.
Corresponding tendencies exist in recent decision-making theory – e.g., Braybrooke / Lindblom, p. 54ff. → B509, with reference to the need for clarification of the problem formulation __in__ the decision process.
