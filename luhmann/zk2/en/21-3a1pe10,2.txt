→ 21/3a1pe10 __On the Cultural Evaluation of the New__
Revaluation of the new compared to the old as a consequence of the invention of printing – suggested by Eisenstein 1969, p. 41 → B736. For printed books are more reliable than older ones (because they were printed with knowledge of the old), whereas newly written books are worse because burdened with additional sources of error.
