The problem of a __biased__ solution to the rationality problem, which leans one-sidedly toward avoiding error and thereby rejects suitable candidates.
As a result, many unsuitable candidates are admitted as a precaution, only to have frustrating experiences later on.
Dale and Griffith (1965) provide a compelling account of this. → B3151. See also Kornadt 1975, pp. 19ff. → B2805 points this out. For a research overview on this topic, see Völker 1974, pp. 226ff. → B2793
