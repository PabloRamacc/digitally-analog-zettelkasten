→ 536/11d __System Context of Communication__
In communication, in addition to pursuing specific communicative intentions, the systemic context in which communication takes place is also taken into account.
Both participants understand the system as a “set of constraints” and use it, when appropriate, as a basis for interpretation.
Certain utterances cannot be meant seriously in certain contexts.
A joke is largely an unusual shift of the utterance relative to the system context, intended either to alter the utterance in relation to its manifest content or to draw attention to the system context; see → 5313/2b1kA .
