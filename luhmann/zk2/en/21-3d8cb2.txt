Further elaboration of this thought: Conflicts must, like all experience, have a __specific focal point__, a specific theme, and thus presuppose an accepted horizon of experience. Agreement on this order must exist among the disputants. However, the prerequisite of the horizon is not thematized and insofar not subject to an articulated consensus; it is tacitly assumed together for now.
