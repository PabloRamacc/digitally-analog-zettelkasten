4.) __Learning__ is the term for that one cannot observe how a system changes its structure based on __individual__ information without interrupting its identity; cf. → 54/1.
"Learning 'amounts to the assumption that the input __now__ is identifiable with the input __then__, and that the system has preserved its formal identity. Again this is an assumption based either on ignorance of, or the impossibility of discovering, the internal states of the system in all completeness.'" Zopf 1962, p. 329 → B456
