Central to interaction theory is the distinction between (at least) two subjects __with their respective subjective modes of experience.__
Principle: Non-identity of orientations, with mutual subjectivity.
Everyone is a subject, even though everyone is an object to the other.
Basic terminology: ego – alter.
Objectivity develops through the involvement of a third party. Every participant in an interaction has the opportunity to achieve objectivity through consensus with a third party.
