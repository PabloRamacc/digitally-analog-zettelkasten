Comments, p. 162 → B88: "(c.) Reification of groups [as limit to rationality]. The limit of human understanding in the presence of complex social structures leads humans to construct simplified maps (i.e., theories or models) of the social system in which they are acting and to behave as though these maps were reality. To the extent that such maps are held in common, they must be counted among internal constraints on rational adaptation. What we have just said applies, of course, to all systems of classification which, by determining when situations are 'similar' and when 'different', provide the individual with a social definition of the situation.
