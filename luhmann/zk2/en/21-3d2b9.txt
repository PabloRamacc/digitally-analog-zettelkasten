Perhaps one can say that interdependence and equilibrium only converge in the absolute. Static equilibrium is attainable only in a fully determined system. If this ideal model cannot be realized, then the equilibrium model must also be abandoned. __A system with partial and reciprocal dependencies is necessarily unstable__.
