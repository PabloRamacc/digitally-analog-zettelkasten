As with the relative invariance of a system, its interdependence can also be treated as a variable, → 21/3d2b(R) which may take on different values. In this context, variation is conceivable in (at least) two directions: 1.) according to the type of required connection;
2.) according to the number of involved parts. → 21/3d2b1
If one wishes so, one can therefore speak of intensive and extensive variation of interdependence.
