Fundamental considerations on system-environment theory.
The system-environment theory explains the __individualization__ of systems. No system has exactly the same environment as another; and therefore it is compelled to find individual performances in order to sustain itself. The diversity of environments leads to individualization. → 21/3d7ha
Conversely, one can speak of the individuality of a system only with reference to its place in the environment. And this is presupposed when speaking about differences between environments. It is thus not a simple causal process in which the environment creates the individual system or that system creates the differentiated environment, but rather a relationship of mutually interdependent conditioning and stimulation, → 21/3d7h1
