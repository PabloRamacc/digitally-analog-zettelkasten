The stabilizing function of values is particularly needed in uncertain situations without fixed factual expectations, that is, especially in social situations determined by behavioral expectations. In such situations there therefore exists a particular tendency toward value orientation (cf. also Stogdill → B321, cit → 21/3a1pe9a - → 21/3a1pe9a1).
In such situations the individual's values then acquire at once a normative meaning, i.e., one claiming validity for others' behavior as well: a new direction of generalization.
