→ 536/11d A distinction must be made between: → 536/11dAa ; → 536/11dAb [*] 1.) Communication that is specifically and consciously intended to solve systemic problems that have been identified as goals;
2.) Communication arising from interaction that also latently resolves systemic problems (e.g., a taxi driver’s conversation with his passenger, which is determined by the fact that they did not choose each other, will never see each other again, etc.).
See F. Davis 1959 → B1761 (little on this).
