__Elementary Interactions__ → 536/7(R) ; → 536/7,1 ; → 536/7,2
Elementary interactions are those between participants who are physically present.
Their distinctive feature lies in the fact that one can—and must—react immediately, in the presence of the other.
This distinctive feature warrants special thematic treatment, both with regard to the functional contributions of such interactions to larger systemic contexts and with regard to the functional conditions for the maintenance of such interactions.
