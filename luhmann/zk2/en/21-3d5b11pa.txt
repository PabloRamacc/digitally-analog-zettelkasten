→ 21/3d5b11p This point is illustrated by Smelser 1963, p. 175 → B658, in an interesting connection of the generalized exchange and communication medium with the question of sanctions: "Structural differentiation refers to the level of specialization of a given kind of action (e.g., economic), and the degree to which specialized sanctions (money, in this case) are allowed to operate without intervention of other kinds of sanctions (e.g., political controls, kinship and other particularistic demands, etc.)."
To this one can add that the specialization of certain sanction means and forms is a prerequisite for their long-term (not dependent on intimate knowledge of persons) calculability. Knowledge of persons becomes dispensable through this.
