(Something like this, shorter: Goffman, p. 27f. → B739) – Concealment of everything unclean, illegal, cruel, degrading, or otherwise negative, which is practically necessary in the representation itself (p. 28 → B739). – Concealment of all appearances that could reveal inconsistencies among various ideal standards. In doing so, those standards are sacrificed where violations can more easily be concealed (pp. 28f. → B739).
