Bartholomäus Keckermann, *Systema Logicae tribus libris adornatum*, Hannover 1600 → B1101 Clemens Timpler, *Metaphysicae systema methodicum*, Hannover 1606 → B1102
__Preceding__: theological approaches to reformulating the concept of system into a learning and thinking performance, in contrast to which the thing itself is still seen as congruent. Since then, essentially an unresolved opposition between analytical and real concepts of systems.
From that point on, "system" increasingly becomes more and more a system of sentences, a development obviously spurred by subjective philosophy of reflection.
