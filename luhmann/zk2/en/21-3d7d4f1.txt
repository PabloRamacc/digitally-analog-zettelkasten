It is __in the things themselves__ that all external causes—that is, what Thomas Aquinas calls efficient causes—are only of a secondary nature. In the infinite system of interaction, each individual and each system maintains itself for a certain time solely because it is capable of holding onto its own identity amidst changing external influences and producing only those effects that correspond to its internal systemic conditions."
