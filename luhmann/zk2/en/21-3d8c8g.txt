See also some remarks by Rice 1963, p. 5f. → B129, on the problematic of revealing the "true" conflict reasons. The topic formulation of the "unsolvable" conflict material serves a defensive function that enables hiding the actual conflict reasons from oneself or others. At the level of manifest conflict, the conflict cannot be resolved. On the other hand, the parties resist clarification of the true reasons and basically want confirmation of their standpoint by the consultant or confirmation of the unsolvability of the conflict.
Rice → B129 also assumes that the manifest conflict theme has only symptomatic significance.
See also the thought from → 533/4a7b, that withholding information is a consensus strategy.
