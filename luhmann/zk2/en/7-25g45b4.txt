Asymmetry is misunderstood when it is viewed as one side controlling the other through the distribution of power.
In general, the following also applies here: “Control is a prominent process within organizations, but it is accomplished by relationships, not by people.” Weick 1969, p. 37 → B3198.
This means: It is always a __social__ process, not the power __of a__ person or __a__ role over others.
