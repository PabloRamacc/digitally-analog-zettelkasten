Differentiation/Communication Load {1}
Functional-structural differentiation means increasing demands on the communication system. These demands arise from a change in role structure: roles are now more strongly differentiated and integrated only through __complementary__ mutually assigned roles. This implies that integration must increasingly be accomplished by the communication network in the form of explicit communications. In all simply structured systems, however, __personal__ role combinations prevail, which means that integration is carried out by taking into account one's own other roles—that is, without communication.
