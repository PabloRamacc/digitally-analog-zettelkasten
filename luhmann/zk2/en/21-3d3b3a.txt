From Deutsch's → B962 elucidation of the autonomy problem via the inside/outside distinction emerges the finding of a paradox that can no longer be resolved through the concept of autonomy itself, but must remain as it stands. Cf. formulation p. 219 → B962: "Is there perhaps a paradox in the nature of autonomy, in the self-steering and self-rule[*] of each individual personality, as well as of each autonomous human organization? Autonomy is impossible without openness to communication from the outside world; but at the same time autonomy is impossible unless the incoming flow of external information is overridden to a significant extent by internal memories and preferences.
