__the consistency requirement__: __Outside__ the region other appearances and identifications are possible, at least if the region can be communicatively sealed off; within the region a uniformly consistent presentation must be given, or else the overall impression is discredited.
In detail Goffman distinguishes → B739 (p. 66ff.) → B814, p. 106 → B814[*] besides the neutral outside world, which is simply irrelevant, __front region__ and __backstage__. The front region is the place of presentation, the place where identity is portrayed, asserted, brought before an audience. Backstage is the normally inaccessible area where all existence-necessary actions take place (including preparation and recovery), which are inconsistent and therefore not shown.
This distinction makes clear that within the regional boundaries of identity representation there simultaneously lie practical possibilities for dealing with contradictions.
