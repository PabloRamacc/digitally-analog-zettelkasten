Of course, trust cannot be treated solely as internally conditioned; it refers to the world and justifies itself through prior experiences in the world. However, there is considerable room for selection regarding which prior experiences are deemed significant. Objectively speaking, one almost always has both good and bad analogies at hand—that is, fulfilled expectations and disappointed expectations as prior experiences. Only do disappointed expectations weigh more heavily because feelings must be mobilized to overcome them, whereas fulfilled expectations can easily become routine.
