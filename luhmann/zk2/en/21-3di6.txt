Regarding the legal discussion of the 16th century, which was not yet terminologically fixed on "system" but aimed at this in substance, see Troje 1969 → B1083. Main points seem to be: 1.) philosophical critique of Prudentiae;
2.) increasing problematization of security (certitude jurisprudentiae), likely in connection with experiences of time;
3.) systems of classification and arrangement of content, etc.
4.) problematic, poorly clarified relationship to logic.
