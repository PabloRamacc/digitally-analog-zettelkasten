Naturally, what is important for trust-learning in exchange situations is that they actually demonstrate reliability and which one(s). In normal purchases under money/market conditions, one does not learn personal trust in the personality of the exchange partners, but at most abstract trust in the stability of the value of money.
See also some remarks by Blau 1964, p. 94 → B377, which – not very clearly formulated – simultaneously teach that the emergence of trust and gratitude are connected. Trust arises so to speak as trust in gratitude, in the partner's readiness for retaliation.
