__Zettelkasten__ → 9/8,1 __as a cybernetic system__
A combination of disorder and order, of clustering and unpredictable combinations realized through ad hoc access.
Prerequisite: the abandonment of a predetermined order. The preliminary distinction—search aids vs. content; indexes, questions, and ideas vs. existing material—reshapes and, in part, renders superfluous that which must be presupposed as internal order.
