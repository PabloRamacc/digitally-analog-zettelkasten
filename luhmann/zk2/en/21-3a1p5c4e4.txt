From the problem of double publicity arises the problem of effective separation between team and audience. This is an application case of the general problem of audience segregation, cf. → 5313/2b1c, and a problem of maintaining differentiated separate information and communication systems. For this see Goffman, p. 58 → B739.
This separation is moreover an important moment in the loyalty of the team; cf. → 21/3a1p5c4fB1d.
Furthermore, this separation is a prerequisite for keeping secret the extent of cooperation, which is necessary due to the reasons discussed in → 21/3a1p5c4aA11. Also here see Goffman, pp. 64f. → B739, where it is emphasized that generally membership in the team as such does not need and cannot be kept secret, but rather the extent of cooperation can, especially in those aspects that define a group as a presentation team.
